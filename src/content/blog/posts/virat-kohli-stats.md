**Virat Kohli had scored 28,608 international runs and 86 international centuries through India’s ODI against New Zealand on 13 November 2026.** That combines 9,230 Test runs, 15,190 ODI runs and 4,188 T20I runs. His IPL record is listed separately because franchise matches do not count as internationals. The format totals match the [Cricbuzz profile](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1413/virat-kohli-c) and [the format-filtered ODI ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1413/virat-kohli/all-matches/batting?matchType=2).

Kohli has retired from Tests and T20 internationals, while his ODI career remains active. This snapshot uses **13 November 2026 as its ODI cutoff**, rechecked on 14 November. It includes the completed Mount Maunganui ODI, not an assumed result from an earlier schedule.

## ODI numbers through 13 November 2026

At the cutoff, Cricbuzz lists **15,190 runs in 320 ODIs**, including 55 centuries and 80 fifties. His latest included innings was a four-ball duck against New Zealand at Mount Maunganui on 13 November. Before that came seven from seven balls in Wellington on 7 November and 74 from 51 balls in Auckland on 4 November. These appear in the [Cricbuzz ODI match ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1413/virat-kohli/all-matches/batting?matchType=2).

Kohli crossed 15,000 ODI runs against West Indies on 27 September 2026. The [ICC’s milestone report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/virat-kohli-scales-another-remarkable-odi-record) identifies him as the second men’s ODI batter to reach that mark, after Sachin Tendulkar.

ODIs supply approximately **53.1% of his international runs and 64.0% of his international centuries** at this cutoff. The difference shows how strongly his hundred-making record is concentrated in the 50-over format.

His average uses 15,190 runs divided by **260 dismissals**, calculated as 308 innings minus 48 not-outs. Dividing runs by his 320 matches instead would measure runs per appearance.

The [NZC archive’s 10 November Hamilton scorecard](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1455/1455468.html) names Kohli in an abandoned match and explicitly says the completed toss counts as a match played. That appearance produced no batting innings. The career appearance count above is attributed to Cricbuzz, not independently rebuilt from every team sheet; a batting log alone cannot establish total appearances.

## His 2026 ODI record

Through 13 November, the batting ledger records 12 ODI innings for Kohli in 2026. He scored **633 runs from 530 balls**, with one not-out, two centuries and four half-centuries. That produces an average of **57.55** and a strike rate of **119.43**. These year totals are calculated from all twelve 2026 ODI innings in the [format-filtered ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1413/virat-kohli/all-matches/batting?matchType=2). The November duck added four balls and one dismissal without adding runs, so the average fell even though the accumulated runs stayed unchanged.

His 2026 strike rate was **24.93 runs per 100 balls higher** than his career ODI rate of 94.51. That describes scoring speed across 12 innings. It should not be read as proof that every aspect of his long-term game had changed.

The two hundreds in this year-to-date sample are already included in his 55 career ODI centuries. Adding the year column to the career column would count those innings twice.

## Test statistics and retirement

Kohli announced his Test retirement on **12 May 2025**. He finished with 9,230 runs in 123 matches, averaging 46.85, with 30 centuries and 31 fifties. His highest score was 254 not out. [BCCI retirement announcement](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/bcci-congratulates-virat-kohli-on-a-legendary-test-career).

His leadership record adds context that the batting table cannot supply: **40 victories in 68 Tests as captain**, making him India’s most successful Test captain by wins when he retired, according to the BCCI.

Those captaincy appearances are part of his 123 Tests. Likewise, his Test runs are already included within broader first-class statistics and should not be added to them again.

## T20I statistics and the final international innings

Kohli’s final T20 international was the **2024 T20 World Cup final on 29 June**. He scored 76 from 59 balls against South Africa, received the Player of the Match award and announced his retirement from the international format. [ICC final and retirement report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/it-was-now-or-never-departing-kohli-celebrates-india-s-triumph).

His completed T20I record stands at 4,188 runs in 125 matches, with one century and 38 fifties. T20I means international Twenty20 cricket. Retiring from that format did not end his IPL career.
