England beat India by 27 runs in the third men's ODI at Lord's on 19 July 2026. England made 387/3 after choosing to bat; India replied with 360/7. Ben Duckett and Rohit Sharma both scored centuries, but the Player of the Match was Jacob Bethell, whose 91 was followed by the wicket of Rohit. [BCCI match details](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/3068bfaa-fe7f-4a49-8f1c-d1b9077bc171/england-vs-india/match-details).

The result settled a high-scoring decider. It also shows why the biggest individual score is not necessarily the whole story: England combined a large opening stand with a rapid finish, then broke India's chase at important stages.

The two innings produced 747 runs. India's target was 388, while the winning margin is calculated from 387 minus 360. Those numbers answer different questions: what India needed to win, and how far its completed innings finished behind England's.

## England added a fast finish to the opening stand

Duckett made 141 from 135 balls and Bethell 91 from 93. Their first-wicket partnership reached 192. Joe Root finished unbeaten on 74, while Jos Buttler supplied 41 not out from just 13 balls. [Cricket Australia's completed-match report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4538835/england-beat-india-third-odi-result-lords-one-day-international-highlights-scores-report-duckett-rohit-sharma-century).

The scorecard separates the foundation from the closing burst. England were 324/3 when Harry Brook fell at 46.5 overs, then added 63 without another wicket. With 281 legal balls completed at that dismissal, 19 remained. Those 63 runs came at the equivalent of 19.89 per six legal deliveries. [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/129480/india-tour-of-england-2026-3rd-odi).

That is a rate for one short segment, not England's whole innings. Its overall rate was 7.74. Averaging the two rates would be meaningless because the segments cover different numbers of deliveries.

## India had a platform but a demanding target

Rohit's 138 from 110 balls and Shubman Gill's 77 gave India substantial top-order runs. Virat Kohli added 74. Bethell bowled Rohit with India at 260/2, and Sam Curran subsequently removed Ishan Kishan and Shreyas Iyer in the 44th over. Curran finished with 4/75. [Report of India's chase](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4538835/england-beat-india-third-odi-result-lords-one-day-international-highlights-scores-report-duckett-rohit-sharma-century).

At Rohit's dismissal after 38.4 overs, India needed 128 from 68 legal balls. That is an asking rate of 11.29, despite having eight wickets left. Wickets in hand gave India batting resources; they did not erase the scoring requirement.

After the double strike, India were 304/4 at the end of 44 overs. The requirement had risen to 84 from 36 balls, or 14 per over. India added 56 in those final six overs, leaving the total at 360/7. These checkpoints explain why an innings that passed 350 could still fall well short of its target.

## The result and the individual awards

Bethell's contribution crossed innings: he helped build England's total and dismissed India's centurion. Duckett remained England's leading run-scorer in this match, while Root was named Player of the Series with 249 runs. Those distinctions are recorded separately in the [official match details](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/3068bfaa-fe7f-4a49-8f1c-d1b9077bc171/england-vs-india/match-details).
