**India won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2024, defeating South Africa by seven runs** in the final on **29 June at Kensington Oval in Bridgetown, Barbados**. India won the toss and chose to bat. *Completed 2024 men's competition. Historical facts rechecked on 14 November 2026.*

## Dates hosts and tournament format

The ninth men's T20 World Cup ran from **1 to 29 June 2024**, using the hosts' local dates. The West Indies and USA co-hosted the competition, which featured 20 teams.

The first round had four groups of five. The top two teams in each group qualified for the Super Eight, where the remaining sides were divided into two groups of four. The top two from each Super Eight group advanced to the semi-finals, and the winners reached the final. [ICC’s format and schedule](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-icc-men-s-t20-world-cup-2024?appview=true).

First-round standings and Super Eight standings therefore describe different stages. They should not be combined into a single league table.

## Individual awards

**Virat Kohli was Player of the Match in the final** after making 76 from 59 balls. His innings helped India recover from early wickets and establish the total South Africa had to chase. [ICC’s review of Kohli’s innings](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/virat-kohli-s-best-t20-world-cup-innings).

**Jasprit Bumrah was Player of the Tournament.** He took 15 wickets at an average of 8.26 and an economy rate of 4.17 runs per over. [ICC’s review of Bumrah’s campaign](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/a-genius-of-his-craft-reflecting-on-jasprit-bumrah-s-t20-world-cup-2024).

Bowling average and economy measure different things: runs conceded per wicket and runs conceded per over. A bowler can contribute through both wicket-taking and restricting the scoring rate.

## The final five overs

South Africa needed **30 runs from the last 30 balls**, a required rate of six runs per over. Heinrich Klaasen then fell for 52 in the 17th over, caught behind off Hardik Pandya. Bumrah subsequently dismissed Marco Jansen, and South Africa needed 20 from the final two overs.

David Miller was caught by Suryakumar Yadav off the first ball of the last over. India completed the defence. [ICC’s contemporaneous final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/live-india-and-south-africa-face-off-in-t20-world-cup-final?appview=true).

The arithmetic shows the turnaround:

- Target of 177 minus 30 still needed = **147 runs already scored**.

- Final score of 169 minus 147 = **22 runs added in the last five overs**.

- 22 divided by five = **4.4 runs per over** during that period.

There is also a scoring distinction. South Africa finished eight short of the winning target of 177, but the official defeat margin was seven runs because India had scored 176. A total of 176 would have tied the scores.

## Leading batters and bowlers

Rahmanullah Gurbaz led the batting chart with **281 runs for Afghanistan**. Rohit Sharma followed with 257 for India, just ahead of Australia's Travis Head on 255.

The leading wicket-takers were tied: Afghanistan's **Fazalhaq Farooqi and India's Arshdeep Singh each took 17**. Bumrah and South Africa's Anrich Nortje both finished with 15.

Other tournament leaders were:

- **Highest individual innings:** Nicholas Pooran, 98 against Afghanistan.

- **Best innings bowling figures:** Farooqi, 5/9 against Uganda.

- **Centuries:** None.

These are edition totals and performances, rather than career records or current rankings. [ICC’s statistical review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/big-performers-stat-leaders-from-the-men-s-t20-world-cup-2024).

## Records established during the 2024 edition

India and South Africa each finished with eight wins. India's other fixture, against Canada, was washed out, so unbeaten champions does not mean nine victories.

At the end of the edition, India's 176/7 was the highest total in a men's T20 World Cup final. Farooqi and Arshdeep's 17 wickets each also established a new single-edition benchmark.

Lockie Ferguson produced another unusual performance against Papua New Guinea: **four maiden overs and figures of 3/0**. Pooran hit 17 sixes across the competition, setting a men's single-edition record at the time. [ICC’s records review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/the-records-broken-at-t20-world-cup-2024).

These historical marks describe what the 2024 tournament established. They are not a claim that every record remained unbroken in later editions.
