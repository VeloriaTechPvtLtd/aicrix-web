Bangladesh recovered from 26/6 in the first innings of the second Rawalpindi Test against Pakistan in 2024, then won by six wickets. The result completed a 2–0 series victory, Bangladesh’s first Test-series win over Pakistan. [ICC series assessment](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/confident-najmul-ready-for-india-challenge-after-pakistan-triumph), [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/101032/pak-vs-ban-2nd-test-bangladesh-tour-of-pakistan-2024)

## Litton arrived before the sixth wicket

Litton Das came in at 26/5, not 26/6. Shakib Al Hasan then fell without the total changing. Litton went on to make 138, supported by Mehidy Hasan Miraz’s 78 and Hasan Mahmud’s unbeaten 13. [ICC’s recovery report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/litton-das-rescue-act-leaves-rawalpindi-test-in-the-balance)

Litton and Mehidy added 165 for the seventh wicket, moving the total to 191/7. After another wicket, Litton and Hasan added 69 from 193/8 to 262/9. Bangladesh finished at 262, only 12 behind Pakistan’s 274. [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/101032/pak-vs-ban-2nd-test-bangladesh-tour-of-pakistan-2024)

The two major stands totalled 234. That does not mean Litton personally scored 234: partnerships contain both batters’ contributions and any extras during the stand. His own innings remained 138.

## Bowlers created a chase of 185

Pakistan made 172 in their second innings. Hasan took 5/43 and Nahid Rana 4/44. The target was therefore **185**: 274 + 172 − 262 + 1. [Bowling and innings card](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/101032/pak-vs-ban-2nd-test-bangladesh-tour-of-pakistan-2024)

Bangladesh reached 185/4. All six batters used in the chase passed twenty, with Zakir Hasan’s 40 the highest score. Rather than requiring another century, this innings spread the work across the batting order. [Chase card](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/101032/pak-vs-ban-2nd-test-bangladesh-tour-of-pakistan-2024)

The recovery and the result are separate achievements. Litton’s innings reduced the first-innings deficit; the subsequent bowling performance limited the target, and the final innings completed it. Omitting either later phase would make the comeback appear automatic when the match still required both wickets and runs.
