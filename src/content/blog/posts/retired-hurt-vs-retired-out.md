A batter leaving the field has not necessarily been dismissed. The official retirement classification matters, both for a possible return and for the final statistical record. “Retired hurt” and “retired out” should not be treated as interchangeable scorecard labels.

## First establish which rules govern the match

The October 2026 men's Test conditions distinguish illness, injury or another unavoidable cause from other retirements. The former allows the batter to resume; if they do not, the entry is retired not out. Other retirements are recorded out. [ICC Test clause 25.4](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/a8114zro5tkioifz0jqg.pdf).

This guide uses the October 2026 men's Test conditions. Other competitions can use different return rules. Check the conditions for that competition rather than transferring this answer to the IPL or WPL. Under these Test conditions, a permitted return takes place at a wicket or another batter's retirement.

## Why the classification changes an average

Batting average divides runs by dismissals, not by appearances. Here is a hypothetical three-innings ledger:

| Innings | Runs | Final status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30 | Out |
| 2 | 40 | Not out |
| 3 | 20 | Retired out |

The total is 90 runs with two dismissals, so the average is 45. If the third entry were instead a final retired-not-out innings, the same 90 runs would have one dismissal and an average of 90. Nothing about the scoring rate changed; the denominator did. [ACS batting-average explanation](https://acscricket.com/wp-content/uploads/ACS-FAQ.pdf).

If all three innings were not out, the denominator would be zero. Display a dash or an explicit “not defined,” not zero and not 90 divided by one. These are arithmetic illustrations, not a player's actual record.

## Preserve the final ledger, not just the exit message

For a stats export, retain the player, competition, date, runs and final retirement status. If the batter returned later, one temporary departure should not become an additional batting innings. Reconcile the final scorecard before calculating totals.

For fantasy research, check the platform's own rules separately. A batting-average classification does not establish a platform's dismissal penalty or participation award.

The useful sequence is: identify the rulebook, confirm the final retirement entry, then calculate the statistic. That keeps a tactical event, a medical departure and a numerical denominator from being confused.
