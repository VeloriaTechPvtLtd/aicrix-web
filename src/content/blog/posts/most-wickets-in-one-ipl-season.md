*Records through the completed 2026 IPL season. Checked on 14 November 2026.*

**Dwayne Bravo and Harshal Patel share the record for the most wickets in one IPL season: 32.** Bravo reached that total for Chennai Super Kings in 2013; Harshal matched it for Royal Challengers Bangalore in 2021. The record remained intact after IPL 2026. [Official Bravo record](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/quick-stats-final-csk-vs-mi), [RCB’s Harshal season review](https://www.royalchallengers.com/rcb-cricket-news/news/harshal-patel-the-unearthing-of-an-uncut-diamond-who-shone-brightly), [completed-season historical table](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2026/cricket-news/ipl-2026-purple-cap-full-list-who-is-leading-the-wicket-takers-table-after-todays-ipl-match).

## How Bravo set the record

Bravo finished IPL 2013 with **32 wickets**, including **4/42 against Mumbai Indians in the final**. Those four wickets lifted his season total from 28 to 32.

His final wickets were Ambati Rayudu, Harbhajan Singh, Mitchell Johnson and Lasith Malinga. Chennai lost the match by 23 runs, but Bravo’s individual record was complete. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/report-final-csk-vs-mi).

The IPL’s contemporary statistics report identifies 32 as the new single-season record, ahead of the previous 28-wicket performances by Malinga in 2011 and James Faulkner in 2013. A record-season wicket total and a championship victory are separate achievements. [Official final statistics](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/quick-stats-final-csk-vs-mi).

## How Harshal equalled 32

Harshal reached **32 wickets in the Eliminator against Kolkata Knight Riders at Sharjah on 11 October 2021**. He dismissed Shubman Gill and then Venkatesh Iyer, with Iyer becoming his 32nd wicket of the season. RCB lost the knockout, leaving Harshal’s completed-season total at 32. [Official Eliminator report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/vivo-ipl-2021-eliminator-rcb-vs-kkr-match-report).

RCB’s post-season review confirms **32 wickets in 15 matches**, equality with Bravo’s record and Harshal’s Purple Cap award. Royal Challengers Bangalore is the appropriate historical team name for that 2021 campaign. [RCB season review](https://www.royalchallengers.com/rcb-cricket-news/news/harshal-patel-the-unearthing-of-an-uncut-diamond-who-shone-brightly).

These two examples also show that the full-season record includes playoff wickets. Bravo’s final and Harshal’s Eliminator are part of their totals, not matches to discard after counting the league stage.

## Equal totals with different workloads

Bravo’s 32 came in 18 bowling innings; Harshal’s came in 15. Calculated wickets per bowling innings were therefore:

- **Bravo:** 32 ÷ 18 = **1.78**.

- **Harshal:** 32 ÷ 15 = **2.13**.

Harshal took the same number of wickets in three fewer bowling innings. That does not change the joint record, whose unit is total wickets. It adds context to how the two seasons were accumulated.

Wickets per innings also differs from bowling strike rate, which uses balls bowled per wicket. These calculations do not establish which bowler would perform better under the other’s workload or in a future match.

## What Rabada did in 2026

**Kagiso Rabada won the 2026 Purple Cap with 29 wickets for Gujarat Titans.** Gujarat Titans’ completed-season review confirms the award, and the IPL’s official season video is titled “All 29 wickets by Kagiso Rabada in TATA IPL 2026.” [Gujarat Titans’ season review](https://www.gujarattitansipl.com/news/gujarat-titans-tata-ipl-2026-season-review-kagiso-rabada-shubman-gill-sai-sudharsan), [dated 2026 wickets compilation](https://www.iplt20.com/videos/s-all-29-wickets-by-kagiso-rabada-in-tata-ipl-2026-cv5jd).

Rabada’s wicket of Devdutt Padikkal in the final secured the award. Bhuvneshwar Kumar finished on 28, as confirmed in the official final report. [IPL 2026 final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-final-rcb-v-gt-match-report).

Rabada’s 29 was three short of the 32-wicket record. Winning that edition’s Purple Cap therefore did not mean setting the all-season single-campaign record. His 30-wicket season for Delhi in 2020 remains the higher of his two entries in the table. [Delhi Capitals’ season retrospective](https://www.delhicapitals.in/news/delhi-capitals-ipl-2021-season-preview).

## Keep the record scope clear

“Most wickets in one IPL season” adds a bowler’s wickets across one completed edition, including playoffs. Career wickets add multiple seasons. Best bowling figures describe one innings. These questions need different tables.

For this record, **32 equals the leading total and 33 would exceed it**. Keep the completed-season cutoff visible when reading older lists or following a new campaign.
