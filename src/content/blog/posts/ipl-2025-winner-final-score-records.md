**Royal Challengers Bengaluru won IPL 2025, defeating Punjab Kings by six runs in the final on 3 June 2025.** RCB scored 190/9 and restricted Punjab to 184/7 at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad. Krunal Pandya was named Player of the Match. [Official IPL final details](https://www.iplt20.com/matches/9d39ed1b-0cee-4eff-a018-e6f7e1e59a17/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-punjab-kings/match-details).

It was RCB’s first IPL championship. This archive covers the completed 2025 campaign, including the final, the playoff route and individual season awards. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-final-rcb-vs-pbks-match-report).

RCB’s 190 set Punjab a target of 191. The six-run winning margin is the difference between the two completed innings totals. Both teams used their full 20 overs. [Official match record](https://www.iplt20.com/matches/9d39ed1b-0cee-4eff-a018-e6f7e1e59a17/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-punjab-kings/match-details).

## How RCB won the final

RCB built their innings through contributions across the batting order. Virat Kohli top-scored with 43, while Mayank Agarwal, Rajat Patidar and Liam Livingstone each reached the twenties. Jitesh Sharma added 24 from 10 balls, giving the innings a useful late burst. Arshdeep Singh and Kyle Jamieson took three wickets each for Punjab. [IPL final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-final-rcb-vs-pbks-match-report).

Krunal took 2/17 in the chase, dismissing Prabhsimran Singh and Josh Inglis, while Romario Shepherd removed captain Shreyas Iyer for one. Bhuvneshwar Kumar also took two wickets. Shashank Singh finished unbeaten on 61, but his late hitting could not get Punjab past RCB’s total. Krunal’s combination of wickets and economical bowling was central to the defence. [IPL final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-final-rcb-vs-pbks-match-report).

## The playoff route to the title

RCB entered the playoffs after finishing second in the league table with 19 points from 14 matches. Their final league game brought a six-wicket win over Lucknow Super Giants, with Jitesh Sharma’s unbeaten 85 from 33 balls helping them chase a target of 228. [IPL’s LSG–RCB report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-70-lsg-vs-rcb-match-report).

The knockout matches then unfolded as follows:

- **Qualifier 1, 29 May:** RCB bowled Punjab out for 101 and won by eight wickets with 10 overs remaining. Phil Salt scored 56 from 27 balls. The victory sent RCB directly into the final. [Qualifier 1 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-qualifier-1-pbks-vs-rcb-match-report).

- **Eliminator, 30 May:** Mumbai Indians beat Gujarat Titans by 20 runs after posting 228/5. Rohit Sharma made 81, and Mumbai advanced to face Punjab. [Eliminator report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-eliminator-gt-vs-mi-match-report).

- **Qualifier 2, 1 June:** Punjab recovered from their Qualifier 1 defeat by chasing Mumbai’s 203/6. Shreyas Iyer finished unbeaten on 87 from 41 balls as Punjab reached 207/5, setting up another meeting with RCB. [Qualifier 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-qualifier-2-pbks-vs-mi-match-report).

The top-two playoff route mattered: Punjab could recover from one defeat, while RCB’s first playoff win secured their final place immediately. Finishing second in the league and winning the championship were separate achievements, decided at different stages.

## Orange Cap Purple Cap and major awards

The championship and individual awards went to different teams and players.

| Award | IPL 2025 winner |
| --- | --- |
| Orange Cap | Sai Sudharsan, Gujarat Titans: 759 runs |
| Purple Cap | Prasidh Krishna, Gujarat Titans: 25 wickets |
| Most Valuable Player | Suryakumar Yadav, Mumbai Indians |
| Emerging Player | Sai Sudharsan, Gujarat Titans |
| Fair Play Award | Chennai Super Kings |

Sai Sudharsan led the run-scoring chart, while Prasidh Krishna finished one wicket ahead of Noor Ahmad. Suryakumar Yadav scored 717 runs and won the season’s MVP award. Krunal Pandya’s Player of the Match award applied specifically to the final. The leading scorer, season MVP and final’s match winner were therefore three different players. [Wisden’s awards summary](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2025/cricket-news/ipl-2025-final-presentation-full-list-tournament-award-winners).

## A league-stage record and the title

RCB’s campaign included a notable league-stage record: they became the first IPL team to win all seven away matches in a season. That consistency helped put them in the top two before their two playoff victories over Punjab secured the trophy. [IPL’s final league-match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-70-lsg-vs-rcb-match-report).
