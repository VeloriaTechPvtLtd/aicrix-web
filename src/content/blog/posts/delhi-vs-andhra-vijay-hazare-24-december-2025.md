Delhi beat Andhra by **four wickets on 24 December 2025**, reaching 300/6 in 37.4 overs in pursuit of 299 at the BCCI Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru. Virat Kohli's 131 was the largest Delhi contribution, but two other substantial innings and Simarjeet Singh's five wickets make this more than a one-player scorecard. [Full match scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/125514/ap-vs-del-group-d-vijay-hazare-trophy-elite-2025-26).

This was a Group D match in the **2025–26 Vijay Hazare Trophy**, a domestic List A competition. The December 2025 date belongs within that season; Kohli's hundred was not an additional ODI century for India. [Match classification and summary](https://cricketfieldguide.com/match/andhra-v-delhi-01m4y5zd6tfqnsss1jm199d8pt).

## Bhui built the total while two Delhi bowlers took all eight wickets

Ricky Bhui made 122 from 105 balls for Andhra. Simarjeet finished with **5/54 in ten overs**, while Prince Yadav took **3/50 in ten**. Between them they accounted for all eight dismissals, although the other bowlers supplied the remaining overs. [Innings and bowling table](https://cricketfieldguide.com/match/andhra-v-delhi-01m4y5zd6tfqnsss1jm199d8pt).

Their combined figures were eight wickets for 104 runs from twenty overs: an economy of **5.20 runs per over**. That describes the overs they delivered, not the whole innings. It would be wrong to divide their combined runs by all fifty overs, or to imply that the remaining bowlers did not contribute simply because they took no wickets.

## Four wickets remaining does not tell the whole story

Delhi's final total required **226 legal balls**: 37 × 6 + 4. That left 74 of the fifty-over allocation unused, equivalent to twelve overs and two balls. A four-wicket margin can therefore coexist with a substantial time cushion. [RCB result account](https://www.royalchallengers.com/rcb-cricket-news/news/virat-kohli-extends-purple-patch-into-domestic-cricket-with-131-as-delhi).

There was still a late cluster of wickets. Kohli departed at 274/3, and Delhi reached 292/6 before completing the chase. That sequence is a reason to retain both innings progress and the final result: the finish was not wicket-free, but the early scoring had left a modest distance to the target.
