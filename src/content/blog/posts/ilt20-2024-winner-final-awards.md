**MI Emirates won ILT20 2024**, beating Dubai Capitals in the final. The franchise identifies itself as the 2024 champion on its [official website](https://miemirates.com/AboutUs).

## The final score

At Dubai International Cricket Stadium on 17 February, MI Emirates made 208 for three and restricted Dubai Capitals to 163 for seven, winning by 45 runs. Nicholas Pooran finished on 57 not out from 27 balls, while Andre Fletcher made 53 from 37. Muhammad Waseem contributed 43 and Kusal Perera 38. [Final scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/82917/mie-vs-dcp-final-international-league-t20-2024)

Pooran struck two fours and six sixes: 44 of his 57 runs came in boundaries, approximately 77.2%. That calculation describes his final innings, not his whole season.

## The four belt awards

| Belt | Recipient | Award |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | James Vince | Leading run-scorer |
| White | Waqar Salamkheil | Bowling award |
| Red | Sikandar Raza | Most Valuable Player |
| Blue | Muhammad Waseem | Outstanding UAE player |

The season-wrap press release records Vince’s 356 runs, two more than Pooran’s 354. It also records **17 wickets each for Salamkheil and Fazalhaq Farooqi**, with the White Belt decided on economy rate. Salamkheil was the award recipient, but not the sole player with the most wickets. [Season-wrap press release carried by Al Bawaba](https://www.albawaba.com/business/pr/dp-world-ilt20-season-2-leaves-indelible-mark-one-best-and-keenly-fought-franchise)

## How the edition was structured

The second edition began on 19 January 2024. Six teams contested 30 league games before four playoff fixtures, including the final. The top four advanced; the top two met in Qualifier 1, whose loser had another route through Qualifier 2. [ICC tournament guide](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/ilt20-2024-all-you-need-to-know-about-the-league-and-how-to-watch-on-icc-tv)
