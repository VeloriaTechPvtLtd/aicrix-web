South Africa won the 2012 Test series in England 2–0 and took the top position in the ICC Test team rankings. They won the first and third matches, with a draw between them. Hashim Amla’s unbeaten 311 belonged to The Oval, not the deciding match at Lord’s. [ICC series report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/south-africa-claim-famous-win-over-england), [ICC Oval assessment](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/kallis-rises-to-second-in-reliance-icc-test-batting-rankings)

## The Oval: a single innings was enough

South Africa reached 637/2 declared, with Amla on 311 not out, Jacques Kallis unbeaten on 182 and Graeme Smith contributing 131. They won by an innings and 12 runs; Dale Steyn took 5/56 in England’s second innings. [ICC’s contemporary assessment](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/kallis-rises-to-second-in-reliance-icc-test-batting-rankings)

The three named batters supplied **624 runs**, leaving thirteen in the total for the rest of the batting and extras. The scorecard shows Alviro Petersen was dismissed for zero and thirteen extras completed the 637. [Oval scorecard](https://www.cricketweb.net/statsspider/test/2049.php)

That is why a table should retain the declaration marker. South Africa did not lose all ten wickets and did not need a second innings.

## Headingley: hundreds without a winner

The second Test, played from 2–6 August, was drawn. Alviro Petersen made 182 for South Africa and Kevin Pietersen replied with 149 for England. [Headingley scorecard](https://www.cricketweb.net/statsspider/test/2051.php)

Petersen’s 182 should not be confused with Kallis’s unbeaten 182 in the previous match. Matching run totals can describe different players, grounds and outcomes.

## Lord’s: a target of 346

England needed 346 to win and square the series. Matt Prior’s 73, Jonathan Trott’s 63 and Jonny Bairstow’s 54 kept the chase competitive, but South Africa won by 51 runs on 20 August. That sealed both the series and the rankings change. [ICC final-day report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/south-africa-claim-famous-win-over-england)

The 2–0 label counts matches, not innings. It includes the Headingley draw, while the innings margin at The Oval and run margin at Lord’s describe two different forms of victory within the same tour.
