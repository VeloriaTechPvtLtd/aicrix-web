India B won the opening Duleep Trophy match against India A at Bengaluru in September 2024 by 76 runs. The four innings totals explain the result: India B made 321 and 184; India A replied with 231 and 198. [Match scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/104202/inda-vs-indb-1st-match-duleep-trophy-2024)

## A rescue with only three wickets left

India B were 94/7 when Navdeep Saini joined Musheer Khan. They added 205 for the eighth wicket before Musheer fell for 181. Saini finished with 56, and India B reached 321. Rajasthan Royals’ contemporary report records the stand as lasting 403 balls. [Report on Saini and Musheer](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/duleep-trophy-2024-navdeep-saini-highest-first-class-score-record-partnership)

Their partnership supplied approximately **63.9%** of the innings: 205 ÷ 321 × 100. Its value was not just the runs, but the starting position. With seven wickets already lost, India B had little room for another dismissal. Survival and accumulation converted a threatened small total into a substantial one.

## Why 184 was enough for the second innings

India A’s 231 left India B a first-innings lead of 90. Adding the second-innings 184 produced an overall lead of 274 and a winning target of **275**. [Innings totals](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/104202/inda-vs-indb-1st-match-duleep-trophy-2024)

A second-innings total should therefore not be judged alone. The first-innings advantage travelled forward: 321 − 231 + 184 + 1 = 275.

## Three quick wickets disrupted the chase

India A lost their third wicket at 75 and their fourth and fifth at 76. KL Rahul made 57, but the innings ended at 198. [Fourth-innings scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/104202/inda-vs-indb-1st-match-duleep-trophy-2024)

The margin is 274 − 198 = **76**, not 275 − 198. One calculation measures the lead remaining; the other measures how many further runs India A needed to win. The first-innings rescue and the later cluster of wickets both belong in the explanation of the result.
