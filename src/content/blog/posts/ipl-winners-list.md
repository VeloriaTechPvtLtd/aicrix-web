## IPL title totals by franchise

**Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings have five titles each.** Kolkata Knight Riders follow with three, while RCB have two. Four other franchises have won once.

These counts are calculated from the 19 season rows above: **5 + 5 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 19**. Eight different franchises have won the competition. A runner-up appearance does not add to a team’s title total, and a change in name does not create a new championship.

## IPL titles by winning captain

**MS Dhoni and Rohit Sharma have each captained five IPL final victories.** Dhoni led Chennai’s five title wins; Rohit led Mumbai’s five. The [official Dhoni captaincy retrospective](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ruturaj-gaikwad-takes-over-chennai-super-kings-captaincy-from-ms-dhoni) lists all five Chennai seasons.

Gautam Gambhir won twice with KKR, in 2012 and 2014. Rajat Patidar won twice with RCB, in 2025 and 2026. Shane Warne, Adam Gilchrist, David Warner, Hardik Pandya and Shreyas Iyer each captained one championship-winning final.

That makes nine different winning captains across the 19 editions. This is a captaincy count, so winning a trophy as another player in the squad does not add a title to this particular list. It also avoids treating the current captain of a franchise as the captain of its earlier championship teams.

## The three successful title defences

Only three franchises won consecutive editions within this 2008–2026 ledger:

- **Chennai Super Kings:** 2010 and 2011, under MS Dhoni.

- **Mumbai Indians:** 2019 and 2020, under Rohit Sharma.

- **Royal Challengers Bengaluru:** 2025 and 2026, under Rajat Patidar.

The latest defence came after RCB’s six-run win over Punjab Kings in 2025. In the 2026 final, they chased Gujarat’s 155/8 and finished on 161/5 with 12 balls remaining. The result added a second trophy, rather than replacing the maiden 2025 title. ## Team names that can change the answer

**Deccan Chargers and Sunrisers Hyderabad have one title each.** Deccan won in 2009; Sunrisers won in 2016. They are separate franchises. An [IPL release from March 2013](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/yes-bank-partners-with-ipl) describes Deccan as the former Hyderabad franchise and Sunrisers as a new franchise. Combining their trophies would incorrectly give SRH two titles.

Royal Challengers Bangalore and Royal Challengers Bengaluru are the same franchise. The team [changed its name in 2024](https://www.royalchallengers.com/rcb-cricket-news/news/rcb-renamed-as-royal-challengers-bengaluru-at-rcb-unbox-2024). The earlier name therefore remains appropriate for the 2009, 2011 and 2016 runner-up rows, while the 2025 and 2026 winners use Bengaluru.

Likewise, Kings XI Punjab’s 2014 runner-up finish and Punjab Kings’ 2025 runner-up finish belong to the same franchise. The [official Punjab rename announcement](https://www.punjabkingsipl.in/news/kings-xi-punjab-is-now-punjab-kings) is dated 17 February 2021.

## How to use the history

For a question limited to 2008–2025, stop before the 2026 row: RCB then have one title. For a list through 2026, include both RCB wins. Keep the season cutoff attached when comparing older articles or screenshots.

Championships summarize completed seasons. They do not by themselves predict the next match or identify a fantasy selection: current squads, roles, availability and conditions can change. The 2027 champion remains undetermined in this November 2026 reference.
