England beat South Africa by seven wickets at The Oval on 19 June 2013. South Africa recovered from 80/8 to 175; England reached 179/3 in 37.3 overs. [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/11560/eng-vs-rsa-1st-semi-final-icc-champions-trophy-2013)

## England created the crisis

James Anderson took 2/14 from eight overs and James Tredwell 3/19 from seven. [Bowling record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/11560/eng-vs-rsa-1st-semi-final-icc-champions-trophy-2013)

Together they took five wickets for 33 runs across 15 overs. The combined economy rate was 2.20, while Anderson’s individual rate was 1.75. Those figures describe both restriction and wickets; neither needs to be inferred from the final result.

## One partnership more than doubled the score

David Miller finished unbeaten on 56 and Rory Kleinveldt made 43. Their ninth-wicket stand added 95 before Stuart Broad removed Kleinveldt and Lonwabo Tsotsobe on successive deliveries. The innings ended in 38.4 overs. [Batting and dismissal sequence](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/11560/eng-vs-rsa-1st-semi-final-icc-champions-trophy-2013)

The stand supplied approximately 54.3% of the final total. It more than doubled the score from its starting point, yet South Africa still left 68 legal balls unused. Once the ninth wicket fell, the innings ended immediately on the next ball. Remaining overs could not compensate for the absence of batting resources.

## England’s repair had more room

Jonathan Trott made 82 not out. His third-wicket partnership with Joe Root, who scored 48, added 105 from 41/2 to 146/3. [Chase record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/11560/eng-vs-rsa-1st-semi-final-icc-champions-trophy-2013)

That stand covered approximately 59.7% of the target of 176. It began with eight wickets available, unlike South Africa’s rescue with only two. Both partnerships were valuable, but their starting positions and remaining risks were different. England’s finish left 75 legal balls unused and seven wickets intact.
