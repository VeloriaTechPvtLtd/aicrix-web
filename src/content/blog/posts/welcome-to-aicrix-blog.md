Welcome to the AICRIX blog. This is a place for cricket explainers and practical guides to understanding match information, statistics and predictions.

Read each article in context. A rules explainer should identify the relevant rules, a statistic should name its sample, and a match preview should show which fixture it covers. Older previews are historical material, not current team news.

Predictions express uncertainty. They are not guarantees of a result or a substitute for confirmed match information. Where an article discusses a general analytical method, that should not be read as a promise about a particular feature in the app.
