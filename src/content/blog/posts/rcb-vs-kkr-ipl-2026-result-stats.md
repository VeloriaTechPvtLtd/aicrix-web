## How KKR reached 192

KKR recovered from 48/2 through Angkrish Raghuvanshi's 71 from 46 balls. He shared 68 with Cameron Green, who made 32, and another 76 with Rinku Singh. Rinku finished unbeaten on 49 from 29 deliveries.

Those partnerships supplied the middle and late innings after the openers had departed. ## Kohli supplied the innings that lasted

Virat Kohli's unbeaten 105 was the central contribution in RCB's reply. His second-wicket partnership with Devdutt Padikkal added 92 from 59 balls; Padikkal made 39. Kartik Tyagi's 3/32 and Sunil Narine's 1/31 kept producing resistance, but neither removed Kohli.

[The official match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-57-rcb-vs-kkr-match-report) records Jitesh Sharma hitting the finishing boundary. The important contrast was not simply that RCB had a century and KKR did not: Kohli remained available through the conclusion of the chase, while partners changed around him.

## Three comparisons behind the headline

**The chase used 115 legal balls.** Cricket notation makes 19.1 overs equal to 19 × 6 + 1, not 19.1 decimal overs. That leaves five of the scheduled 120 deliveries unused. A six-wicket margin describes wickets remaining; it does not mean the chase ended six overs early.

**Kohli scored 54.1% of RCB's total.** His 105 divided by 194, multiplied by 100, gives that share, including extras in the team total. His own strike rate was 175.00. The two measures describe different things: contribution to the total and scoring speed.

**KKR's successful wicket-takers were also economical relative to the chase.** The [Cricbuzz scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/152130/rcb-vs-kkr-57th-match-indian-premier-league-2026) records four overs each for Tyagi and Narine. Together they conceded 63 and took all four wickets, an economy of 7.875, or 7.88 rounded. That does not prove how every individual matchup unfolded, but it prevents their losing team's result from obscuring their returns.

The match summary is therefore an RCB win built around a sustained chase. It is useful historical context, not evidence that either side will have the same XI, roles or advantage at their next meeting.
