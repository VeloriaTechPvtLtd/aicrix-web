## Dubai at 8 PM IST: seam, spin, dew

Dubai under lights is a sequence.

**New ball.** Some seam and bounce early. A hard length in the powerplay is not free.

**Middle overs.** The surface typically dries and grips. Spinners who change pace, not just rip, control this phase. That is Thailand's lane.

**Dew.** An 8:00 PM IST start is an evening game. Late-August Dubai humidity regularly brings dew later. The ball skids, grip dies, slower balls stop biting. Chasing sides usually get the easier half.

**Totals.** Men's T20s here often cluster around a mid-140s first innings, with 160+ strong. Women's T20s will sit lower. Treat **130–150** as the practical conversation until this strip speaks. There is no clean published "women's Dubai average" worth pretending we have.

**Toss lean.** Bowl first if the strip looks dry and dew is expected. Default Dubai night call. If the new ball is doing plenty and dew looks light, the lean flattens. Rebuild after the toss.
