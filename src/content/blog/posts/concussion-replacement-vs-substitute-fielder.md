A substitute fielder and a concussion replacement are not interchangeable. Seeing a new face on the boundary does not establish that the team has changed its playing personnel for every purpose. The useful first question is not simply who came on, but which replacement process was approved.

## What changes in the match

Under the October 2026 ICC men’s ODI conditions, an ordinary substitute covers fielding, with umpire permission. He cannot bat, bowl or captain; wicketkeeping requires consent. The original nominated player can return, subject to applicable absence restrictions. An approved concussion replacement instead joins the nominated players for the remainder, able to participate fully within the referee’s restrictions and subject to inherited warnings, penalty time and suspensions. The replaced player cannot resume; both count as match participants for statistics. [Conditions, clauses 1.2.7, 24–25 and Appendix A8](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bxkzkcyvfn6tvi70zh0w.pdf).

Medical diagnosis of concussion or suspected concussion follows a qualifying head or neck injury during play. The medical representative or manager promptly requests approval from the Match Referee, normally using a predesignated replacement. The referee considers likely remaining duties, can restrict involvement to prevent excessive advantage, and makes the final decision. It is not an unrestricted tactical swap.

## Read the approval rather than the player label

Consider a hypothetical substitute who takes a catch. That contribution alone does not establish that he can later bowl. Now imagine an all-rounder proposed for a batter’s concussion replacement. The label “all-rounder” is not the decision: the relevant question is what participation the referee permits. These examples illustrate why a roster category cannot replace the official match-specific status.

For match analysis, keep three separate pieces of information: who was originally nominated, what change was approved, and what the incoming player actually did. A short commentary label such as “sub” may not answer all three. This also avoids mistaking a new squad call-up for an in-match replacement. They concern different stages of selection.

## A practical viewing checklist

Look for the official replacement announcement and any role restriction before changing an expected batting or bowling contribution. Treat a later medical update, competition eligibility and confirmed selection as separate facts. For another competition, check its own conditions rather than importing this ODI explanation into the IPL, WPL or a club match. Sporting permission also does not determine a fantasy platform’s points: those require that platform’s applicable scoring rules.
