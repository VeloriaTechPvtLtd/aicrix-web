Jemimah Rodrigues completed the 2026 Women's Premier League with 771 career WPL runs for Delhi Capitals. Her record covered 37 matches but 34 batting innings, with eight not-outs. That distinction matters: the published batting average of 29.65 is based on 26 dismissals, not on every match in which she appeared. [Career batting ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/13517/jemimah-rodrigues)

Her highest season total came in 2026, when she scored 264. Yet her strongest published season average and strike rate remained those from 2024. The four-year comparison shows why “most runs” and “best scoring rate” should not be used interchangeably.

The increase from 146 runs in 2025 to 264 in 2026 was 118 runs, or approximately 80.82%. That is a useful description of the change in output, but not a standalone measure of improved efficiency: opportunity and the number of innings also matter. In 2024 she scored 29 fewer runs than in 2026 while posting the higher average and strike rate.

## Checking the 2026 denominators

Cricbuzz's 2026 season panel explicitly records ten innings, two not-outs and 186 balls faced for Rodrigues. That gives eight dismissals, making the average 264 divided by eight, exactly 33. Her scoring rate is 264 divided by 186 and multiplied by 100, or 141.94 to two decimal places. [Season panel](https://m.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/139786/player/13517/batting)

These are separate calculations. Average measures runs per dismissal; strike rate measures runs per hundred balls. Neither is obtained by dividing runs by matches. The exact balls-faced denominator comes from the ledger, not from working backwards from a rounded strike rate.

Her 33 fours and two sixes in 2026 supplied 144 boundary runs. That represents approximately 54.55% of her 264; the remaining 120 runs were not credited as boundaries. This distribution describes how the runs were accumulated, without assuming that every non-boundary run was a single. [Season statistics](https://m.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/139786/player/13517/batting)

## Reading the career aggregate correctly

The career ledger records 549 balls faced. Using the full totals gives 771 divided by 549 times 100, or a strike rate of 140.44. The average follows a different denominator: 771 divided by 26 dismissals is 29.65. [Career ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/13517/jemimah-rodrigues)

A simple mean of the four season strike rates would give every season equal weight, irrespective of the balls faced. That is not how the career figure should be calculated. The same caution applies to averaging seasonal batting averages without accounting for dismissals.

The most useful summary is therefore specific: 2026 brought Rodrigues her largest WPL season run total through this cutoff, while 2024 retained the stronger season rate figures. Those completed records support comparison; they do not establish what she will score in a future fixture or guarantee a particular batting position.
