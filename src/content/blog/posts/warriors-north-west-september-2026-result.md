## Why 15.3 overs means 27 balls left

Cricket’s over notation is not decimal notation. Fifteen completed overs plus three deliveries equals 93 legal balls. A full T20 allocation contains 120, so 120 minus 93 leaves 27. Reading 15.3 as 15.3 ordinary units would give the wrong answer.

Nor was the winning margin three runs simply because 131 minus 128 equals three. The chasing side won with seven wickets remaining. Target 129 says what was needed; final score 131 records where the innings actually finished. Those numbers answer different questions.

This match supports a clear historical conclusion: North West’s early wickets created a manageable chase, and Hermann finished it comfortably. It does not establish a permanent Centurion par score, prove that choosing to field caused the win, or supply current team selections. Keep that single-match boundary when using the result for later comparisons.
