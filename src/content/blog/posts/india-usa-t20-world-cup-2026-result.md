India beat the USA by 29 runs at Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai on 7 February 2026. India finished on 161/9 and restricted the USA to 132/8 in its opening match of the men's T20 World Cup. Suryakumar Yadav was Player of the Match for an unbeaten 84 from 49 balls. [BCCI match details](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/96904c17-3f58-482b-8539-389f189270dc/india-vs-united-states-of-america/match-details).

The margin alone misses how difficult India's innings became. This was a recovery from six wickets down, followed by a defence in which early wickets left the USA needing to rebuild while keeping up with the required rate.

The target was 162. The winning margin is the difference between the completed team totals, 161 minus 132; it is not the number the USA still needed to win.

## Three wickets in an over changed the innings

Ali Khan removed Abhishek Sharma without scoring. Ishan Kishan and Tilak Varma then added 37, before Shadley van Schalkwyk dismissed Ishan, Tilak and Shivam Dube in the sixth over. India ended the powerplay at 46/4.

A 26-run partnership between Suryakumar and Rinku Singh slowed the wicket loss without fully repairing the innings. Rinku and Hardik Pandya fell in successive overs. Axar Patel then supplied 14 in a 41-run seventh-wicket stand with Suryakumar. [ICC opening-day report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/pakistan-v-netherlands-t20-world-cup-opener).

The partnerships mattered in different ways. Surviving after four early wickets was not the same task as accelerating with the lower order. Treating the whole innings as one scoring rate hides that change in circumstances.

## The recovery from 77 for six

The fall-of-wickets ledger places India at 77/6 after 12.4 overs. That notation means 76 legal balls had been delivered, leaving 44 of the scheduled 120. India added 84 runs in that remaining period, a rate of 11.45 per six legal balls. [Full scorecard and wicket sequence](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/138974/ind-vs-usa-3rd-match-group-a-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2026).

Those 84 were team runs during a segment. They must not be confused with Suryakumar's individual 84 across his entire innings. The identical totals do not mean he scored every run after the sixth wicket.

His final contribution was 52.17% of India's 161, using the team total including extras as the denominator. That shows how concentrated India's scoring became without suggesting the other partnerships were irrelevant.

## Early wickets made the USA chase harder

Siraj and Arshdeep struck early, and the USA slipped to 13/3. Milind Kumar and Sanjay Krishnamurthi added 58 before Milind was stumped for 34. The recovery kept the chase alive, but it did not remove the need to accelerate. [ICC account of the defence](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/pakistan-v-netherlands-t20-world-cup-opener).

At 71/4 after 12 overs, the USA still needed 91 from 48 balls, an asking rate of 11.38. Siraj finished with 3/29; Arshdeep and Axar took two wickets each. The USA completed its allocation rather than being bowled out, but remained 29 runs short of India's total.

The historical lesson is specific: India survived a batting collapse and defended the recovery. This match does not establish a universal winning score at Wankhede, nor does one completed result determine a future India–USA contest.
