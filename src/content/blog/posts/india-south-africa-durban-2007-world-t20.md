**India beat South Africa by 37 runs at Kingsmead, Durban, on 20 September 2007.** India made 153/5 and restricted South Africa to 116/9, both innings lasting 20 overs. Rohit Sharma’s unbeaten 50 earned the match award; RP Singh took 4/13. [Complete match card](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/south-africa-vs-india-scorecard-icc-world-t20-s9920-122503/).

This was the Super Eight Group E match, not the final. South Africa’s winning target was **154**. The defeat also eliminated the hosts, while India and New Zealand progressed from a group in which all three finished on four points. [ICC’s historical Group E account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/throwback-revisiting-the-previous-t20-world-cup-meetings-between-india-and-south-africa?sf193189401=1).

## Rohit and Dhoni repaired the first innings

India chose to bat but lost three wickets between 32 and 33. Gautam Gambhir made 19, Virender Sehwag 11 and Dinesh Karthik nought. Robin Uthappa’s dismissal left India 61/4 after 10.3 overs. Rohit and MS Dhoni then added 85 before Dhoni was run out for 45 at 146/5. [Batting and wicket ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/3684/rsa-vs-ind-24th-match-group-e-icc-world-t20-2007).

That fifth-wicket stand occupied **55 legal balls**, from 10.3 to 19.4. Its team rate was therefore 85 × 6 ÷ 55 = **9.27 runs per six balls**. India’s first 63 legal deliveries had produced 61, or 5.81 per six balls. The recovery changed the scoring pace as well as preserving wickets.

Rohit finished on 50 from 40 balls and Dhoni made 45 from 33. Their combined individual contribution was 95, or 62.1% of India’s total. It was not a 95-run partnership: Rohit had already started scoring before Dhoni arrived and remained after Dhoni departed. The actual stand is fixed by the score moving from 61/4 to 146/5.

Shaun Pollock’s 2/17 from four overs remained an economical spell despite India’s recovery. The other five South African bowlers together conceded 132 from sixteen overs, or 8.25 per over. Four byes and leg-byes completed the difference between the 149 runs charged to bowlers and India’s 153. [Full bowling figures](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/south-africa-vs-india-scorecard-icc-world-t20-s9920-122503/).

## Five early wickets changed the chase

RP Singh removed Herschelle Gibbs and Graeme Smith early. Sreesanth dismissed AB de Villiers, Justin Kemp was run out, and Pollock was bowled by RP. South Africa were 31/5 after 5.5 overs. The [completed scorecard](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/south-africa-vs-india-scorecard-icc-world-t20-s9920-122503/) records RP’s four-wicket performance and the 37-run result.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Start, target 154 | 154 | 120 | 7.70 |
| 31/5 after 5.5 overs | 123 | 85 | 8.68 |
| 100/6 after 16.4 overs | 54 | 20 | 16.20 |

These requirements are calculated from the [fall of wickets](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/3684/rsa-vs-ind-24th-match-group-e-icc-world-t20-2007). They describe the match chase only, not a qualification target or probability of victory.

Mark Boucher and Albie Morkel each made 36. Their sixth-wicket partnership added 69 in 65 legal balls, taking South Africa from 31/5 to 100/6. That was substantial resistance after the early damage, but its rate of 6.37 per six balls was below the winning requirement when they came together. The innings was rebuilt without bringing the match target closer at the necessary pace.

Source: [bowling card](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/south-africa-vs-india-scorecard-icc-world-t20-s9920-122503/). RP and Irfan conceded 29 in eight overs, a combined 3.63 per over. That partnership of restriction included a wicketless spell: Irfan’s contribution would be missed by a wickets-only comparison.

South Africa used all 120 legal balls and finished nine wickets down, rather than all out. Their last 20 balls after Boucher’s dismissal brought only 16 more runs and three wickets. The final margin was 153 − 116 = 37; the shortfall from the winning target was 154 − 116 = 38.

## Why the result also ended South Africa’s tournament

The final Group E table placed India first, New Zealand second and South Africa third, all with two wins and four points. Their published net run rates were +0.750, +0.050 and −0.116 respectively. England finished fourth with no points. [Historical table](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/icc-t20-world-cup-2007-points-table-s9920/) · [ICC’s qualification explanation](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/throwback-revisiting-the-previous-t20-world-cup-meetings-between-india-and-south-africa?sf193189401=1).

The match result and group outcome answer different questions. India won this game by defending 153; the final standings determined which teams advanced. A qualification calculation should not replace 154 as the target required to win the match.
