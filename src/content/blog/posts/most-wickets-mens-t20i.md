## Rashid Khan's milestone and the current tally

The ICC reported that Rashid became the first bowler to reach 200 T20I wickets on 29 October 2026, during Afghanistan's second T20I against Zimbabwe. He reached the mark in his 120th appearance. The same report listed Adil Rashid on 171. [ICC milestone report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/rashid-makes-t20i-history-in-afghanistan-s-epic-comeback)

A milestone report and a current career table answer different questions. The former identifies when a landmark was reached; the latter records the total visible at a subsequent check. Keeping those dates separate prevents a writer from attaching a milestone's match count to a later total without verifying that the two belong together.

The difference between the two leading wicket totals in this snapshot is 29. That is a gap in accumulated wickets, not a rating-point lead or a forecast of how long the order will remain unchanged.

## Why Arshdeep's 150-wicket headline and 151 total both fit

DD News reported Arshdeep Singh's 150th T20I wicket on 30 October 2026 against New Zealand at Eden Park. He became the first Indian bowler to reach that milestone. [Dated milestone report](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/arshdeep-singh-becomes-first-indian-bowler-to-claim-150-t20i-wickets/)

His subsequent Cricbuzz profile records 151 T20I wickets and a further 1/40 against New Zealand on 1 November. That supplies the bridge between the dated headline and the later career total, rather than treating the difference as an unexplained contradiction. The profile's international career column is labeled “T20,” while IPL is shown separately; its opponent and career-date fields identify the international scope used here. ## How to compare the list without changing its meaning

Start with the question you want to answer. For international career accumulation, use this table. For wickets across franchise and international T20 cricket, use an all-T20 record. For World Cup performances, apply the tournament filter. Substituting one population for another can produce a plausible-looking number that answers the wrong question.

Career wicket totals also do not by themselves establish who is the best bowler for a specific upcoming match. They reflect accumulated outcomes. A selection analysis needs separate evidence about current availability, expected workload, recent performance and the opposition. None of those is supplied by position in this list.

For a refreshed edition, check the entire group and the first players below the threshold together. Updating only the leader risks missing a new qualifier or an altered tie. Preserve the check date alongside the table so readers can distinguish a verified snapshot from a live statistics feed.
