The subsequent trade is a separate fact: on 11 November 2026, the IPL confirmed his move to Mumbai Indians in the trade taking Hardik Pandya to Punjab Kings. His historical Punjab statistics should not be relabelled as Mumbai performances. ## What changed between 2025 and 2026?

The wicket count fell from 21 to 14, but opportunity also changed: Arshdeep delivered 32 fewer legal balls. Comparing balls per wicket separates those effects. He needed 16.67 deliveries per wicket in 2025 and 22.71 in 2026, so the reduction in wickets was not simply a consequence of fewer overs.

Run prevention moved in the same direction. He conceded 23 more runs despite bowling fewer deliveries. His economy rose from 8.88 to 10.21 runs per six-ball over. Bowling average, which divides conceded runs by wickets, increased from 24.67 to 38.64. These describe a more expensive and less wicket-efficient season; they do not explain which tactics or conditions caused it.

For a workload comparison, 318 balls equal exactly 53 overs. The previous season’s 350 balls are 58 overs and two balls, not 58.2 decimal overs. Calculating economy directly from legal balls avoids that common conversion error.

## An important performance inside a difficult season

Against Mumbai at Wankhede on 16 April 2026, Arshdeep struck twice in the third over and reached the 100-wicket IPL milestone. Punjab ultimately won by seven wickets. His season-best figures were 3 for 22 against Mumbai. [Official match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-24-mi-vs-pbks-match-report).

That match belongs alongside the full-season figures, not instead of them. A strong spell can be decisive while the broader season remains expensive. Equally, a season average should not erase a specific successful contribution.

## The career baseline is broader than a phase label

Across his career, 2,032 legal balls and 3,111 conceded runs produce an economy of 9.19. His bowling average is 28.03 and strike rate is 18.31 balls per wicket. These are combined-count calculations, not simple averages of eight annual rates. [Career bowling record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/13217/arshdeep-singh).

The league’s trade statement describes both new-ball and late-innings skills. It does not allocate a fixed number of powerplay or death overs in Mumbai’s future matches. That allocation depends on selection, conditions and captaincy decisions. A claim about his exact phase performance would require delivery-level analysis, which this career table does not provide.

As of 14 November 2026, the summary is 111 IPL wickets through 2026, a weaker 2026 return than 2025, and a confirmed move to MI ahead of 2027. Keep those three timelines clear when reading a squad page that still displays Punjab branding.
