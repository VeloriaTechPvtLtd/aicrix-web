## Why the average is not 30.50

Dividing 305 runs by ten innings gives 30.50 runs per innings. Batting average uses dismissals instead: 305 divided by nine gives 33.89. Both are useful, but they answer different questions. Dropping the not-out information would produce an incorrect batting average.

The combined strike rate is calculated from 305 runs and 207 balls, not by averaging the ten individual strike rates. A six-ball innings and a 49-ball innings should not receive equal weight in that calculation.
