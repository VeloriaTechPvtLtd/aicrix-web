Antigua and Barbuda Falcons won the CPL 2026 final by eight wickets, chasing Jamaica Kingsmen’s 170/9 with 20 balls remaining. The Falcons finished on 173/2 to claim their first Caribbean Premier League title. Sufyan Moqim’s 4/14 broke the middle order before Amir Jangoo and Hasan Nawaz completed an unbeaten 89-run partnership. ## Sadaqat’s century did not prevent a middle order collapse

Maaz Sadaqat made 114 from 58 balls, including eight fours and nine sixes. Saim Ayub’s 29 was Jamaica’s next-highest score. The other nine batting entries added only 15 runs, while extras contributed 12. Those components reconcile to the team’s 170. [Full batting ledger](https://livescore.sportzwiki.com/matches/antigua-and-barbuda-falcons-vs-jamaica-kingsmen-98194/scorecard/)

Sadaqat and Ayub added 80 for the third wicket, taking Jamaica from 30/2 to 110/3. That recovery makes the next sequence especially important: Powell fell at the same total, Morris at 112 and Hassan Khan at 113. Jamaica had moved from two wickets down to six without adding a fourth run beyond 110.

Moqim removed Ayub, Powell, Hassan Khan and Keemo Paul. His four overs cost 14 runs, an economy rate of 3.50. Shadab Khan took Morris’s wicket and finished with 1/28. The two spells combined for five wickets and 42 runs from eight overs, but Moqim’s 4/14 remains the individual innings figure. [Bowling and fall-of-wicket ledger](https://livescore.sportzwiki.com/matches/antigua-and-barbuda-falcons-vs-jamaica-kingsmen-98194/scorecard/)

Sadaqat stayed until the penultimate ball, helping the Kingsmen add 57 after their sixth wicket. The century therefore supplied both early scoring and late resistance. It was not enough to give the chasing side a demanding finish.

## The Falcons changed the required rate early

Rahkeem Cornwall scored 20 from six balls before losing his wicket at the end of the first over. The Falcons then reached 76/1 after six overs, compared with Jamaica’s 59/2 at the same stage. That was a 17-run advantage with one fewer wicket lost. [CPL account of both powerplays](https://cplt20.prezly.com/falcons-complete-dream-flight-to-maiden-title)

The effect on the chase is clearer when the target and balls remaining are kept together:

These are calculations from the score checkpoints, not win-probability estimates. Lewis’s dismissal at 84/2 briefly raised the required rate, but the Falcons still needed fewer than eight runs per over. They did not need every remaining batter to match Cornwall’s opening burst.

## Jangoo and Nawaz finished without another wicket

Jangoo ended on 57 not out from 34 balls and Nawaz on 47 not out from 35. Their unbroken stand carried the Falcons from 84/2 to 173/2. Do not add their full innings scores and call that the partnership: Jangoo had already scored runs before Nawaz arrived. [Final innings scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/154711/abf-vs-jkm-final-cpl-2026)

Nawaz struck the winning six at 16.4 overs. That notation means 100 legal balls had been completed, leaving 20 of the available 120. The boundary took the score beyond the minimum target of 171, but the result is still expressed as an eight-wicket win, not a two-run victory. [Final-ball commentary](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scores/154711/final-caribbean-premier-league-2026)

Moqim received the player-of-the-match award; Shadab was named player of the tournament. Those are different awards for different scopes. The final’s defining contrast was Jamaica’s dependence on one large innings against a chase shared across four batters, with two wickets lost.
