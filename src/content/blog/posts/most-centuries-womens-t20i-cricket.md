**Esha Oza leads the women’s T20I century list with five hundreds.** Chamari Athapaththu and Laura Wolvaardt follow with four each. The career records retrieved on 14 November 2026 contain seven batters with at least three centuries. This is an international T20 list, so domestic leagues and the other international formats are excluded. [ACS career-century record](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Womens_International_Twenty20/Overall/Players/Most_Centuries_in_Career.html).

The qualification is three or more, not a fixed five-player list. All four players on three therefore belong in the table.

The team column gives career affiliations, not a country-by-country split of the centuries. Crickrida’s opened table is labelled 9 November 2026; the retrieval date is not an independently established last-match cutoff.

The joint ranks also matter. Athapaththu and Wolvaardt both occupy second place; neither is demoted to third by an unstated alphabetical or innings tiebreak. Three players are above the group on three hundreds, making that group joint fourth.

## Why the list includes more than the usual major teams

Oza, Kibasu and Blake belong in an international record regardless of whether their teams play Test cricket. Restricting a list to a few familiar national teams would answer a different question. The record here follows the women’s T20I classification, rather than silently applying a Full Member-only filter.

An August 2026 RevSportz interview with Oza already described her five hundreds and the UAE’s ambitions for the Asia Cup. That is useful historical corroboration of her lead, but not a substitute for a later career ledger. Her subsequent appearances should be checked before the list is republished. [Oza interview, 22 August 2026](https://revsportz.in/looking-forward-to-beating-at-least-one-test-playing-nation-uae-skipper-esha-oza-ahead-of-womens-asia-cup/).

## Wolvaardt’s four and her eighteen are different records

Wolvaardt scored an unbeaten 126 against Zimbabwe on 15 September 2026. The ICC identified it as her fourth T20I century and her eighteenth international hundred across formats. The second number does not replace the first in this table. [ICC milestone report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/wolvaardt-record-ton-gives-proteas-unassailable-series-lead).

A reader looking at a headline about an international-century record can therefore see a much larger number without either table being inconsistent. First identify the set of matches: T20Is alone, ODIs alone, Tests alone or their combined international total.

Relative to Oza’s recorded five, a player on four needs one further T20I hundred to draw level and two to move ahead, assuming Oza’s count does not change. Those are conditional arithmetic examples, not forecasts of who will score next.

## Wyatt-Hodge’s third was also her first World Cup century

Wyatt-Hodge’s unbeaten 105 against Sri Lanka in England’s 2026 tournament opener was her third T20I hundred. It was also her first at the Women’s T20 World Cup. The ICC’s follow-up reported both facts. [ICC account, 13 June 2026](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/womens-t20-worldcup-2026/news/doing-it-for-daisy-wyatt-hodge-reveals-inspiration-for-ton).

That distinction prevents another common scope error. A World Cup-only century list is a subset of a career T20I list. A hundred in a qualifying T20I outside the World Cup still belongs in the career record; a domestic hundred does not.

## What a century count cannot tell you

A career count records repeated three-figure innings; it does not adjust for opposition, batting position, available runs in a chase or the number of opportunities to bat. Two players with three hundreds are tied on this metric, not proven equally strong in every aspect of batting.

Likewise, the player with the most total T20I runs need not have the most hundreds. A long series of useful scores below 100 increases career runs without changing this column. Use the dedicated run-scoring record for that question and a named player’s profile for her innings history. Keep this page focused on the century record and its dated boundary.
