*Records checked on 14 November 2026. Scope: men’s cricket.*

**Sahil Chauhan holds the men’s T20I record with a 27-ball century for Estonia against Cyprus on 17 June 2024.** The wider T20 category includes domestic and franchise matches, while T20I covers internationals with that status. That distinction explains why several different “fastest century” answers appear in cricket searches. [Current record overview](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/fastest-century-t20-cricket-list).

## The record depends on the competition

The following are category leaders, rather than four consecutive places in one ranking:

| Category | Batter and team | Opponent | Balls to century | Match date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All men’s T20Is | Sahil Chauhan, Estonia | Cyprus | 27 | 17 June 2024 |
| Men’s T20Is by a batter representing a Full Member | Abhishek Sharma, India | Afghanistan | 30 | 17 September 2026 |
| Men’s T20 World Cup | Finn Allen, New Zealand | South Africa | 33 | 4 March 2026 |
| IPL | Chris Gayle, Royal Challengers Bangalore | Pune Warriors India | 30 | 23 April 2013 |

The match reports and record confirmations for each category are linked below. “Bangalore” is the franchise name used in the 2013 IPL report.

## Sahil Chauhan and the 27-ball T20I record

Chauhan reached his hundred in 27 deliveries during Estonia’s tour of Cyprus, then finished **144 not out from 41 balls**. He batted at number four as Estonia chased a target of 192, and struck 18 sixes in the innings.

His century lowered the previous men’s T20I mark by six balls. Namibia’s Jan Nicol Loftie-Eaton had needed 33 earlier in 2024. Those are milestone timings: Chauhan’s eventual 41-ball innings does not change the 27-ball record. [ICC report, 17 June 2024](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/world-record-knock-sees-fastest-ever-men-s-t20i-century-hit-off-just-27-balls?sf191820426=1).

Estonia does not need Test status for this innings to count. The ICC extended T20I status to qualifying men’s T20 matches between its members from **1 January 2019**. Match-status requirements still apply; an international-looking fixture is not automatically a T20I. [ICC status announcement](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/all-t20-matches-between-icc-members-to-get-international-status).

## Abhishek Sharma and the Full Member distinction

Abhishek’s 30-ball hundred against Afghanistan in New Delhi on **17 September 2026** set India’s men’s T20I record. He finished with **108 from 34 balls**. The ICC described it as the third-fastest T20I century overall. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/sensational-abhishek-blazes-to-record-hundred?appview=true).

The current record overview also identifies it as the fastest by a batter representing an ICC Full Member. The BCCI separately calls it the fastest against a Test-playing nation. These filters refer to different sides of the fixture: the batter’s team and the opposition. Abhishek’s innings meets both descriptions. [Full Member record](https://www.olympics.com/ioc/news/fastest-century-t20-cricket-list), [BCCI record summary](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/abhishek-sharma-breaks-records-galore-with-superb-30-ball-hundred-vs-afg).

For the unrestricted T20I comparison, the BCCI places Chauhan’s 27 ahead of Muhammad Fahad’s 29 for Türkiye against Bulgaria in 2025, followed by Abhishek’s 30. Leaving Associate teams out would answer a narrower question than “fastest T20I century.”

## Finn Allen and the men’s World Cup record

Allen made **100 not out from 33 balls** against South Africa in the 2026 semifinal at Eden Gardens. New Zealand won by nine wickets on 4 March.

That innings set the men’s T20 World Cup record, replacing Chris Gayle’s 47-ball hundred against England in 2016. A World Cup-only record can be slower than the overall T20I record because it draws from a smaller set of matches. [ICC semifinal report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/live-new-zealand-take-on-unbeaten-south-africa-in-semi-final) and [completed-tournament record review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/all-records-broken-at-the-icc-men-s-t20-world-cup-2026?appview=true).

## Chris Gayle and the IPL record

Gayle reached 100 in **30 balls** for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Pune Warriors India on 23 April 2013. He went on to finish **175 not out from 66 balls**. [Official IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/report-match-31-rcb-vs-pwi).

A Rajasthan Royals review in April 2026 still identified his 30-ball hundred as the IPL benchmark. His performance belongs in franchise T20 records; it was not a West Indies international innings. [IPL record confirmation](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/vaibhav-sooryavanshi-records-century-rr-vs-srh-ipl-2026).

## How to read a fastest-century claim

Check the gender category, competition and match date first. Then separate **balls used to reach the century** from **balls faced in the complete innings**.

A final score of 108 from 34 balls cannot, by itself, establish whether the century arrived on ball 30 or ball 34. That requires milestone commentary or a reliable record report. Preserve the category when comparing players: all T20Is, Full Member T20Is, World Cup and IPL answer different questions.
