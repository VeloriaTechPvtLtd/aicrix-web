## A bigger one day score is not necessarily an ODI record

England’s 498/4 against the Netherlands, Tamil Nadu’s 506/2 against Arunachal Pradesh and Bihar’s later 574/6 are all men’s one-day batting milestones. Only the England innings came in an ODI. The domestic scores belong to List A cricket without becoming international scores.

List A is the wider statistical category in this comparison. An ODI innings can appear in both an ODI table and a List A table; a domestic Vijay Hazare Trophy innings belongs in the latter, not the former. That is why two pages can show different leading totals without contradicting each other.

Bihar’s later performance is recorded in the [completed scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/125261/arnp-vs-bih-plate-vijay-hazare-trophy-plate-2025-26). This selected chronology is not an exhaustive highest-scores list through November 2026.

## What changed when Tamil Nadu reached 506

The ICC’s November 2022 account explicitly compared Tamil Nadu’s domestic total with England’s earlier innings. The numerical improvement was eight runs: 506 − 498 = 8. That changed the broader men’s List A comparison, not the identity of the ODI innings.

To see the filter effect, take just the three rows above. With the domestic rows included, Bihar has the largest total. Apply an ODI-only filter and only England remains. The filter changes which innings qualify; it does not reduce Tamil Nadu’s or Bihar’s runs.

This is also why “50 overs” is not enough information for a record heading. Match classification and the teams involved must be retained alongside the innings length. A domestic game does not become an ODI because its schedule resembles an international one.

## Bihar supplied a later milestone

Bihar scored 574/6 in 50 overs at Ranchi on 24 December 2025. The innings exceeded Tamil Nadu’s 506 by 68 runs. The [completed match scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/125261/arnp-vs-bih-plate-vijay-hazare-trophy-plate-2025-26) preserves that total and the result. The ICC’s [dated account of the Bihar match](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/u19-cricket-worldcup-2026/news/sooryavanshi-scripts-list-a-history-as-u19-world-cup-looms?appview=true) identifies the senior domestic competition, opponent and Ranchi setting.

The comparison here remains deliberately limited to three men’s innings. It is not a combined men’s and women’s leaderboard, nor a claim that no later innings has exceeded these totals. A historical headline establishes what its writer reported at the time; it does not automatically establish the leading score on the day you read it.

## The highest total and biggest win are different records

More first-innings runs did not give Bihar the larger winning margin of these two domestic matches. Tamil Nadu dismissed Arunachal Pradesh for 71; Bihar later bowled them out for 177. [ICC’s Tamil Nadu report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/sangakkaras-odi-record-among-list-a-records-shattered-in-indias-domestic-one-day-competition) and [Bihar match scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/125261/arnp-vs-bih-plate-vijay-hazare-trophy-plate-2025-26).

| Match | First innings | Reply | Winning margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tamil Nadu against Arunachal Pradesh, 2022 | 506 | 71 | 435 runs |
| Bihar against Arunachal Pradesh, 2025 | 574 | 177 | 397 runs |

Bihar added 68 more first-innings runs, but the opposition reply increased by 106. The margin therefore fell by 38: 68 − 106 = −38. Direct subtraction confirms 435 − 397 = 38. Neither result makes one batting total smaller; the two records measure different things.

## Read the whole record label

Before comparing a one-day total, identify whether the table covers men’s or women’s cricket, ODIs or wider List A matches, team totals or individual innings, and a historical date or current ledger. Keep the opponent and match date with the score so the entry can be checked.

The useful answer is not that domestic records matter less. It is that a total must retain its category. England’s ODI innings and the two Vijay Hazare performances can all be described accurately without forcing them into one interchangeable “highest ODI score” list.

## Store the innings once, apply the right filter

In a record worksheet, give each match a single entry containing the date, teams, competition, match classification and innings total. Then apply a filter for the question being answered. England’s innings does not become two separate performances because it qualifies for both the ODI and broader List A views. Equally, excluding a domestic innings from the ODI view does not erase it from the wider comparison.

Use a separate field for the result margin. The first-innings total alone cannot supply that margin: the opposition reply is essential. In the two domestic examples above, the larger first innings and the larger victory belong to different matches. Preserving both fields is more useful than attaching a single ambiguous “record score” label.
