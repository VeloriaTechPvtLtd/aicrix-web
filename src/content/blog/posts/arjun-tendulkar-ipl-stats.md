Arjun Tendulkar’s IPL record through the completed 2026 season comprises six appearances, four wickets and 18 runs. The left-arm pace bowler’s record is spread across 2023, 2024 and 2026; this is an IPL-only snapshot, not an all-format biography. From those inputs, his bowling average is **37.50** (150 ÷ 4), strike rate **24.25 balls per wicket** (97 ÷ 4), and economy **9.28** (150 × 6 ÷ 97), rounded to two decimals.

The 97 balls equal 16 overs and one ball. Treating the notation 16.1 as a decimal denominator would give the wrong economy. The raw delivery count makes the rate reproducible.

## A catch and a wicket are separate contributions

Against Punjab Kings on 23 May 2026, Tendulkar caught Priyansh Arya off Mohammed Shami and later dismissed Prabhsimran Singh lbw. Punjab won by seven wickets. The catch belongs to his fielding record; it does not add a second wicket to his bowling figures. [Official IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-68-lsg-vs-pbks-match-report)

His four-over spell that season also accounts for 24 of his 97 career deliveries. A single appearance therefore forms a substantial part of this small bowling sample.

## Reading the batting record

The franchise table records 13 runs in 2023 and five in 2026, with one career not-out and an overall batting average of 18.00. It displays 0.00 for the 2026 average despite the unbeaten innings. [Official table](https://www.lucknowsupergiants.in/team/arjun-tendulkar-69659-profile)

For an innings with runs but no dismissal, runs per dismissal is undefined, not zero. That display should not be interpreted as a scoreless season. Likewise, appearances are not the batting-average denominator. Keep the season, opportunity and dismissal count attached before comparing this short record with a regular batter or bowler.
