**WPL 2026 ran from 9 January to 5 February, with 22 matches split equally between Navi Mumbai and Vadodara.** Royal Challengers Bengaluru won the final against Delhi Capitals. This is the completed 2026 archive, not the upcoming 2027 fixture list. [Official schedule announcement](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/tata-womens-premier-league-2026-schedule-announced), [official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-final).

The schedule consisted of 20 league matches, one Eliminator and one final. Below, times are the **announced Indian Standard Time starts**, not a claim that every first ball was delivered at exactly that moment. Results are final outcomes. ## How the playoff route finished

RCB reached the final directly as the league-stage leader. Gujarat and Delhi contested the Eliminator on 3 February. Delhi chased Gujarat’s 168/7, finishing on 169/3 in 15.4 overs. The final followed two calendar days later, on 5 February. [Official Eliminator report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-eliminator).

In the final, Delhi made 203/4 and RCB reached 204/4 in 19.4 overs. That was a six-wicket win with two legal deliveries left. The final report was published on **6 February**, but its publication date is not the match date. [Official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-final).

## The two afternoon starts

Only matches 2 and 10 were scheduled for 3:30 pm IST. Each was the first fixture of a double-header, on 10 and 17 January respectively. The other 20 matches had 7:30 pm IST scheduled starts.

That gives 22 matches across **20 match dates**: two dates each contained two games. The venue switch came after the 17 January double-header; the next fixture was in Vadodara on 19 January. These details make an archive more useful than a date range alone.

## Use the right edition

A season archive should remain labelled 2026 even when the league website promotes a later edition. For historical research, use the match number, opponent, date and venue together. For an upcoming game, move to the confirmed schedule for that season rather than treating a completed fixture as “today’s match.”
