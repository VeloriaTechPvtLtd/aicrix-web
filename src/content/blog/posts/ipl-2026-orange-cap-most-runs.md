## Vaibhav Sooryavanshi finished first with 776 runs

**Vaibhav Sooryavanshi won the IPL 2026 Orange Cap with 776 runs for Rajasthan Royals.** Shubman Gill finished second with 732 and Sai Sudharsan third with 722. The winner’s margin over Gill was 44 runs. These are completed-tournament totals, including the playoffs, rather than a league-stage or mid-season snapshot. [Official IPL season summary](https://www.iplt20.com/) · [Final standings](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2026/cricket-news/ipl-2026-orange-cap-full-list-who-is-leading-the-run-getters-table-after-todays-ipl-match).

## What changed at the finish

Kohli scored 75 not out in the final and finished the season on 675. Subtracting that innings gives a pre-final total of 600, below Klaasen’s completed 624 and Kishan’s 602. His final innings therefore explains why a table that still shows Klaasen fourth and Kishan fifth is not the final batting order. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-final-rcb-v-gt-match-report) · [Kohli’s final and season record](https://m.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/155409/player/1413/batting).

Gujarat’s own season review confirms Gill’s 732 from 16 matches and Sudharsan’s 722 from 17. Their ten-run separation is smaller than either player’s gap to Sooryavanshi: 44 for Gill and 54 for Sudharsan. Only those three reached 700 in the final table. ## Equal totals do not mean equal opportunities

Marsh and Abhishek both made 563, but in 13 and 15 batting innings respectively. Their runs per innings were therefore **43.31** and **37.53**, rounded to two decimals. This is an opportunity comparison, not a replacement Orange Cap ranking and not automatically their batting averages.

Batting average uses dismissals rather than innings. Kohli’s checked record contains 16 innings and four not-outs: 675 ÷ (16 − 4) = **56.25**. Dividing by all 16 innings instead gives **42.19 runs per innings**. Both calculations can be meaningful, but labelling the second one his batting average would be wrong. ## Use the right record for the question

This table answers who accumulated the most runs in IPL 2026. It does not rank career runs, the fastest scorers, the most valuable players or future fantasy returns. Six of its ten players represented Gujarat or Hyderabad, but that count is not those teams’ share of all tournament runs.
