**Mark Boucher’s 555 dismissals lead the men’s Test wicketkeeping career record.** Adam Gilchrist is next on 416, followed by Ian Healy and Rod Marsh. These four are the complete group with at least 300 in the [ACS table checked on 12 November 2026](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Test/Overall/Wicket_Keeping/Most_Dismissals_by_Wicketkeeper_in_a_Career.html).

The record combines catches taken while keeping and stumpings in Tests. It is separate from a player’s ODI keeping record and from catches taken as an ordinary fielder. Boucher’s lead is a count of completed dismissals, not a claim that he converted every chance or had the fewest errors.

The booklet is explicitly dated 1 January 2026; the later ACS check supplies the current threshold check.

MS Dhoni is immediately below the group on 294, six short of 300. The threshold is inclusive, so a player exactly on 300 would belong. None of these four totals is tied.

## Boucher’s lead is larger than the next two gaps combined

Boucher is 139 dismissals ahead of Gilchrist. Gilchrist leads Healy by 21, while Healy leads Marsh by 40. The second-to-fourth gap is therefore 61, compared with 139 between first and second.

Boucher’s components also explain why the leader does not need to lead every category. He has 153 more keeping catches than Gilchrist, but 14 fewer stumpings: **153−14=139**. The overall ranking follows the sum, not the stumping column alone. [Guinness’ Boucher record](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/wicket-keeping-most-dismissals-in-test-cricket), [ICC’s Gilchrist career summary](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/waqar-and-gilchrist-to-be-inducted-into-icc-hall-of-fame).

That distinction is especially useful when a headline says “most dismissals” without naming the field. A stumping leaderboard would be a different calculation and could have a different order. This table does not claim to be that leaderboard.

## Gilchrist and Marsh played the same number of Tests

Gilchrist and Marsh each played 96 Tests. Gilchrist finished 61 keeping dismissals ahead: 416 compared with 355. The difference consists of **36 more catches and 25 more stumpings**. The [ICC’s Gilchrist summary](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/waqar-and-gilchrist-to-be-inducted-into-icc-hall-of-fame) and [Marsh tribute](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/icc-expresses-sadness-at-the-passing-of-rodney-marsh) corroborate those career totals.

Equal match counts remove one obvious difference in career length, but do not make the opportunities identical. A Test can contain different amounts of time in the field, different numbers of opposition wickets and different kinds of chances. The 61-dismissal gap is a useful comparison of outcomes, not an experiment that isolates keeping skill.

## Why older reports name a different record holder

Marsh retired with 355, then a world record. Healy subsequently passed that mark and finished with 395, comprising 366 catches and 29 stumpings. His [Sport Australia Hall of Fame profile](https://sahof.org.au/hall-of-fame-member/ian-healy/) records the 356th dismissal in October 1998 and the final career total.

The record changed hands again during the Boucher–Gilchrist era. Gilchrist took the lead during his last Test at Adelaide and finished with 416. His final Test ended on **28 January 2008**, as confirmed by [ABC’s contemporary report](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2008-01-28/gilly-bows-out-as-test-ends-in-a-draw/1025836). The final 416 is independently preserved in the ICC’s later career summary.

Boucher regained the lead in February 2008 with dismissal number 417 against Bangladesh. The [contemporary Cricbuzz account](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-news/2672/south-africas-boucher-reclaims-wicketkeeping-record-cricbuzzcom) identifies the catch of Mushfiqur Rahim off Jacques Kallis that took him past Gilchrist.

A report calling Gilchrist the record holder can therefore be correct for its moment without answering the current question. The record-breaking total, the player’s final total and today’s table are three separate checkpoints. Do not replace one with another simply because a page has a recent crawl date.

## Keep the record’s role and format attached

A Test keeping table can include all recognized Test teams, rather than just country-only appearances. The SCG booklet accordingly labels Boucher’s career South Africa and World XI. A national-team filter should not silently be substituted for that all-Test population.

For a different format, use the men’s ODI keeping-dismissals table. For catches taken outside the keeper role, use the Test fielders’ catch record. Keeping those destinations separate avoids adding stumpings to an outfield-catching total or treating a familiar keeper’s every catch as a keeping event.

The answer here is the four-player Test group, led by Boucher on 555. A future update should check the complete threshold and its role notes, not merely change the first row while leaving the boundary unchecked.
