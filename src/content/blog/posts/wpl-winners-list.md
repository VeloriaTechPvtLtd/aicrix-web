*Complete through WPL 2026. Checked on 14 November 2026.*

**Mumbai Indians and Royal Challengers Bengaluru have won two WPL titles each.** Mumbai won in 2023 and 2025 under Harmanpreet Kaur. RCB won in 2024 and 2026 under Smriti Mandhana. Delhi Capitals were the runners-up in all four completed editions.

**RCB are the latest champions**, following their six-wicket victory over Delhi in the final on 5 February 2026. [Official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-final), [2026 final scorecard](https://www.delhicapitals.in/schedule-fixtures/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-delhi-capitals-blrdcw02052026268186).

Harmanpreet Kaur and Smriti Mandhana each led two title-winning finals. The four season rows produce four trophies in total: two for Mumbai and two for RCB. Neither team has won consecutive editions within this period.

Delhi’s four runner-up finishes represent four final appearances, not four championships. As of this cutoff, Mumbai and RCB share the most WPL titles; neither leads outright.

## The inaugural final in 2023

**26 March 2023, Brabourne Stadium, Mumbai:** Delhi finished on **131/9 in 20 overs**. Mumbai reached **134/3 in 19.3 overs**, winning by seven wickets with three balls remaining.

This was the first WPL championship. The league began in 2023, so its winners list starts there rather than in 2008. [Mumbai Indians final scorecard](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/scores/dc-vs-mi-match-dcwmin03262023221929), [BCCI inaugural-season announcement](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/bcci-announces-schedule-for-womens-premier-league-2023).

## RCB won their first title in 2024

**17 March 2024, Arun Jaitley Stadium, Delhi:** RCB bowled Delhi out for **113 in 18.3 overs**, then made **115/2 in 19.3 overs** to win by eight wickets.

Sophie Molineux took three wickets in one over after Delhi’s opening partnership of 64. Shreyanka Patil finished with 4/12, helping turn that start into a target of 114. [Official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2024-final), [final scorecard](https://www.delhicapitals.in/schedule-fixtures/delhi-capitals-vs-royal-challengers-bengaluru-dcwblr03172024240683).

## Mumbai regained the trophy in 2025

**15 March 2025, Brabourne Stadium, Mumbai:** Mumbai made **149/7**, then restricted Delhi to **141/9**, winning by eight runs. Both teams completed 20 overs.

Harmanpreet scored **66 from 44 balls** and received the Player of the Match award. Mumbai became the first team to win a second WPL title. Their total was 149; Delhi’s target was 150. [Official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2025-final), [final scorecard](https://www.delhicapitals.in/schedule-fixtures/delhi-capitals-vs-mumbai-indians-dcwmin03152025255541).

## RCB drew level with Mumbai in 2026

**5 February 2026, BCA Stadium, Vadodara:** Delhi posted **203/4 in 20 overs**. RCB reached **204/4 in 19.4 overs**, winning by six wickets with two balls left.

Mandhana scored **87 from 41 balls**, and Georgia Voll made **79 from 54**. RCB’s successful pursuit of 204 was the highest chase in WPL history through the end of that season. [Official final report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-final), [final scorecard](https://www.delhicapitals.in/schedule-fixtures/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-delhi-capitals-blrdcw02052026268186).

## The next WPL champion is still to be decided

The fifth edition is scheduled for **14 January to 7 February 2027**, with the final at Brabourne Stadium in Mumbai. RCB enter that edition as defending champions; the 2027 winner is not yet known in this November 2026 reference. [BCCI’s WPL 2027 schedule announcement](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/tata-wpl-2027-vadodara-and-mumbai-to-host-fifth-edition).

Keep these WPL counts separate from the men’s IPL trophy totals. The franchises share familiar names, but the two competitions have different championship histories.
