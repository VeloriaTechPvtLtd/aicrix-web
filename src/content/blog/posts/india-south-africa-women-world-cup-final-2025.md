## India won their first women’s ODI World Cup

India beat South Africa by 52 runs in the women’s ODI World Cup final at Dr DY Patil Sports Academy, Navi Mumbai, on 2 November 2025. India made 298/7 from 50 overs and dismissed South Africa for 246 in 45.3 overs.

It was India’s first title in this competition. Shafali Verma scored 87 and took two wickets; Deepti Sharma contributed 58 and 5/39. South Africa captain Laura Wolvaardt made 101, but her century did not carry the chase to its target of 299. ## India built a total rather than relying on one innings

Shafali’s 87 came from 78 balls. Smriti Mandhana made 45 in an opening partnership worth 104, giving India a base before the middle order took over. Deepti’s 58 came from 58 balls, while Richa Ghosh added 34 from 24.

The score moved from 245/5 after 43.1 overs to 298/7 at the end. That is 53 additional runs from 41 legal balls, equivalent to about 7.76 runs per six balls over that closing stretch. This is a calculation from the scorecard checkpoints, not a separate official phase statistic.

That contribution matters when reading the final through its most prominent individual score. Shafali supplied the largest innings, but India still needed runs after her dismissal. Ayabonga Khaka took three wickets for South Africa, and the completed total reflects both that wicket-taking and India’s later additions.

## Shafali also interrupted the chase

South Africa reached 114 before losing their third wicket. Shafali dismissed Sune Luus and then Marizanne Kapp, leaving the chase at 123/4. Her final bowling figures were 2/36 from seven overs.

This is why a batting-only summary misses part of her final. Her two wickets removed middle-order partners while Wolvaardt remained at the crease. The innings still had time to recover, but South Africa had fewer established wickets available for the later acceleration. ## Wolvaardt and Dercksen kept South Africa involved

After Sinalo Jafta’s dismissal at 148/5, Wolvaardt and Annerie Dercksen added 61 for the sixth wicket. Dercksen made 35. Their partnership took the score to 209 before Deepti removed Dercksen at 39.3 overs.

## Deepti finished the decisive wickets

Deepti dismissed Wolvaardt for 101 at 220/7 and then Chloe Tryon at 221/8. She finished with five wickets for 39 runs from 9.3 overs. South Africa’s final total remained 246 when Nadine de Klerk was dismissed, completing the 52-run victory.

Do not confuse the number needed to win with the official margin. At 246, South Africa were 53 runs short of the target of 299, but India’s winning margin was 298 minus 246: 52 runs. Reaching 298 would have levelled the scores, not won the chase.

## What to retain from this final

The result combines three different contributions: India’s opening platform, runs added late in the first innings, and wickets that repeatedly reduced South Africa’s options. Wolvaardt’s century is central to the chase, but an individual milestone and a successful team chase are different outcomes.
