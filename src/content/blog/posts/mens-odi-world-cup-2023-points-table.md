## Two qualification lines mattered

The first four reached the semifinals. India faced New Zealand at Wankhede Stadium on 15 November, while South Africa faced Australia at Eden Gardens on 16 November. The ICC confirmed these pairings before India’s last league match; the table above includes all nine league games per side. [ICC semifinal announcement](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/semi-finalists-confirmed-for-knockout-stage-of-cricket-world-cup)

Eighth place also mattered. The top eight in this table, including hosts Pakistan, qualified for the **2025 Champions Trophy**. Bangladesh took the final place, with Sri Lanka and the Netherlands outside that group. This was the qualification arrangement for that edition, not a standing promise about future tournaments. [ICC qualification explanation](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/champions-trophy-2025/news/icc-champions-trophy-2025-ultimate-guide-everything-you-need-to-know)

## Reading negative NRR correctly

Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and the Netherlands each had two wins and four points. Their NRR values were all negative, but −1.087 is greater than −1.419 and −1.825. A negative NRR is not a points deduction.

The completed rows reconcile to 45 wins and 45 losses across 45 league matches. Knockout appearances do not belong in these nine-match totals. Keep the league standings separate when describing what happened after qualification.
