India beat South Africa by 95 runs in the ICC KnockOut semifinal at Nairobi on 13 October 2000. Sourav Ganguly’s unbeaten 141 underpinned 295/6; South Africa were dismissed for 200. Zaheer Khan, Anil Kumble and Sachin Tendulkar took two wickets apiece. [ICC retrospective](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/india-v-south-africa-icc-champions-trophy-2017-a-look-ahead)

## Three stands, one constant

Ganguly faced 142 balls. Tendulkar made 39, Rahul Dravid 58 and Yuvraj Singh 41. India’s first three partnerships contributed 66, 145 and 82, bringing the score to 293 before the third wicket fell. [Full scorecard](https://img.cricinfo.com/db/ARCHIVE/2000-01/OD_TOURNEYS/ICCKO/SCORECARDS/IND_RSA_ICCKO_ODI-SEMI2_13OCT2000.html)

The second partnership supplied about 49.2% of India’s total, while Ganguly himself scored about 47.8%. These are different measurements: a partnership contains both batters’ runs and any extras during the stand. They cannot be added as independent contributions.

## A strong total with a poor finish

India lost four wickets during the final seven legal deliveries, moving from 293/2 to 295/6. Yuvraj fell at 48.6, Robin Singh at 49.1, Vinod Kambli at 49.2 and Vijay Dahiya at 49.6. [Scorecard sequence](https://img.cricinfo.com/db/ARCHIVE/2000-01/OD_TOURNEYS/ICCKO/SCORECARDS/IND_RSA_ICCKO_ODI-SEMI2_13OCT2000.html)

That is four wickets for two runs, not an uninterrupted final acceleration. The larger total was built before this sequence. Ganguly remaining unbeaten did not prevent wickets at the other end.

## The defence began with disruption

South Africa were 50/4. Mark Boucher later made 60 and Jonty Rhodes 32, but the innings ended after 41 overs. [Chase record](https://img.cricinfo.com/db/ARCHIVE/2000-01/OD_TOURNEYS/ICCKO/SCORECARDS/IND_RSA_ICCKO_ODI-SEMI2_13OCT2000.html)

South Africa therefore had 54 legal balls unused, but no wickets left with which to use them. The winning margin was 295−200=95; the target of 296 was 96 beyond their final score. India’s early partnerships and early wickets worked together: the batting created a demanding target and the defence removed much of the top order before it could establish comparable continuity.
