West Indies won their first Women's World T20 title in Kolkata on 3 April 2016, beating Australia by eight wickets. Australia made 148/5 from 20 overs; West Indies reached 149/2 in 19.3. Hayley Matthews scored 66, Stafanie Taylor 59 and Deandra Dottin an unbeaten 19. Matthews was Player of the Match. [ICC result and scores](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/west-indies-dethrones-australia-to-win-maiden-icc-womens-world-twenty20-title)

The result is best understood as a complete chase rather than a sudden finishing burst. West Indies first kept the requirement under control, then retained enough batting resources to absorb late wickets without losing their direction.

## Australia’s innings: two half-centuries, 148/5

Elyse Villani and Meg Lanning both made 52, while Ellyse Perry contributed 28. Dottin finished with 2/33, Matthews with 1/13 and Anisa Mohammed with 1/19. [ICC score summary](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/west-indies-dethrones-australia-to-win-maiden-icc-womens-world-twenty20-title)

## Dottin's last over deserves a place in the story

Australia were 112/2 with five overs remaining. Dottin conceded just one run in the final over and dismissed Perry after three dot balls. That restrained the finish and kept the total below 150. [ICC match narrative](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/taylor-matthews-take-west-indies-women-to-maiden-world-t20-title)

## Matthews and Taylor did not need an explosive first three overs

West Indies began with nine runs from three overs but reached 76 without loss at halfway. Matthews made 66 from 45 deliveries, including six fours and three sixes; Taylor's 59 occupied 57 balls. With five overs left, the target had been reduced to 32. [ICC chase account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/taylor-matthews-take-west-indies-women-to-maiden-world-t20-title)

The two innings had different tempos. Matthews's boundary contribution was 42 runs, calculated from her fours and sixes. Taylor's longer presence helped provide continuity. It would be misleading to treat their different strike rates as evidence that only one of them mattered.

## Late wickets did not undo the earlier work

Matthews was caught off Kristen Beams, and Taylor also fell before the winning runs. Dottin helped complete the chase. [ICC closing sequence](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/taylor-matthews-take-west-indies-women-to-maiden-world-t20-title)

Finishing with eight wickets and three balls unused describes two different resources. The wicket margin was large, while the time margin was smaller. Neither should be substituted for the other when characterising the contest.

The lasting feature of this final is how the winning side joined its contributions together: a restrictive last over with the ball, a sustained top-order chase and a successful handover after the openers departed. It was a change of champion produced through several connected phases, not one isolated moment.
