Zimbabwe scored 344/4 against Gambia on 23 October 2024 in the men’s T20 World Cup Africa Sub Regional Qualifier B. At the time, it overtook Nepal’s 314/3 against Mongolia as the highest men’s T20I total. This was a qualifying match, not a match in the main World Cup tournament. [ICC’s contemporary report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/zimbabwe-smash-world-record-for-highest-t20i-total)

Raza reached his hundred from 33 balls and finished with 15 sixes. Zimbabwe’s collective 27 sixes also established a men’s T20I record on that date. These are historical claims, not an assertion that every ranking remains unchanged today. [ICC report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/zimbabwe-smash-world-record-for-highest-t20i-total)

## Reconstructing the total

The card lists 30 fours and 27 sixes. Those boundaries account for **282 runs**: (30 × 4) + (27 × 6). That leaves 62, comprising 53 other batter runs and nine extras.

Raza supplied 133 of the 335 batter runs. The other five contributed 202, so describing the innings solely as his hundred would conceal much of the scoring. Marumani and Madande both passed fifty in fewer than 20 balls faced in their completed innings.

Gambia’s innings ended at 54, producing the recorded 290-run defeat: 344 − 54. The scorecard’s absent-hurt entry is a reason not to infer ten ordinary dismissals merely from a compact total display.
