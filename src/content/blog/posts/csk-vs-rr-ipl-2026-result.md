Rajasthan Royals beat Chennai Super Kings by **eight wickets on 30 March 2026**, reaching 128/2 after dismissing CSK for 127 at Barsapara Cricket Stadium in Guwahati. This archive covers that completed match, not the rivalry’s all-time balance. [Official match details](https://www.iplt20.com/matches/237143/More/match-details).

## Bowling created the advantage before the chase

Burger and Jofra Archer removed CSK's top three with only 19 on the board. CSK ended the powerplay at 41/4, then lost two wickets in Ravindra Jadeja's first over. Jamie Overton's 43 from 36 balls provided resistance, but Chennai were eventually all out. Burger received the award for his early breakthroughs rather than for taking more wickets than every other bowler. [IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-3-rr-vs-csk-match-report).

## Sooryavanshi reached fifty in 15 balls

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi reached fifty in 15 balls and finished with **52 from 17**. Rajasthan's opening surge took them to 74/0 after six overs. Yashasvi Jaiswal stayed unbeaten on 38, while Dhruv Jurel and Riyan Parag contributed 18 and 14 not out. [IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-3-rr-vs-csk-match-report).

These numbers answer different questions. Fifteen balls measures the fifty milestone; seventeen measures the completed individual innings. The eventual chase took 73 legal balls because other batters also faced deliveries. None of those counts should substitute for another in a records table.

## What the powerplay comparison tells you

Rajasthan's 74 against Chennai's 41 produced a **33-run gap after the same six-over allocation**. Just as importantly, RR had all ten wickets available, while CSK had already lost four. The comparison describes the match's opening phase more clearly than the winning margin alone. [IPL report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-3-rr-vs-csk-match-report).

It does not establish that every Guwahati chase is easier, or that winning the toss guarantees the result. A venue judgement needs a broader, comparable sample. For this match, the useful conclusion is narrower: RR combined early wickets with a rapid start, leaving a small target manageable without using their full batting order.
