## Why 2024 stands apart

Deepti made 295 runs in eight innings in 2024 and finished unbeaten five times. That leaves three dismissals, which explains the average of 98.33. Her runs per innings were 36.88, a different measure of output rather than a competing version of the official average.

That season accounts for approximately 46% of her career WPL runs through 2026. The other three seasons together contributed 343. Her career figure is therefore not a description of four similar campaigns; it combines a particularly productive batting season with much smaller returns.

The strongest illustration of her two-way value came against Delhi Capitals on 8 March 2024. She made 59 from 48 balls and took 4 for 19 as UP won by one run. Her hat-trick spanned two overs: Meg Lanning fell on her last ball of the 14th over, then Annabel Sutherland and Arundhati Reddy on her next two deliveries, at the start of the 19th over. The break between overs did not interrupt the sequence of her own deliveries. [Official match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2024-match-15).

## Fewer wickets also came with fewer deliveries

Deepti bowled in all 33 appearances, but she did not deliver four overs every time. Her 722 legal balls equal 120 overs and two balls. With 1,011 runs conceded, her career economy is 8.40; 34 wickets give a bowling average of 29.74. [Bowling ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/11098/deepti-sharma).

Between 2025 and 2026, her wicket total fell from eight to seven while her deliveries fell from 183 to 150. That is 33 fewer balls despite the same eight matches. Average workload decreased from 22.88 to 18.75 balls per appearance. Balls per wicket actually improved slightly, from 22.88 to 21.43. Looking only at the wicket totals would miss that change in opportunity.

## Batting position changed within the 2026 season

In Match 18 of WPL 2026, UP used Deepti alongside Meg Lanning as a new opening pair. Deepti scored 55 from 43 balls, but UP finished on 143 for eight and lost to RCB. The promotion is evidence about that match, not proof of a permanent opening role. [Official Match 18 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-18).

In UP’s final league game, she contributed 24 and dismissed Laura Wolvaardt and Chinelle Henry during Delhi’s chase. Delhi nevertheless won by five wickets. Those contributions belong in her record without turning them into a claim that an all-round return guarantees a result. [Official Match 20 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-20).

For future matches, the meaningful questions are where she will bat and how many overs she is likely to receive. Her historical record establishes both skills, but neither the 2024 average nor the all-rounder label fixes her next match’s opportunity.
