Hardik Pandya’s IPL record stood at **162 matches, 2,955 runs and 82 wickets** in the league’s 11 November 2026 trade announcement. That release confirmed his move from Mumbai Indians to Punjab Kings ahead of IPL 2027, with Arshdeep Singh moving the other way. [Official IPL advisory](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/hardik-pandya-and-arshdeep-singh-complete-major-trade-between-pbks-and-mi)

His completed 2026 statistics below belong to his Mumbai season, not to performances already made for Punjab.

Nine dismissals give a batting average of 206 ÷ 9 = **22.89**. His strike rate is 206 ÷ 149 × 100 = **138.26**. These are different from his 20.60 runs per innings.

Bowling calculations use a separate sample: 259 ÷ 4 = **64.75 runs per wicket**, 136 ÷ 4 = **34 balls per wicket**, and 259 × 6 ÷ 136 = **11.43 runs per over**. The delivery count is 22 overs and four balls, not 22.4 decimal overs.

## Best figures do not imply a full spell

The dated bowling ledger shows his 1/18 against Gujarat on 20 April at an economy of 18 runs per over. It was a one-over contribution, not four overs for 18 runs. The nine listed spells also show why ten match appearances cannot be assumed to mean ten bowling innings. [Season bowling entries](https://iplrecords.com/players/ipl-2026/hardik-pandya)

## Team announcement versus playing record

The November release gives a 2027 joining fee of ₹18 crore for Pandya and describes the completed trade. It does not establish a future batting position, captaincy assignment at Punjab or four-over role. [League trade details](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/hardik-pandya-and-arshdeep-singh-complete-major-trade-between-pbks-and-mi)

For a future fixture, those role questions need fresh team evidence. For this historical profile, keep three labels separate: career totals at the announcement, performances in IPL 2026, and the subsequent franchise move.
