## March: MI's opening partnership changed the chase

KKR's 220 included Ajinkya Rahane's 67 and Angkrish Raghuvanshi's 51. Shardul Thakur took 3/39. MI then received 78 from Rohit Sharma and 81 from Ryan Rickelton, with their opening partnership reaching 148 before Rohit fell at 11.5 overs. [Official Match 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-2-mi-vs-kkr-match-report).

At that wicket, MI needed 73 from 49 legal balls. The required rate had fallen from 11.05 at the start to about 8.94 runs per over. That is a more precise account of the stand's value than saying MI were “ahead”: it reduced the scoring demand while leaving nine wickets available.

Those calculations use a target of 221 and the recorded 148/1 at 11.5. In cricket notation, 11.5 means 71 balls, not 11.5 decimal overs. [March scorecard and fall of wickets](https://sports.ndtv.com/cricket/mi-vs-kkr-scorecard-live-cricket-score-ipl-2026-match-2-mikr03292026267883).

## May: KKR needed a recovery, not another 220-plus chase

The return match began very differently. MI lost four wickets by 5.3 overs. Corbin Bosch's unbeaten 32 helped them reach 147/8. In reply, Manish Pandey made 45 and Rovman Powell 40; their 64-run partnership restored KKR's innings after the third wicket. [Official Match 65 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-65-kkr-vs-mi-match-report).

KKR were 54/3 at 7.1 overs and 118/4 at the end of the 15th. The first checkpoint left 94 needed from 77 balls, around 7.32 per over. The second left 30 from 30. The partnership's value was not simply its size: it brought the requirement to a run a ball before the closing phase. [May fall of wickets](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/152218/kkr-vs-mi-65th-match-indian-premier-league-2026).

## How the finishes differed

MI completed the March chase at 19.1 overs, with five legal deliveries unused. KKR finished the May chase at 18.5, with seven left. The apparently similar timing should not conceal the difference in scoring: one pursuit began at 221 and the other at 148. Nor are the winning margins interchangeable. MI lost four wickets and won by six; KKR lost six and won by four.

The match awards also highlighted different contributions. Shardul Thakur was Player of the Match in March after taking three wickets in a 220-run innings. Manish Pandey took the May award after his 45 helped rebuild KKR’s chase. Neither award needs to be inferred from the highest individual score: the official reports identify both recipients. ## What the two games can, and cannot, tell you

The starting targets differed by 73 runs. Both chasing teams won, but two games do not establish a reliable “always chase” rule, a permanent venue par score or a forecast for the next meeting.

For a useful comparison, preserve the target, balls remaining and wickets at the same checkpoint. Do not compare 73 needed from 49 with 30 needed from 30 as if the raw runs alone described the pressure. Nor should these two results be presented as the teams' all-time head-to-head.
