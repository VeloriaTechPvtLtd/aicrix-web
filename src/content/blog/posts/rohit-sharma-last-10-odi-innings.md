## How the figures are calculated

The ten scores total 502. Dividing by ten dismissals gives the 50.20 average; 502 × 100 ÷ 477 gives the 105.24 strike rate, rounded to two decimals. Averaging the individual innings strike rates would produce a different and inappropriate combined rate.

There were two centuries and two additional scores above fifty. Those four innings contributed 410 runs, or 81.67% of the sample; the other six contributed 92. The median score was 29, halfway between 26 and 32. That difference from the average shows how strongly the larger innings influence this short window. It does not establish a probability for his next score.

The 138 from 110 balls at Lord’s came in a 27-run defeat. His 101 from 75 against West Indies came in an eight-wicket win while India chased 406. A large individual contribution therefore did not mean the same team result in both games.
