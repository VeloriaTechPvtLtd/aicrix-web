Ellyse Perry’s WPL record through the completed 2026 season remains **972 runs from 25 innings** across the 2023–2025 editions. She withdrew from the 2026 tournament; that absence is not a zero-run playing season. Wisden’s dated report reproduces the league statement confirming her withdrawal. [Withdrawal report](https://www.wisden.com/series/wpl-2026/cricket-news/star-australia-all-rounders-ellyse-perry-sutherland-withdraw-from-wpl-2026-replacements-named)

## Batting by played season

| Season | Matches / innings | Runs | Balls | Not outs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2023 | 8 / 8 | 253 | 205 | 2 |
| 2024 | 9 / 9 | 347 | 276 | 4 |
| 2025 | 8 / 8 | 372 | 250 | 4 |
| Total | 25 / 25 | 972 | 731 | 10 |

Source: [Annual WPL table](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/wpl/player-stats/ellyse-perry/59956), corroborated by the [career ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/9115/ellyse-perry).

Ten not outs leave 15 dismissals, so her career batting average is 972 ÷ 15 = **64.80**. Her strike rate is 972 ÷ 731 × 100 = **132.97**. Runs per innings, a different measure, is 38.88.

The 2025 season brought more runs than 2024 despite one fewer innings. It also used fewer balls: 372 from 250, compared with 347 from 276. The respective strike rates are 148.80 and 125.72, calculated from those season inputs.

## Bowling opportunity was different

Her career bowling record contains 20 innings, 314 legal deliveries and 14 wickets. In 2025 she bowled in five of her eight appearances, delivering 78 legal balls. An all-rounder label therefore does not establish that she bowled in every match. [Season bowling ledger](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/wpl/player-stats/ellyse-perry/59956)

On 12 March 2024 against Mumbai, Perry took 6/15 in four overs and then scored 40 not out as RCB won by seven wickets. [Cricket Australia match report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3931775/perry-takes-wpl-best-ever-figures-in-dominant-display-royal-challengers-bangalore-defeat-mumbai-indians-report-scores)

That single spell accounts for six of her 14 career WPL wickets, approximately 43%. It is a major part of the record, not a repeatable expectation per appearance.

RCB subsequently included her in the official 28 September 2026 retention list for WPL 2027. Retention establishes that dated team decision; it does not reverse her 2026 absence or confirm availability for every future fixture. [League announcement](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/tata-wpl-2027-player-retention-list-announced)
