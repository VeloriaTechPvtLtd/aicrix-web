Meg Lanning finished WPL 2026 with 1,200 career runs from 35 innings. Her first three seasons were with Delhi Capitals; her 2026 season was with UP Warriorz. A profile that labels all those runs as a Delhi record therefore misses a material change in her career.

The split is 952 runs in 27 appearances for Delhi and 248 in eight for UP. Across the two teams, she has 11 fifties, no hundred and a highest score of 92 through the completed 2026 competition.

## What changed after the move?

Lanning’s Delhi aggregate works out to 35.26 runs per innings, compared with 31.00 in her first UP season. Those are runs-per-innings figures, not the same as batting average: her three Delhi not-outs reduce the number of dismissals used in the latter.

Her season total declined from 276 in 2025 to 248 in 2026, but she played one fewer innings and faced 34 fewer balls. Meanwhile, her scoring rate increased from 118.97 to 125.25. Calling the lower total simply slower batting would reverse what the delivery-based numbers show.

The boundary mix changed too. In 2025 she struck 45 fours and one six; in 2026 she hit 37 fours and five sixes. Those boundaries supplied 186 of 276 runs in 2025 and 178 of 248 in 2026, or approximately 67.4% and 71.8%. Fewer fours did not mean a smaller proportion of runs from boundaries because the six count rose.

These are descriptive changes, not proof that the team move caused a new approach. Venues, opposition and the states of individual matches would need separate analysis before making that argument.

## The 70 against Mumbai

The [official WPL Match 10 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-10) confirms Lanning’s 70 from 45 balls and a 119-run partnership with Phoebe Litchfield in UP’s 22-run win over Mumbai Indians. Lanning was named Player of the Match.

That innings was her season high and one of two fifties in 2026. It illustrates the difference between a particular match and a season rate: her strike rate in that innings was 155.56, well above the 125.25 she recorded across the whole campaign. A career profile should preserve both without presenting the highlight as her usual output.

## Which team does this record cover?

That is a dated team statement, not an assumption about her 2027 contract or captaincy.
