Richa Ghosh’s WPL record through 2026 contains 814 runs from 35 matches, with a highest score of 90. The headline total is only part of her contribution: she is a wicketkeeper-batter, and her fielding record needs to be kept separate from her scoring rate.

Her batting ledger also contains a small but important distinction. She has 33 innings and nine not-outs. An unbeaten appearance without facing a delivery still counts as a batting innings; it is not automatically a did-not-bat entry. That matters when calculating averages or comparing opportunities.

## Why 0 not out is not DNB

The [official RCB scorecard for 29 January 2026](https://www.royalchallengers.com/t20-live-cricket-score/2356/270/royal-challengers-bangalore-%28women%29-vs-up-warriorz-%28women%29) lists Ghosh as 0 not out from zero balls against UP Warriorz. Georgia Voll fell at 12.6 overs; Smriti Mandhana hit the winning boundary from the next delivery. Ghosh was at the other end without having faced a ball.

That entry adds one innings and one not-out, but neither runs nor balls faced. It changes neither the dismissal count nor the aggregate strike-rate denominator. Labeling it a duck would be wrong because she was not dismissed; excluding it from innings would also misrepresent the scorecard.

## Catches, stumpings and run-outs are not one statistic

The [TOI fielding ledger](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/wpl/player-stats/richa-ghosh/74530) lists 17 catches, 19 stumpings and four run-out involvements across her WPL career. Its 2026 row lists three catches, three stumpings and two run-outs.

These categories should not be collapsed into a claim of 40 wicketkeeping dismissals. A run-out may involve more than one fielder, and the catches column does not by itself distinguish every catch taken as keeper from one taken elsewhere. A complete keeper-only dismissal total requires an event-level audit.

The January 29 scorecard provides a concrete verified keeping contribution: Chloe Tryon was stumped by Ghosh off Grace Harris. That is distinct from a catch, and it does not establish anything about fantasy-platform credits.

## What the profile can tell you

Ghosh’s record combines rapid scoring with a separate set of keeping contributions. Compare her batting with balls faced and dismissals, not just appearances. Compare fielding categories by their actual definitions. Neither total establishes a future batting position, a guaranteed number of deliveries or a projected fantasy score.
