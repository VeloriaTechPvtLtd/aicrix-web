Australia beat South Africa by 19 runs at Newlands, Cape Town, on 26 February 2023. Australia made 156/6 and South Africa finished on 137/6. Beth Mooney's unbeaten 74 earned Player of the Match. Both sides lost six wickets, but Australia converted the same twenty-over allowance into more runs. [ICC final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/australia-surge-to-sixth-t20-world-cup-title-with-clinical-performance)

## Mooney recovered from a scoreless over

Mooney faced 53 balls. After Alyssa Healy's dismissal, Shabnim Ismail bowled a maiden to her. Mooney nevertheless stayed through the innings and reached an unbeaten 74. [ICC Mooney account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/mooney-australia-never-get-tired-of-winning)

Her overall strike rate, calculated as 74 × 100 ÷ 53, was 139.62. That average includes the maiden: it does not mean she scored at a uniform rate throughout. The useful point is the recovery within the innings, not a rule that an established batter will always make up for a slow passage.

## The partnership revived, but did not complete, the chase

South Africa were 52/2 after ten overs. Laura Wolvaardt and Chloe Tryon later added 55; Wolvaardt made 61 from 48 balls and Tryon 25. Megan Schutt dismissed Wolvaardt lbw, and the final over began with 26 still required. [ICC match account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/australia-surge-to-sixth-t20-world-cup-title-with-clinical-performance)

At halfway the target of 157 left 105 needed from 60 balls, a required rate of 10.5 per over. The partnership improved the chase, but that earlier shortfall still had to be recovered. Faster scoring alone was not enough: it needed to catch up with the remaining requirement.

## Result margin and title count

The formal margin is 156 − 137 = 19 runs. South Africa finished twenty below the winning target of 157; that separate calculation must not replace the official result line.

Australia's win brought their sixth Women's T20 World Cup title and third consecutively at that point. [ICC post-final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/mooney-australia-never-get-tired-of-winning) The final's specific lesson is visible in the innings sequence: Mooney completed her recovery, while South Africa's later acceleration did not erase the burden left by the first half of the chase.
