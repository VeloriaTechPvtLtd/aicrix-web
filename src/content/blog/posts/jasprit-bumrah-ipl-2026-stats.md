Jasprit Bumrah finished IPL 2026 with **four wickets in 13 matches**, a bowling average of 102.50 and best figures of 1/15. Those are completed IPL-season figures, not his international or career totals. [Official Mumbai Indians statistics](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/player-stats)

## Comparing two completed seasons

| Wickets | 18 | 4 |

Inputs: [Annual bowling ledger](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/players/jasprit-bumrah-ipl-p6684/), with legal-ball totals corroborated by [the season comparison](https://iplrecords.com/jasprit-bumrah-ipl-2026-wickets).

The workloads differ by just ten legal balls. The wicket totals differ by 14, while runs conceded rose by 94. This is not simply a comparison of a long season with a very short one, although opponents, conditions and bowling assignments are not controlled here.

The 2025 notation 47.2 means 47 overs and two balls: 284 legal deliveries. For 2026, 410 divided by 49 overs gives 8.3673, rounded here to **8.37**. Mumbai’s official table displays 8.36. The raw-input calculation explains the displayed difference rather than silently mixing conventions.

## Wicketless spells can have different costs

The 2026 match ledger records 0/20 against RCB and 0/54 against SRH. Both contain zero wickets, but the second conceded 34 more runs. A wicket count alone cannot distinguish their run-control contribution. [Season match figures](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/players/jasprit-bumrah-ipl-p6684/)

His first wicket of the season came from the first ball of Gujarat’s innings on 20 April, according to the league’s report. Mumbai won that match by 99 runs. [Official match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-30-gt-vs-mi-match-report)

The season record therefore needs both economy and wicket frequency. Neither reputation nor one standout spell should replace the completed totals, and this historical comparison does not diagnose a cause or predict the next season.
