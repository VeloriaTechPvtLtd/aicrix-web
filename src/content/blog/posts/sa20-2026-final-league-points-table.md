**Sunrisers Eastern Cape finished first in the SA20 2025–26 league stage with 28 points.** Pretoria Capitals and Paarl Royals both had 24, while Joburg Super Kings secured the fourth playoff position on 22. Durban’s Super Giants and MI Cape Town finished outside the top four. [Completed league table](https://statz.ai/cricket/competitions/sa20/table)

This is the historical table for the season that ended in January 2026, not the forthcoming 2027 competition. The table stops at the league phase. A later playoff win does not add another league win or increase this points total.

## Why five wins could mean 28 or 24 points

An ordinary win earned four points; a bonus-point win earned five. A no result earned two. An unresolved tie, where a Super Over could not produce a result, earned three. A loss earned zero. The applicable provisions are in clauses 16.10.1–16.10.3 of the [official 2025–26 conditions](https://media-cdn.cortextech.io/eb7b0e4b-c43e-4c82-9517-e3c1c21f2ed3.pdf).

Sunrisers’ total reconciles as **5 × 4 + 2 × 2 + 4 = 28**. Pretoria’s is **5 × 4 + 1 × 2 + 2 = 24**. Both won five league games, but Sunrisers had one more no result and two more bonus points.

Those two differences account for the entire four-point gap. It would be inaccurate to explain the lead simply by saying Sunrisers won more matches, or to treat 28 divided by four as seven wins.

Joburg’s fourth-place total similarly reconciles as **4 × 4 + 2 × 2 + 2 = 22**. Durban’s total is **3 × 4 + 3 × 2 + 1 = 19**. No-results points formed part of the final standings, but the table alone does not tell us what would have happened had every interrupted match been completed.

## The bonus test is not a season NRR test

The bonus uses a match run-rate ratio of 1.25, not a season NRR threshold. The ratio was described in [SuperSport’s original announcement](https://supersport.com/cricket/news/6303daef-c8e5-4f34-917a-de7178f4a041/betway-sa20-playing-conditions-announced) and remains in clause 16.10.3 of the checked 2025–26 conditions. NRR is a difference between scoring and conceding rates, not a count of bonus points.

For a simple fictional example with two completed 20-over innings, 200 versus 160 produces rates of 10 and 8 runs per over. Their ratio is **10 ÷ 8 = 1.25**. The rate difference is **2**, not 1.25. Confusing the ratio with the difference changes the calculation.

That illustration deliberately avoids rain adjustments and early all-outs. It explains the distinction without pretending that every real match can be adjudicated from two totals alone.

## Why Pretoria finished above Paarl

Pretoria and Paarl had equal points and equal wins. The next relevant tie-break was NRR, so **+0.218 ranked ahead of −0.922**. The official order begins with wins after equal points, then NRR; it does not jump directly from points to the teams’ names or reputations. [Tie-break provisions](https://media-cdn.cortextech.io/eb7b0e4b-c43e-4c82-9517-e3c1c21f2ed3.pdf)

Paarl’s negative NRR did not prevent third place. Points came first. Joburg’s positive NRR was not enough to overtake a team with two more points. These are two different comparisons, which is why sorting the NRR column alone does not reproduce the standings.

## Keep the league table separate from the champion

League position determined the playoff route; it was not the final trophy result. In the Cricbuzz ledger, postseason matches are listed beneath teams while the league row stays at ten games. Do not count those extra results into the table above.
