Chris Gayle reached his IPL century in 30 balls against Pune Warriors in 2013. Later milestones include Vaibhav Sooryavanshi’s 35-ball hundred against Gujarat Titans in 2025 and his 36-ball hundred against Sunrisers Hyderabad in April 2026. This is a comparison of documented innings through 25 April 2026, not an exhaustive live ranking. [IPL’s Gayle statistics](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/quick-stats-match-31-rcb-vs-pwi), [IPL’s 2025 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-47-rr-vs-gt-match-report), [Royals’ 2026 report](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/rajasthan-royals-vs-sunrisers-hyderabad-ipl-2026-rr-srh-match-report)

## Compare the same measurement

| Batter | Opponent and year | Balls to hundred |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chris Gayle | Pune Warriors, 2013 | 30 |
| Vaibhav Sooryavanshi | Gujarat Titans, 2025 | 35 |
| Vaibhav Sooryavanshi | Sunrisers Hyderabad, 2026 | 36 |
| Yusuf Pathan | Mumbai Indians, 2010 | 37 |
| Heinrich Klaasen | Kolkata Knight Riders, 2025 | 37 |

The IPL’s 2013 statistics identify Pathan’s earlier 37-ball mark; its 2025 report records Klaasen matching that speed. [Pathan comparison](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/quick-stats-match-31-rcb-vs-pwi), [Klaasen report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-68-srh-vs-kkr-match-report)

These are **five innings by four players**. Combining Sooryavanshi’s two performances into one entry would lose a distinct innings; counting them as two different batters would be equally wrong.

## Milestone balls and innings balls differ

Klaasen finished on 105 not out from 39 balls despite reaching his century in 37. Sooryavanshi’s April 2026 innings ended at 103 from 37 after he reached a hundred on ball 36. [Klaasen’s figures](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-68-srh-vs-kkr-match-report), [Sooryavanshi’s figures](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/rajasthan-royals-vs-sunrisers-hyderabad-ipl-2026-rr-srh-match-report)

A final scorecard’s balls-faced column is therefore insufficient on its own to rank the fastest hundreds. You need the ball on which the batter first reached the milestone. Keep the two columns separate whenever presenting this record.
