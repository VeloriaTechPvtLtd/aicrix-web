That matched his 2023 wicket total, but the two seasons required different workloads and came at very different run costs. [Career ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/10808/mohammed-siraj).

## Comparing his two Gujarat seasons

Siraj took three more wickets in 2026 than in 2025 while bowling five additional overs. The improvement was not limited to extra opportunity. Balls per wicket fell from 21.38 to 19.58, and economy improved from 9.25 to 9.06.

His conceded runs increased from 527 to 562, but judging that rise alone would be misleading: 562 runs came from 372 balls rather than 342. The rate per over fell slightly even though the total rose. Bowling average also improved, from 32.94 to 29.58, reflecting the additional wickets.

Across both Gujarat seasons combined, he delivered 714 legal balls and took 35 wickets for 1,089 runs. That means 119 overs, an economy of 9.15 and an average of 31.11. Those combined figures are calculated from the underlying totals rather than by giving each season equal weight.

## Why 19 wickets does not describe the whole season

Siraj’s 2023 and 2026 wicket totals are identical, but in 2023 he needed 300 balls; in 2026 he needed 372. The difference is 12 overs. His strike rate was 15.79 balls per wicket in 2023 against 19.58 in 2026.

Run costs differed too: 375 conceded in 2023 versus 562 in 2026. The respective economies were 7.50 and 9.06. This is a comparison of recorded outcomes, not proof that one change in technique explains everything. Team, venues, opponents and match situations also differ, and those effects require more detailed analysis.

## His best IPL spell

Siraj’s best remains 4 for 17 against Sunrisers Hyderabad on 6 April 2025. The official report records two wickets in his initial three-over spell and two more in his final over, demonstrating contributions at different stages of that particular innings. Gujarat won by seven wickets. [Official IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-match-20-srh-vs-gt-match-report).

The four wickets account for a quarter of his 16-wicket 2025 season. That concentration is worth knowing when a highlight is used to summarize the campaign. The spell is an important part of the record, but it is not a typical match return.

## What to carry into a future assessment

His full career economy is 8.78, bowling average 30.55 and strike rate 20.88. The 2,672-ball workload is 445 overs and two balls; the final “two” is deliveries, not a decimal fraction of an over. [Career totals](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/10808/mohammed-siraj).

For an upcoming match, establish his availability and expected allocation before applying those figures. The season record does not promise a four-over spell or a permanent new-ball/death split. The most defensible recent assessment is a modest rate improvement from 2025 to 2026, alongside a larger workload, rather than treating 19 wickets as sufficient evidence on its own.
