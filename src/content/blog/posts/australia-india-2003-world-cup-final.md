Australia's 2003 men's World Cup final victory over India was built around Ricky Ponting's unbeaten 140 and Damien Martyn's unbeaten 88. Their unbroken third-wicket stand of 234 carried Australia to 359 for two. India were dismissed for 234, leaving Australia with their third title. [ICC's 2003 tournament overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/mens-cricket-world-cup-2003-overview)

## The openers supplied the first advantage

India chose to field. Adam Gilchrist and Matthew Hayden put on 105 in 14 overs; Gilchrist made 57 and Hayden 37. Ponting eventually finished with four fours and eight sixes in his 121-ball innings, while Martyn's 88 took 84 deliveries. [ICC's Ponting career review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/ricky-ponting-greatest-hits)

## An innings with a clear change of tempo

Cricket Australia's retrospective records Ponting reaching 50 from 74 balls, then adding his next 90 from 47. The partnership included 55 runs from Australia's final four overs. Those checkpoints explain much more than describing the whole innings as uninterrupted attacking batting. [Cricket Australia's match retrospective](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3260942)

Calculated from those figures, the first phase scored at about 67.6 runs per 100 balls and the second at about 191.5. These are phase-specific strike rates, not separate innings or a forecast of what Ponting would do against another attack.

The comparison also illustrates a limitation of averages. His overall strike rate blends two very different passages. A single number is useful for a summary, but cannot fully describe how the innings developed or when the scoring pressure on the fielding side increased.

## Martyn's role should remain visible

In a later Cricket Australia feature, Ponting specifically praised Martyn's contribution and described his importance to the team. The feature presents Martyn's unbeaten innings as an important part of the victory, not a footnote to the captain's score. [Cricket Australia's Ponting retrospective](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3297116/australia-beat-india-2003-odi-world-cup-final-johannesburg-ricky-ponting-damien-martyn-johannesburg-wanderers-sachin-tendulkar)

## India's reply and the limits of one innings

Sachin Tendulkar fell to Glenn McGrath for four. Virender Sehwag made 82 from 81 balls, but India could not approach Australia's total. Tendulkar nevertheless finished as the tournament's leading scorer with 673 runs and was Player of the Series. His final innings and his tournament achievement are different records. [ICC's 2003 overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/mens-cricket-world-cup-2003-overview)

The winning margin was 125 runs. That is the difference between 359 and 234; India's original target was 360. Australia's eventual victory is not evidence that a chase of that size was literally impossible before it started.
