## Why one appearance has no bowling innings

Against Kolkata on 6 April, Jansen was in Punjab’s listed XI, but rain ended the match with Kolkata at 25/2 after 3.4 overs. Only Arshdeep Singh and Xavier Bartlett bowled. Jansen’s appearance therefore does not add a bowling spell. [Full scorecard and team list](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/149732/kkr-vs-pbks-12th-match-ipl-2026)

This is not the same as a wicketless spell: he delivered no ball in that game. The distinction explains the 13 appearances versus 12 bowling innings without treating a missing bowling entry as an error.

## Two identifiable contributions

His season-best 2/33 came against Lucknow on 23 May and included Nicholas Pooran’s wicket. Punjab won by seven wickets. [Official report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-68-lsg-vs-pbks-match-report)

Earlier, his 20 against Gujarat on 3 May helped Punjab reach 163/9 after they had been 47/5. Gujarat won by four wickets. [Official report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-46-gt-vs-pbks-match-report)

Both performances belong within the completed season totals, but neither should become a promised role or points return for a later fixture. The useful baseline is the record with its actual batting and bowling opportunities attached.
