India beat Afghanistan by an innings and 300 runs in the one-off Test at New Chandigarh, completing the match on 8 June 2026. India’s 564/8 declared was followed by Afghanistan’s 152 and 112 after the follow-on. The match began on 6 June; the advertised 6–10 June window was the scheduled allocation, not five days of actual play. [BCCI match centre](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/0e966904-9653-4b3f-937c-90f01e42d0e2/india-vs-afghanistan/scorecard) · [ICC’s 8 June report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/dream-debut-guides-india-to-record-test-win-over-afghanistan).

Shubman Gill and KL Rahul supplied India’s centuries. Debutant Manav Suthar then led the first-innings bowling, before Washington Sundar and Kuldeep Yadav took the larger share of wickets in Afghanistan’s second innings. Those are separate contributions rather than one uninterrupted individual spell.

## The three innings and the 300 run margin

| Innings | Total | Overs | Position at the close |

| India first | 564/8 declared | 127 | Afghanistan still to bat |
| Afghanistan first | 152 | 58.4 | India led by 412 |
| Afghanistan second, following on | 112 | 35.5 | India won by an innings and 300 |

The venue was Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium in New Chandigarh, also identified with Mullanpur. It is not the established IS Bindra ground in Mohali. [BCCI venue record](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/0e966904-9653-4b3f-937c-90f01e42d0e2/india-vs-afghanistan/scorecard).

The first-innings lead was 564 minus 152, or 412. Afghanistan’s next 112 reduced that deficit to 300 but did not eliminate it. India therefore never needed a second batting innings. Adding Afghanistan’s two innings gives 264, still 300 below India’s single total. This is an innings victory, not a successful chase of a 300-run target.

## India built beyond the two centuries

Gill made 126 and Rahul 100. Sai Sudharsan and Rishabh Pant each contributed 81, while Sundar finished unbeaten on 52. The full batting ledger places these innings alongside 22 extras in the 564 total. [Complete scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/148382/afghanistan-tour-of-india-2026-one-off-test). ICC’s later awards release independently identifies Gill’s 126 in this Test, separately from his ODI runs on the same tour. [ICC July release](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/icc-player-of-the-month-nominees-for-june-revealed-x8051).

The centurions’ combined 226 were 40.07% of the team total. Sudharsan, Pant and Sundar added another 214, or 37.94%. Together those five supplied 440 runs, leaving 124 from other batters and extras. That distribution matters: the large total was not simply two centuries with negligible support.

India declared with two wickets still available. Its 127 overs are 762 legal balls; 564 across that allocation gives 4.44 runs per over. The declaration ended the opportunity to add further runs, so the total should not be described as an all-out batting ceiling.

## Suthar’s six wickets established the lead

Suthar took 6/33 in Afghanistan’s first innings. Rahmat Shah resisted with 60, but the innings ended on 152 and India enforced the follow-on. The ICC report identifies this as Suthar’s Test debut. [ICC match account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/dream-debut-guides-india-to-record-test-win-over-afghanistan).

His 22 overs included ten maidens: 132 legal balls and an economy of 1.50 runs per over. Prasidh Krishna took three wickets and Sundar the other one. That separates the leading wicket-taker from the rest of the attack without treating a wicketless spell as no bowling opportunity. [First-innings bowling](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/148382/afghanistan-tour-of-india-2026-one-off-test).

## Nine dismissals ended Afghanistan’s second innings

Afghanistan’s second attempt reached 42 before the first wicket, but the innings ended at 112. Sundar took four wickets and Kuldeep three; Suthar and Mohammed Siraj took one each. Sharafuddin Ashraf was recorded absent hurt, while Ziaur Rahman Sharifi remained unbeaten. There were nine actual dismissals, not ten wickets credited to bowlers. [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/148382/afghanistan-tour-of-india-2026-one-off-test) · [Contemporary account of the finish](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/cricket/ind-vs-afg-live-score-one-off-test-day-3-afghanistan-tour-of-india-2026-updates-highlights-new-chandigarh).

Suthar’s match return was 7/62, combining 6/33 with 1/29. Sundar’s five wickets and Kuldeep’s three completed a different second-innings pattern, rather than a repeat of the debutant’s six-wicket haul. Across both innings, India’s bowlers claimed 19 wickets. The absent-hurt entry explains why a completed innings and ten bowler wickets are not interchangeable.

The margin describes this completed match. It does not establish a future Mullanpur scoring level, a current team hierarchy or an expected result when these sides next meet.
