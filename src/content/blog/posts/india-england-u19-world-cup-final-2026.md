**India beat England by 100 runs in the 2026 ICC Under-19 Men’s Cricket World Cup final at Harare Sports Club.** India made 411/9 in 50 overs, then dismissed England for 311 in 40.2 overs. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi’s 175 from 80 balls led India to a record sixth title. ## How Sooryavanshi made 175

Sooryavanshi reached fifty in 32 balls and his hundred in 55. He finished with **15 fours and 15 sixes**, giving him a strike rate of **218.75**. His 175 exceeded Unmukt Chand’s 111 not out in the 2012 final to become the highest individual score in a men’s U19 World Cup final at the time. [ICC innings and record report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/u19-cricket-worldcup-2026/news/indian-batter-sets-new-u19-world-cup-final-record-with-blistering-century).

The boundary contribution is straightforward: 15 fours produced 60 runs and 15 sixes produced 90, a combined **150 of his 175 runs**, or **85.7%**. The other 25 runs came outside those boundaries. The ECB’s match report independently confirms the 150 boundary runs. [ECB final report](https://www.ecb.co.uk/news/4445788/falconer-hits-ton-as-young-lions-beaten-in-u19s-world-cup-final).

His hundred’s speed also needs its proper record category. Will Malajczuk had reached a hundred in **51 balls against Japan** earlier in the same tournament. Sooryavanshi’s final was remarkable for its score and occasion, but his 55-ball hundred was not that edition’s fastest. [ICC tournament review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/records-tumbled-as-future-stars-shone-bright-at-icc-u19-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2026).

## How India reached 411

After the early loss of Aaron George, Sooryavanshi and Mhatre added **142 for the second wicket**. Mhatre scored 53 from 51 balls. Sooryavanshi’s dismissal left India at 251/3 in the 26th over, giving the remaining batters a substantial platform.

Abhigyan Kundu contributed 40 from 31 balls, and Kanishk Chouhan finished unbeaten on 37 from 20. James Minto took 3/63 for England. India added another 160 runs after Sooryavanshi’s wicket to reach 411/9. [ICC final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/sooryavanshi-fires-india-to-icc-u19-men-s-cricket-world-cup-glory), [ICC innings account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/u19-cricket-worldcup-2026/news/dominant-india-beat-england-to-lift-the-u19-world-cup).

## England lost four wickets for three runs

Ben Dawkins made 66, Ben Mayes 45 and Rew 31. England reached 174/3 before the middle order lost four wickets for only three additional runs. The sequence shows why the chase changed so sharply.

The final row is the end of the 22nd over. England still needed **235 from 168 balls**, a required rate of **8.39 per over**, with only three wickets left. That rate was not far above the starting requirement of 8.24, but the available wickets had changed dramatically. Required rate alone cannot describe the difficulty of a chase. [Scorecard and fall of wickets](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/138680/engu19-vs-indu19-final-icc-under-19-world-cup-2026).

## Falconer kept the chase alive

Caleb Falconer and Minto added **92 for the eighth wicket** before R.S. Ambrish dismissed Minto for 28. Falconer continued to attack, reaching his hundred in 63 balls and finishing with **115 from 67**, including nine fours and seven sixes.

He was last out, caught by Khilan Patel off Chouhan, ending England’s innings at 311. Ambrish finished with 3/56, the most wickets for India in the final. [ICC chase account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/u19-cricket-worldcup-2026/news/dominant-india-beat-england-to-lift-the-u19-world-cup), [ECB report](https://www.ecb.co.uk/news/4445788/falconer-hits-ton-as-young-lions-beaten-in-u19s-world-cup-final).

England were all out with **58 legal balls unused**. The 40.2 on the scorecard means 40 complete overs and two balls, not 40.2 decimal overs. The innings ended because England lost their tenth wicket, rather than because the 50-over allocation expired.
