England won the men's T20 World Cup final at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on 13 November 2022, beating Pakistan by five wickets. Pakistan finished on 137/8 from 20 overs; England reached 138/5 in 19. England had chosen to field, and Sam Curran received the Player of the Match award. Those are the essentials of the result, but the final score alone understates the difficulty of the chase. [BCCI match details](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/4a111d53-fd0f-4555-a727-e01bffb1573f/england-vs-pakistan/match-details)

The useful question is not simply whether 138 was a modest target. It is how England kept enough batting resources available to finish a chase in which Pakistan continued to create pressure.

## Start with the bowling, not the winning shot

Curran took 3/12 and Adil Rashid 2/22. Rashid removed Babar Azam for 32, while Shan Masood made 38. Their combined returns show why the target remained manageable: England were taking wickets without repeatedly surrendering expensive overs. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/stokes-the-hero-as-england-claim-second-t20-world-cup-title-in-style)

Together, Curran and Rashid claimed five wickets for 34 runs. England were not relying on one economical bowler: the pair also removed half the available Pakistan wickets.

## Stokes supplied continuity rather than a boundary exhibition

Ben Stokes finished unbeaten on 52 from 49 balls. Haris Rauf took 2/23, and Shaheen Afridi removed Alex Hales early. England still needed 41 from their last five overs. It was a live contest, not an automatic procession to the trophy. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/stokes-the-hero-as-england-claim-second-t20-world-cup-title-in-style)

Stokes's innings works out to a strike rate of approximately 106.12. Read without context, that number might seem ordinary for T20 cricket. Read alongside the target and the need to retain a batter through the chase, it describes a different kind of contribution.

## The injury changed the available bowling options

Afridi had hurt himself completing a catch. When he tried to return, he could deliver only one ball of his third over before leaving the field. Iftikhar Ahmed completed the over, and Stokes struck its final two deliveries for four and six. Moeen Ali also contributed late boundaries. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/stokes-the-hero-as-england-claim-second-t20-world-cup-title-in-style)

The change matters because a captain plans a defence around particular bowlers, not interchangeable over slots. Losing a preferred option restricts those choices. Yet it would be too strong to say the injury proves Pakistan otherwise would have won. That alternative match was never played.

## Read the margin and the remaining time separately

Five wickets was the official victory margin. Six legal deliveries remained because England finished after 19 complete overs of a 20-over chase. These describe different dimensions of the finish: unused wickets and unused time.

When revisiting this final, keep three questions separate: how England restricted the first innings, how Pakistan made the chase difficult and how England converted the closing opportunity. That produces a more faithful explanation than crediting a single shot, a single injury or one headline innings with the entire result.
