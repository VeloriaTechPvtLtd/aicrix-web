## The season was not nine similar scores

Her 96, 54 not out and 87 supplied 237 runs, or 62.86% of the total. The other six innings supplied 140. The ordered scores were 5, 6, 18, 26, 38, 47, 54, 87 and 96, so the middle score was 38.

These are complementary descriptions rather than competing verdicts. The aggregate rewards the whole season’s production. The median locates the centre of the nine-score sample. The three larger innings show how heavily the total depended on substantial contributions. None of those figures makes the next innings predictable.

Her 96 against Delhi on 17 January was the season high, but it was not her fastest substantial innings. She faced 61 balls for that score. In the final she made 87 from 41, using twenty fewer balls for nine fewer runs. Comparing the score alone would miss the difference in pace. ## Two unbeaten chases, two different supporting roles

On 12 January, Mandhana made 47 not out from 32 balls while Grace Harris made 85 from 40. The official account describes their opening partnership as central to RCB’s nine-wicket win. Mandhana did not need a fifty for that innings to help complete the chase. [WPL Match 5 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-5).

In the later UP Warriorz meeting, she finished on 54 not out from 27. RCB won by eight wickets with 41 balls remaining against a target of 144. The two unbeaten innings therefore belong in the same season ledger without being described as identical performances. The latter was exactly two runs per ball; the former was 146.88 runs per hundred balls when rounded. [29 January match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-18).

That distinction also prevents a common reading error: not-out status tells us that the innings ended without her dismissal, not how quickly the runs were scored or how many further balls she would have faced.

## What the final added

Before the final, the eight league innings totalled 290 runs from 205 balls, a strike rate of 141.46. Adding 87 from 41 raised the completed-season rate to 153.25. Her final innings alone had a calculated strike rate of 212.20 when rounded to two decimals.

The final contributed 23.08% of her season runs. That is a meaningful description of the completed campaign, not evidence that the preceding eight innings should be discarded. A preview written before the final and a retrospective written afterwards have different information available; the later performance must not leak into an earlier assessment.
