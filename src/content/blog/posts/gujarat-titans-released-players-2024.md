Gujarat Titans released **eight players** ahead of IPL 2024. Hardik Pandya’s departure was a separate trade to Mumbai Indians, not a ninth name on that release list.

## The eight released players

The league’s 26 November 2023 retention announcement lists:

- Alzarri Joseph
- Dasun Shanaka
- KS Bharat
- Odean Smith
- Pradeep Sangwan
- Shivam Mavi
- Urvil Patel
- Yash Dayal

This is the pre-auction release list for the **2024 season**, announced in calendar year 2023. It does not describe current contracts. [IPL retention announcement](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-2024-player-retentions-list)

## Why Hardik appears in a retained squad first

The November 26 announcement included Hardik among 18 retained GT players. The following day, IPL confirmed his move to MI and supplied an updated GT retained squad of 17.

The same announcement described Cameron Green’s move from MI to Royal Challengers Bangalore as a separate trade. It was not a swap sending Green to GT. [November 27 trade update](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/hardik-pandya-and-cameron-green-traded-to-mi-and-rcb)

Two dated snapshots can therefore both be accurate. The earlier retained list does not mean Hardik remained with GT for the season; the later update does not turn his departure into a release.

## The purse and squad-place changes

| Snapshot | Players | Available purse | Open places |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| November 26 | 18 | ₹23.15 crore | 7 |
| November 27 | 17 | ₹38.15 crore | 8 |

The change was one fewer player and ₹15 crore more available purse. Both snapshots showed six overseas players and two overseas vacancies. Those overseas places were included within the total vacancies, not added on top.

## Shami’s replacement came later

On 20 March 2024, IPL announced Sandeep Warrier as Mohammed Shami’s replacement while Shami recovered from heel surgery. Warrier joined at ₹50 lakh. This was a later injury replacement, not evidence that Shami had appeared in the November release list. [Official replacement announcement](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/gt-name-sandeep-warrier-as-replacement-for-mohd-shami-mi-add-kwena-maphaka-to-squad-for-the-injured-dilshan-madushanka)

For a historical roster, preserve the order: releases, trades, auction additions, then replacements. Combining them into a single undated list loses the reason each player’s status changed.
