South Africa led the World Test Championship standings after the Melbourne Test ended on 30 December 2024. Australia were second and India third. This snapshot belongs to the **2023–25 cycle**: it is neither the final cycle table nor today’s standings. [ICC’s 30 December assessment](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/world-test-championship-state-of-play-ahead-of-2025-final)

## What remained undecided

South Africa had secured a final place. Australia still had one home Test against India and two away against Sri Lanka; the ICC said one win from those three would secure qualification. India’s best possible finish was 55.26%, while Sri Lanka’s was 53.85%. [Contemporary qualification assessment](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/world-test-championship-state-of-play-ahead-of-2025-final)

Australia’s displayed lead over India was **8.68 percentage points**: 61.46 − 52.78. But position alone did not settle qualification. Remaining matches gave teams different opportunities to change their percentage, so a historical table needs its cutoff and fixture context together.

## Do not mix two versions of the table

A later final table would answer a different question. Replacing these percentages with eventual cycle figures while leaving “2024 year end” in the title would turn a valid historical lookup into an inconsistent record.

Similarly, the word “qualified” should be read at the report’s date. South Africa’s confirmed place and Australia’s conditional route were different statuses on 30 December, even though both appeared near the top of the same table.
