## How the rivalry changed by season

The eventual results were:

- **2023:** one win each.

- **2024:** RCB won both.

- **2025:** UPW won both, including the Super Over.

- **2026:** RCB won both.

The running record therefore moved from 1–1 after 2023 to 3–1, then 3–3, and finally 5–3 in RCB’s favour. Across the latest five meetings in this archive, RCB won three and UPW two.

RCB’s five eventual wins represent **62.5% of these eight matches**. That historical share is not a probability for the next fixture. The season-by-season reversals show why the overall tally alone leaves out useful context.

## Keep the 2025 Super Over separate

On 24 February 2025, RCB’s 180/6 and UPW’s 180 all out produced a regulation tie. UPW then scored **eight runs** in the Super Over and restricted RCB to **four**.

The eight Super Over runs should remain separate from UPW’s 180, just as RCB’s four remain separate from their 180. A combined display of 188 against 184 can obscure the original tie and should not be presented as the ordinary innings score. The official report identifies this as the WPL’s first Super Over. [Official match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2025-match-9), [Cricket Australia report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4223071/up-warriorz-defeat-royal-challengers-bengaluru-rcb-in-first-wpl-super-over-ellyse-perry-kim-garth-sophie-ecclestone).

## Highest totals and close finishes

UPW’s **225/5** in Lucknow on 8 March 2025 is the highest team total in these eight meetings. RCB’s **213** in reply is their highest total against UPW in this archive. The 12-run margin is 225 minus 213; the chase target was 226.

The narrowest run-margin win was RCB’s **two-run victory** on 24 February 2024. Asha Sobhana took **5/22**, helping defend 157/6. A low total relative to another game did not prevent a win, just as scoring 213 in Lucknow did not secure one. ## What happened in the two 2026 games

UPW finished on 143 in both meetings, losing five wickets in the first and eight in the second. RCB chased those targets in 12.1 and 13.1 overs, respectively.

Those notations represent **73 and 79 legal balls**, leaving **47 and 41** of the scheduled 120 balls unused. They are not decimal-over figures.

Grace Harris scored **85 from 40 balls** in the first win and **75 from 37** in the second. Combined, those two innings produced **160 from 77 balls**, a strike rate of **207.79**. This is a two-match 2026 calculation, not her career head-to-head record.
