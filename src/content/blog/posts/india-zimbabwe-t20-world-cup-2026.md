## India had contributions throughout the top six

By the time Suryakumar became the fourth wicket at **172/4 after 14.5 overs**, India still had 31 legal deliveries available. [Batting and wicket sequence](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/139426/ind-vs-zim).

Tilak finished with 44 from 16 balls; Hardik reached his half-century by hitting sixes from the final two deliveries. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/live-india-take-on-zimbabwe-in-battle-for-survival).

Calculated from the [end-of-over scores](https://sports.ndtv.com/cricket/ind-zim-over-comparison/ind-vs-zim-super-8-match-8-chennai-inzm02262026268119).

India’s 36-run powerplay advantage accounted for half the eventual margin. The middle nine overs added another nine runs to the difference, and the final five added 27. Together, 36 + 9 + 27 equals the 72-run result.

Zimbabwe had all ten wickets available after six overs, but still needed **213 from 84 balls**, or **15.21 per over**. At halfway they were 73/2, leaving **184 from 60 balls**. Preserving wickets had not kept the chase close to the required pace.

## Bennett remained unbeaten but the target kept receding

Bennett’s 97 included eight fours and six sixes. He and Sikandar Raza added 72 for the third wicket, with Raza contributing 31 from 21 balls. Bennett supplied **52.7% of Zimbabwe’s final total**, calculated as 97 ÷ 184 × 100. [Detailed scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/139426/ind-vs-zim).

Arshdeep Singh took **3/24 from four overs**, dismissing Raza, Ryan Burl and Tony Munyonga. Axar Patel, Varun Chakaravarthy and Shivam Dube took one wicket each. Hardik and Jasprit Bumrah both returned 0/21 from three overs. [Bowling figures and dismissals](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/139426/ind-vs-zim).

After 15 overs, Zimbabwe needed 126 from 30 balls. Their final five overs produced 53, leaving them 72 short of India’s score and 73 short of the winning target. The one-run distinction comes from needing to pass 256, rather than equal it.

## What the result meant at the time

The win kept India in contention for a semifinal place, but did not itself secure qualification. Zimbabwe were eliminated, South Africa had qualified, and India’s following match against West Indies would decide the other place from their group. [ICC qualification context on 26 February](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/live-india-take-on-zimbabwe-in-battle-for-survival).
