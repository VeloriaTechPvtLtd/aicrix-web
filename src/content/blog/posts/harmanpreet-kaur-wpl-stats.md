## Two unbeaten innings, different results

On 9 March 2024, she made 95 not out from 48 balls against Gujarat Giants. Mumbai chased 191 and won by seven wickets. The final over began with 13 needed; her finishing contribution turned a large individual score into a completed chase. [Official 2024 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2024-match-16)

Against Gujarat on 30 January 2026, her 82 not out from 48 balls did not deliver the same result. Mumbai finished at 156/7 chasing 168, losing by 11 runs. [Official innings listing](https://www.wplt20.com/videos/s-captain-harmanpreet-kaurs-gritty-8248-under-pressure-baujru), [match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-19)

The target and team result belong beside the score. An unbeaten innings means the batter was not dismissed; it does not mean her team won.
