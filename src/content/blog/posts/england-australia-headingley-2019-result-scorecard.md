## Headingley 2019 result

England beat Australia by one wicket at Headingley on 25 August 2019, levelling the men’s Ashes series at 1–1 with two Tests remaining. Ben Stokes finished on 135 not out; Jack Leach was unbeaten on one. Their unbroken last-wicket partnership carried England from 286/9 to 362/9. [ICC’s contemporary report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/ben-stokes-remarkable-135-leads-england-to-incredible-one-wicket-victory).

The target had been 359, not 362. England finished above it because Stokes struck a boundary with the scores level. That distinction also explains why the result is a one-wicket win, not a victory by the difference between the two teams’ aggregate runs.

## Four innings and a 112 run deficit

The match began on 22 August. England chose to bowl, dismissed Australia for 179, then were themselves bowled out for 67. Australia’s second innings of 246 left England facing a very different task from simply matching that third-innings score. [Full match ledger](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/england-vs-australia-2019-3rd-test-scorecard-44040/).

| Innings | Total | Overs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Australia first | 179 all out | 52.1 |
| England first | 67 all out | 27.5 |
| Australia second | 246 all out | 75.2 |
| England second | 362/9 | 125.4 |

Australia’s first-innings lead was 179 − 67 = 112. Adding 246 made the overall lead 358, so England needed 359 to win. Equivalently: 179 + 246 − 67 + 1 = 359. The early deficit remained part of the calculation even after England had taken all ten Australian wickets again.

Jofra Archer’s 6/45 had helped England restrict Australia, but Josh Hazlewood answered with 5/30. Joe Denly’s 12 was England’s only double-figure score in the first innings. Marnus Labuschagne then followed his first-innings 74 with 80 in Australia’s second. [Batting and bowling scorecards](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/england-vs-australia-2019-3rd-test-scorecard-44040/).

## The chase was more than its final pair

England lost both openers with 15 on the board. Root and Denly added 126 before Denly fell for 50; Root eventually made 77. Later, Stokes and Jonny Bairstow put on 86 for the fifth wicket. Those stands supplied a platform without removing the danger: England subsequently reached 261/7 and then 286/9. [Fall of wickets](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/england-vs-australia-2019-3rd-test-scorecard-44040/) and [ICC report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/ben-stokes-remarkable-135-leads-england-to-incredible-one-wicket-victory).

When Stuart Broad was dismissed, England still needed 73. Stokes was on 61 from 174 balls. He scored another 74 from his next 45 deliveries, whereas Leach’s role was principally to remain at the other end. [Sky Sports’ retrospective](https://www.skysports.com/cricket/news/11794475/ashes-2019-ben-stokes-headingley-heroics-remembered-ahead-of-englands-return-to-leeds-in-2023).

## Why Leach’s one was not the whole partnership story

The stand total was 362 − 286 = 76. Stokes contributed 74, Leach one and the remaining run was an extra. It is therefore inaccurate to assign all 75 runs other than Leach’s single to Stokes. Likewise, Stokes’s full 135 includes runs scored before Leach arrived.

Leach faced 17 deliveries without being dismissed. His small personal run total did not mean that his presence was dispensable: losing his wicket before the target was reached would have ended England’s innings. His single off Cummins brought the scores level, and Stokes’s next-ball four completed the chase. [ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/ben-stokes-remarkable-135-leads-england-to-incredible-one-wicket-victory) and [Cricket Australia’s final-day account](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3303304/the-moments-that-mattered-on-heart-stopping-fourth-day).

## The finish still contained chances for Australia

The outcome was not an uninterrupted sequence of clean hits. Harris failed to hold a difficult chance off Stokes; Lyon then missed a run-out opportunity with Leach out of his ground. Stokes survived the following lbw appeal, and Australia had no review remaining. These were separate events before the winning over, not dismissals erased from the scorecard. [Cricket Australia](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3303304/the-moments-that-mattered-on-heart-stopping-fourth-day).

Headingley kept England in the series; it did not itself secure the Ashes. The 2019 series eventually finished 2–2, with Australia retaining the trophy. The match’s extraordinary ending and the series’ ultimate outcome answer different questions.
