## How the match developed

Mumbai lost Ryan Rickelton and Tilak Varma early. Rohit Sharma's 35 and Suryakumar Yadav's 51 helped repair the innings, while Naman Dhir contributed 28 later. Delhi also lost two early wickets in pursuit, but Pathum Nissanka's 44 and Rizvi's 90 changed the direction of the chase. David Miller finished unbeaten on 21. These are the central contributions documented in the [IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-8-dc-vs-mi-match-report).

The result therefore needs two explanations: Delhi restricted Mumbai before recovering with the bat. Treating the match only as a Rizvi highlight would leave out the first-innings work that kept the target manageable.

## The winning margin and balls remaining

Delhi's target was 163, one more than Mumbai's total. The final score passed that target, but the winning margin is wickets remaining, not the two-run difference between the totals. Four wickets lost left six in hand.

Likewise, 18.1 overs means 18 completed overs plus one legal delivery: 109 balls, leaving 11 of the allotted 120. It is not the decimal number 18.1 multiplied by six.
