**Sunrisers Eastern Cape won SA20 2026, beating Pretoria Capitals by six wickets in the final at Newlands, Cape Town, on 25 January.** They reached 162/4 in 19.2 overs after Pretoria made 158/7, completing the chase with four balls remaining. It was Sunrisers’ third championship in the league’s first four seasons. [Official final score](https://www.sa20.co.za/matches/8516/229143), [SA20 final report](https://www.sa20.co.za/news/report-sunrisers-eastern-cape-win-third-betway-sa20-title).

This recap covers the final, season awards and selected records. The separate final league standings explain qualification and bonus points; playoff results do not change those league totals.

The tournament was Season 4, also listed as SA20 2025-26. It began on 26 December 2025 and ended in January 2026, which explains the two year labels used in fixture lists and score archives. [Official fixture announcement](https://betway-sa20-newsroom.prezly.com/betway-sa20-to-bring-festive-cheer-as-season-4-fixtures-are-announced).

Dewald Brevis gave Pretoria a competitive total with 101 from 56 balls, including eight fours and seven sixes. Marco Jansen returned 3/10 from four overs for Sunrisers. In the chase, Matthew Breetzke finished unbeaten on 68 from 49 balls, while captain Tristan Stubbs made 63 not out from 41. [Full final scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/126653/pc-vs-sec-final-sa20-2025-26).

Brevis contributed approximately **63.9% of Pretoria’s total**, calculated as 101 divided by 158. That shows how heavily the innings depended on him: no other Pretoria batter passed 30. His hundred was the first in an SA20 final. ## How Sunrisers completed the chase

Sunrisers slipped to 48/4 before Breetzke and Stubbs added an unbroken 114 for the fifth wicket. The partnership combined a recovery with a late acceleration: 21 runs came from the 18th over, followed by 12 from the 19th. Stubbs then struck consecutive sixes off Bryce Parsons to finish the match. [Final scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/126653/pc-vs-sec-final-sa20-2025-26), [Official match report](https://www.sa20.co.za/news/report-sunrisers-eastern-cape-win-third-betway-sa20-title).

Their route through the playoffs had included a defeat. Pretoria won Qualifier 1 by seven wickets on 21 January, chasing Sunrisers’ 170/7. Sunrisers recovered against Paarl Royals in Qualifier 2, reaching 117/3 after restricting the Royals to 114/7, before reversing the result against Pretoria in the final. [Official playoff results](https://www.sa20.co.za/matches/results).

## Main season awards

De Kock won both Player of the Season and Batter of the Season. His 390 runs came at a strike rate of 148.85, with four half-centuries.

Baartman was named Bowler of the Season. Jordan Hermann received the Rising Star award after scoring 273 runs, while Sunrisers Eastern Cape won the Spirit of Cricket award.

Brevis won the season’s six-hitting award with 30 sixes, seven of them coming in the final. Jansen received the Moneyball Saver award after delivering 124 dot balls during the season. [Official award winners](https://www.sa20.co.za/news/all-the-season-4-award-winners).

## Records that stood out

Shai Hope made the season’s highest individual score: 118 not out from 69 balls against Durban’s Super Giants at Kingsmead on 7 January. It was also SA20’s highest individual score through the end of Season 4. In the same match, Lungi Ngidi took the competition’s first hat-trick. [Official match report](https://betway-sa20-newsroom.prezly.com/hopes-record-118-and-ngidis-historic-hat-trick-power-capitals-to-victory).

The season also produced the league’s first Super Over, after Joburg Super Kings and Durban’s Super Giants tied at the Wanderers. [Official season review](https://www.sa20.co.za/news/graeme-smith-hails-best-ever-betway-sa20-season-4).

Sunrisers’ championship was their third overall, not a third consecutive title. MI Cape Town had won the preceding edition in February 2025. Keeping that distinction clear matters when comparing SA20 winners across seasons.
