**The announced 2027 men’s ODI World Cup format has a three-team Super Series, two six-team groups, a seven-team round robin and then semifinals and a final.** Fourteen teams enter the event, but only twelve reach its Group Stage. Those numbers describe different rounds, not competing versions of the same team count. [ICC format announcement](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/icc-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2027-schedule-revealed-and-ballot-system-opens).

The tournament is scheduled for 2 October to 21 November 2027 in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. This explanation follows the October 2026 announcements, checked on 14 November. Detailed tie-break and other playing-condition decisions must follow the event’s governing conditions.

## From fourteen entrants to twelve group teams

Ten teams qualify directly for the Group Stage. The winner of the preceding World Cup Qualifier joins them there. The Qualifier’s second-, third- and fourth-placed teams instead enter the Super Series, where they play one another once for the final Group Stage place. [ICC qualification and round structure](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/icc-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2027-schedule-revealed-and-ballot-system-opens).

The arithmetic is:

- Ten direct teams plus four teams from the Qualifier make **fourteen event entrants**.

- Eleven teams already have Group Stage places.

- One of the three Super Series teams joins those eleven, making **twelve**.

## How the Group Stage produces seven teams

The announced groups are:

| Group A | Group B |
| --- | --- |
| India | New Zealand |
| Australia | South Africa |
| Pakistan | Sri Lanka |
| Afghanistan | England |
| Zimbabwe | Bangladesh |
| Qualifier A | Qualifier B |

Source: [ICC group allocation](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/fixture-schedule-announced-for-icc-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2027).

Each team faces the other five teams in its group. The top three from each group advance, along with the better fourth-placed team across the two groups.

That gives **three plus three plus one equals seven**. It does not mean both groups send their top four. A fourth-place finish leaves a comparison with the other group’s fourth-place team, so it is not by itself a confirmed qualification.

## Why there are 57 matches

The announced structure reconciles as follows.

| Stage | Calculation | Matches |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Super Series | Three teams, each pair once | 3 |
| Two groups | Two × six × five ÷ two | 30 |
| Super 7 | Seven × six ÷ two | 21 |
| Semifinals and final | Two plus one | 3 |
| Total | 3 + 30 + 21 + 3 | 57 |

These are fixture counts, not player appearances or a forecast of how many games avoid rain.

The division by two prevents counting every pairing twice. If Team A plays Team B, that is one match even though it appears on both teams’ schedules.

## The Super 7 starts again on points

The ICC says **no Group Stage points carry into the Super 7**. Each of the seven teams faces all six others between 25 October and 14 November. The top four then advance to the semifinals. [ICC event explainer](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/when-does-the-2027-cricket-world-cup-start-key-details-ahead-of-tournament).

For a team entering directly into the groups, reaching the final means five group fixtures, six Super 7 fixtures, a semifinal and the final: **thirteen scheduled matches**. A finalist that came through the Super Series would have two more, making fifteen. These counts exclude matches in the earlier Qualifier.

## Seed labels do not guarantee a team’s place

The ICC preassigned Super 7 slots such as A1 to India and A2 to Australia. These labels help describe possible fixture routes; they are not predictions of final group positions or automatic qualification.

For example, A2 versus B3 on 25 October is Australia versus Sri Lanka **if both qualify**. The ICC’s explanation says Bangladesh would take the B3 slot if Bangladesh qualified and Sri Lanka did not. [ICC conditional-slot explanation](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/fixture-schedule-announced-for-icc-men-s-cricket-world-cup-2027).

A fixture list can therefore show a date and venue before the participating teams are settled. Keep the slot code and the condition when making a calendar; do not turn a seeded name into a confirmed Super 7 appearance.

The Super 7’s top four advance to semifinals, first against fourth and second against third, followed by the final. [ICC event explainer](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/icc-cricket-world-cup-2027/news/when-does-the-2027-cricket-world-cup-start-key-details-ahead-of-tournament).

Identify the current round before reading a qualification headline: reaching the Group Stage, Super 7 and semifinals are different achievements.
