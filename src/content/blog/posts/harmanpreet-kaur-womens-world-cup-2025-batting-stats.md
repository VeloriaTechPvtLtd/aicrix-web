**Harmanpreet Kaur scored 260 runs from 292 balls in the 2025 Women’s ODI World Cup.** She played nine matches but batted eight times, averaging 32.50 with a strike rate of 89.04. Her highest score was 89 against Australia in the semifinal, and she made two fifties. These are completed tournament figures, not her ODI career totals. [Tournament statistics](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-series/10009/icc-womens-world-cup-2025/stats).

Her batting position was consistent: she came in at No. 4 in all eight innings. Her returns were less evenly distributed. The 70 against England and 89 in the semifinal supplied more than three-fifths of her runs, making the match-by-match record more informative than the average alone.

All eight batting innings ended in dismissals, so there were no not-outs to remove from the average’s denominator: 260 divided by eight is 32.50. Dividing by nine appearances would give 28.89 runs per match, a different measure rather than her batting average.

## A fixed position did not mean equal opportunity

Batting fourth did not give her the same number of deliveries in every match. Her completed innings ranged from 11 balls against New Zealand to 88 in the semifinal. Four innings lasted fewer than 25 balls, while her two fifties occupied 158 balls between them.

The position column therefore describes where she entered the order, not a guaranteed workload. It also cannot establish whether another position would have suited her better: this tournament contains no actual innings at a different batting position for that comparison.

Her first four innings produced 71 runs from 94 balls. The England innings then added 70 from 70, almost matching that earlier run total in a single appearance. The later 89 was her largest contribution, but the intervening 10 against New Zealand and final’s 20 remain part of the same record.

The knockout average was higher, but it rests on only two innings. The semifinal’s 89 supplied 81.65% of those 109 knockout runs. The split describes what happened; it does not establish a repeatable ability to score more in knockout matches.

In that semifinal, Harmanpreet and Jemimah Rodrigues added 167 after India had slipped to 59/2. Harmanpreet was dismissed with India at 226/3, so her contribution helped construct the chase rather than finish it. The [ICC’s contemporary report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/awesome-rodrigues-leads-india-to-icc-women-s-cricket-world-cup-2025-final) records her 89 and India’s eventual five-wicket victory.

## What the two fifties explain

The England and semifinal innings combined for 159 runs from 158 balls: 61.15% of her tournament runs from 54.11% of her deliveries. Her other six innings contributed 101 from 134 balls. Keeping both groups visible avoids turning two major contributions into a claim that every innings was productive.

The useful summary is a stable No. 4 position with uneven returns across a short tournament. Read the 260-run total alongside the eight actual innings, the Bangladesh DNB and the concentration in two fifties. None of these historical figures guarantees batting opportunity or output in a later competition.
