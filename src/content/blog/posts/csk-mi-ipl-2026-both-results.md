Chennai Super Kings won both league meetings with Mumbai Indians in IPL 2026: by **103 runs in Mumbai on 23 April**, then by **eight wickets in Chennai on 2 May**. One victory came while defending a big total; the other came through a controlled chase.

The [Mumbai Indians season results](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/fixtures) list the pair's fixtures as Match 33 and Match 44. This archive compares those two matches, rather than mixing them with earlier seasons or treating them as future fixtures.

## Samson and Hosein lead CSK at Wankhede

Mumbai chose to field, but Sanju Samson's unbeaten **101 off 54 balls** gave Chennai the foundation for 207/6. Akeal Hosein then took **4/17**, making the difference with the ball as MI were dismissed for 104.

MI did briefly find resistance through Suryakumar Yadav and Tilak Varma, whose partnership added 60. Hosein dismissed both, preventing that recovery from becoming a sustained chase. Samson received Player of the Match. [Official IPL Match 33 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-33-mi-vs-csk-match-report)

## Chennai restrict MI and complete the chase

Mumbai reversed its toss decision in Chennai, choosing to bat. Naman Dhir's **57 off 37 balls** was the main contribution, but Anshul Kamboj's **three wickets** and Noor Ahmad's **2/26** helped keep the total to 159/7.

Chennai lost Samson early, yet Ruturaj Gaikwad stayed unbeaten on **67 from 48 balls**. Kartik Sharma reached his maiden IPL half-century and helped complete the chase with 11 deliveries remaining. [Official IPL Match 44 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-44-csk-v-mi-match-report)

Gaikwad was Player of the Match, as confirmed by the [official match details](https://www.iplt20.com/matches/b3f0454c-8870-4d01-a75b-3c400374875b/chennai-super-kings-vs-mumbai-indians/match-details). Unlike at Wankhede, Chennai did not need a century or a 200-plus score. Keeping Mumbai's first innings manageable allowed its chase to finish with eight wickets intact.

## What changed between the two games

Mumbai scored **55 more runs** in the return fixture: 159 rather than 104. It also lost seven wickets instead of ten. That was an improvement in its own output, but it did not put the result beyond Chennai's reach.

Across the two matches, CSK lost **eight wickets in total**, while MI lost **17**. This is a useful descriptive contrast, not an adjusted measure of batting quality: the innings had different targets, lengths and match situations.

The winning contributions also changed. Samson and Hosein were central in Mumbai; Gaikwad, Kamboj, Noor and Kartik shaped the return game. Reducing the season double to one player's performance would miss that difference.

## How to interpret the margins correctly

A 103-run victory and an eight-wicket victory cannot be added together into a single meaningful margin. They describe different winning conditions. Nor does Chennai's lower second-match total indicate worse batting: a chasing side stops once it passes the target.

The 18.1-over finish means **109 legal balls**, not 18.1 decimal overs. With 120 balls available, that leaves **11**, matching the official report.

For future analysis, these matches are evidence about two specific games. They do not prove that winning the toss, choosing to bat or playing at either venue guarantees the next result. The clear historical conclusion is narrower: **Chennai won away while defending and at home while chasing, completing the 2026 league double.**

## Keep team totals and individual roles separate

Samson’s 101 represented about 48.8% of Chennai’s 207 at Wankhede. Gaikwad’s 67 was about 41.9% of the successful 160-run chase in Chennai. These percentages describe contributions to different innings; they are not a ranking of which batter played the better match. The first innings set a target, while the second ended when that target was passed.

The same care applies to bowling. Hosein’s four wickets belonged to the Mumbai fixture, whereas Kamboj’s three and Noor’s two came in Chennai. Combining their figures into one match summary would erase the very distinction this two-match archive is intended to preserve.
