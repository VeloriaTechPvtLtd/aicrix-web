England won the 1993 Women’s World Cup final at Lord’s on 1 August by 67 runs. Janette Brittin’s 48 and Carole Hodges’s 45 established the innings, before Jo Chamberlain’s 38 from 33 balls helped England reach 195/5. [Lord’s historical account](https://www.lords.org/lords/our-history/father-time-wall/1993-england-beat-new-zealand-in-the-women-s-world)

## The last phase changed the total

This was a 60-over match. England were 118/4 after 48.3 overs; Chamberlain and Barbara Daniels took the score to 175 before the fifth wicket fell at 57.2. Daniels finished unbeaten on 21. [Full scorecard](https://img.cricinfo.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/WORLD_CUPS/WWC93/ENG-WOMEN_NZ-WOMEN_WWC93_WODI-FINAL_01AUG1993.html)

England added 77 from the last 69 legal balls, a scoring rate of approximately 6.70 runs per over. The calculation treats 48.3 as 48 overs and three balls, not a decimal. Subtracting 291 balls from the scheduled 360 leaves 69.

The fifth-wicket stand was worth 57. It is one component of that 77-run closing phase, not an additional amount to be added on top. Chamberlain’s personal strike rate was approximately 115.15 runs per 100 balls.

## England defended through both pressure and wickets

New Zealand were dismissed for 128. Gill Smith took 3/29, while Karen Smithies conceded just 14 in 12 overs, including four maidens. [Lord’s account](https://www.lords.org/lords/our-history/father-time-wall/1993-england-beat-new-zealand-in-the-women-s-world)

Smithies’s economy rate was approximately 1.17. That is a different contribution from Smith’s three wickets: one restricts how quickly a chase advances, while the other removes batting resources.

New Zealand’s sixth-wicket stand moved the score from 71 to 110, but the final five wickets fell for 18 runs. [Scorecard](https://img.cricinfo.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/WORLD_CUPS/WWC93/ENG-WOMEN_NZ-WOMEN_WWC93_WODI-FINAL_01AUG1993.html)

## Why the finish matters

The 67-run margin is the difference between the two totals. England’s late acceleration had contributed 77, so stopping the story at the early batting platform would miss a major part of the winning innings. The score progression shows a patient foundation followed by a much faster finish, then a defence combining economical overs and regular dismissals.
