## Australia vs Oman 2024: the score and the date

Australia beat Oman by 39 runs at Kensington Oval, Bridgetown, in their opening match of the 2024 men’s T20 World Cup. Marcus Stoinis made an unbeaten 67 and took three wickets, but the final margin conceals how effectively Oman restricted Australia early in the innings. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman).

The local fixture date was 5 June 2024. The scheduled 8:30 pm Barbados start was 00:30 UTC and 6:00 am IST on 6 June, explaining the next-day date used in many reports. This is the Barbados match, not the teams’ 2026 meeting in Pallekele. [Scorecard match information](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/87563/aus-vs-oman-10th-match-group-b-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2024).

| Innings | Score | Overs | What the score meant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Australia | 164/5 | 20 | Oman needed 165 to win |
| Oman | 125/9 | 20 | Australia won by 39 runs |

Oman chose to field. Both teams received their full 20 overs; the result was not a shortened-innings comparison or a revised-target chase. The winning margin comes from 164 minus 125, not from subtracting Oman’s score from the target. [Match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman).

## Why Australia needed a recovery

Mehran Khan dismissed Mitchell Marsh and Glenn Maxwell on consecutive deliveries in the ninth over, leaving Australia 50/3. Stoinis therefore entered with an immediate rebuilding job rather than a ready-made platform for a short finishing burst. Warner and Stoinis subsequently added 102 for the fourth wicket. [ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman) and [Cricket Australia](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4033925/match-report-australia-beat-oman-t20-world-cup-group-b-barbados-kensington-highlights-stoinis-warner-maxwell-duck-aqib-ilyas-catch).

Warner’s 56 from 51 balls and Stoinis’s 67 not out from 36 were different contributions within the same recovery. The latter included six sixes. Reading the two final strike rates alone would hide the changing situation they shared: wickets had already fallen before the partnership could accelerate. [ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman).

## The decisive change was in the final eight overs

Australia were 63/3 after 12 overs, then added 101 in the remaining eight. That divides the innings into two very different scoring periods. [ICC’s match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman) and [Cricket Australia](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4033925/match-report-australia-beat-oman-t20-world-cup-group-b-barbados-kensington-highlights-stoinis-warner-maxwell-duck-aqib-ilyas-catch).

| Australia innings segment | Runs added | Legal balls available | Calculated runs per over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First 12 overs | 63 | 72 | 5.25 |
| Final eight overs | 101 | 48 | 12.63 |
| Whole innings | 164 | 120 | 8.20 |

The last 40% of the legal-ball allocation produced about 61.6% of Australia’s runs. These are team scoring rates, including extras, not individual batting strike rates. The comparison explains why extrapolating the first 12 overs would have badly understated the eventual total; it does not establish a repeatable acceleration rate for another match.

## Oman’s late runs came after the requirement had climbed

Stoinis followed his batting effort with [3/19 from three overs](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/87563/aus-vs-oman-10th-match-group-b-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2024). Australia’s wicket-taking was not confined to him: Starc, Ellis and Zampa each took two. Oman nevertheless completed all 20 overs rather than being bowled out. [ICC](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/live-australia-sent-in-as-they-kick-off-their-tournament-against-oman).

The scorecard’s sixth-wicket checkpoint was 57/6 at 12.6 overs, meaning 13 completed overs. Oman then needed 108 from 42 balls, a required rate of 15.43 per over. They added 68 in the final seven overs, but that was 40 fewer than the 108 required for victory. Ayaan Khan’s 36 and Mehran’s 27 provided resistance without overturning the earlier damage. [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/87563/aus-vs-oman-10th-match-group-b-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2024).

That is why late scoring and a comfortable final margin can coexist. Australia’s acceleration established a much larger target than their early progress suggested; Oman’s recovery improved the final total but started with too many runs still needed. Neither observation is a forecast about these teams, this venue or a future fantasy selection.
