The DRS countdown gives a player a short window to request a review. Under the current men’s international protocol, that window is **15 seconds from the ball becoming dead**. It is not a countdown for the third umpire to finish examining the incident. [ICC men’s T20I conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.2](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bsxcho7ufe2pjban0wjp.pdf)

DRS means Decision Review System. A **Player Review** is requested by a player; an **Umpire Review** is initiated by an official. Seeing the television umpire involved does not, by itself, mean a team has spent a review. [ICC DRS overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/about/cricket/rules-and-regulations/decision-review-system)

## Who requests the review

For a dismissal review, the batter given out makes the request. When the batter is given not out, the fielding captain or acting captain requests it. The dismissal-review procedure excludes Timed Out. [ICC men’s Test conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.1](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/a8114zro5tkioifz0jqg.pdf)

The player makes a T signal with the forearms at head height. The umpire prompts after ten seconds; a request outside the 15-second limit is declined. Batters may consult each other, and the captain may consult teammates on the field. Dressing-room signals are not permitted. Once requested, a review cannot be withdrawn. [ICC men’s T20I conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.2](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bsxcho7ufe2pjban0wjp.pdf)

## How many reviews a team has

The allowance is per team, per innings:

- Men’s Tests: **three unsuccessful reviews**. [Test conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.6.1](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/a8114zro5tkioifz0jqg.pdf)

- Men’s ODIs: **two unsuccessful reviews**. [ODI conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.6.1](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bxkzkcyvfn6tvi70zh0w.pdf)

- Men’s T20Is: **two unsuccessful reviews**. [T20I conditions, Appendix D, paragraph 3.6.1](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bsxcho7ufe2pjban0wjp.pdf)

“Unsuccessful” is the important word. The limit is not simply the number of times a team has made the T signal.

## Why an unchanged decision can retain the review

An overturned decision does not use the allowance. An unchanged decision normally does, but an LBW decision unchanged solely because of Umpire’s Call retains the review. A batter remaining out under a different dismissal mode still counts as unsuccessful. [ICC men’s ODI conditions, Appendix D, paragraphs 3.6.2–3.6.5](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bxkzkcyvfn6tvi70zh0w.pdf)

Umpire’s Call preserves the on-field verdict for specified marginal ball-tracking findings concerning impact or the wicket. It does not automatically mean not out. If that marginal finding is decisive, a batter originally given out remains out; one originally given not out remains not out. [ICC men’s Test conditions, Appendix A, paragraph 5.10, and Appendix D, paragraph 3.4](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/a8114zro5tkioifz0jqg.pdf)

Other exceptions include a not-out decision upheld because a no-ball makes further evaluation unnecessary, or an Umpire Review that makes the Player Review unnecessary. A technology failure can justify reinstatement at the match referee’s discretion; inconclusive evidence from functioning equipment does not automatically qualify. [ICC men’s ODI conditions, Appendix D, paragraphs 3.6.6–3.6.8](https://images.icc-cricket.com/image/upload/prd/bxkzkcyvfn6tvi70zh0w.pdf)

## Read the decision and the allowance separately

Imagine a Test team starts with three reviews available. It loses one ordinary unsuccessful review, retains its next review on a decisive Umpire’s Call, then successfully overturns another decision. It has requested three reviews but still has **two available**. This is an illustrative ledger, not a real match sequence.

When following a review, ask two questions: did the verdict change, and did the allowance change? That distinction explains how a batter can remain out while the batting team keeps its review. For league cricket or a different competition, check its own conditions rather than transferring this men’s international allowance automatically.
