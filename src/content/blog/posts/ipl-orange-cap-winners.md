**David Warner has won the most IPL Orange Caps: three, in 2015, 2017 and 2019.** Chris Gayle and Virat Kohli have won two each. This count covers completed seasons through **IPL 2026**. [Completed winners list](https://news.abplive.com/sports/ipl/full-list-of-ipl-orange-cap-purple-cap-winners-from-2008-to-2026-1847385).

The count refers to the award for finishing a season as the leading run-scorer. It does not count every occasion a batter temporarily wore the cap during the tournament.

## Who won the Orange Cap in 2026

**Vaibhav Sooryavanshi won the 2026 Orange Cap with 776 runs for Rajasthan Royals.** Shubman Gill finished next with 732 and Sai Sudharsan followed with 722. [Wisden’s final run-scoring table](https://www.wisden.com/series/ipl-2026/cricket-news/ipl-2026-orange-cap-full-list-who-is-leading-the-run-getters-table-after-todays-ipl-match).

This was a final season outcome, not a lead from an earlier matchday. With one award, Sooryavanshi did not displace Warner at the top of the career award count.

## Why 848 runs did not give Warner another cap

Warner scored **848 runs in 2016**, but Kohli made **973** in the same season. [IPL’s historical statistical review](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/quick-stats-final-csk-vs-srh).

Kohli’s total was 125 higher. Warner’s 848 exceeded each of his own three Orange Cap-winning totals, yet he did not win the award that year. The distinction is the competition within the same season: there is no fixed run threshold that guarantees an Orange Cap.

It is also separate from the team championship. Warner’s Sunrisers Hyderabad won the IPL 2016 final against Kohli’s Royal Challengers Bangalore by eight runs, while Kohli won the individual run-scoring award. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/match-report-final-rcb-vs-srh), [IPL awards archive](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-awards-2017).

## A temporary leader is not another season winner

During a tournament, the cap can change hands as different batters move ahead. The IPL’s 2017 awards notice explicitly distinguished wearing the cap during the season from receiving the final season award. [Official awards notice](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-awards-2017).

For example, a hypothetical batter who takes the lead, loses it and regains it has not won two season awards. Until the tournament ends, those are changes in the running leaderboard.
