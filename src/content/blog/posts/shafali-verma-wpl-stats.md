## More fours did not mean more boundary runs

Shafali hit five more fours in 2026 than in 2025, yet her boundary-run total fell. The calculation is straightforward: 39 fours and four sixes produced 180 runs, compared with 232 from 34 fours and 16 sixes in 2025.

The extra fours added 20 runs, while the loss of 12 sixes removed 72. The net change was 52 fewer boundary runs. Her overall aggregate fell by 45, so runs not recorded as fours or sixes increased slightly, from 72 to 79. This describes the scoring mix; it does not establish whether pitches, bowling plans or shot selection caused the change.

She also faced eight more balls in one additional innings. Her 2026 scoring rate was roughly 28 runs per 100 balls below 2025. Calling the season simply “259 runs” misses both the opportunity and the reduced pace of scoring.

## A career rate is not the latest-season rate

Before 2026, Shafali had 865 runs from 532 balls, a combined strike rate of 162.59. Adding the 2026 return brings the career rate down to 152.10. This is calculated from total runs and balls, not by averaging the four displayed annual strike rates.

There were no unbeaten innings in 2026, so her batting average and runs per innings are both 25.90. Across her career, three not outs leave 34 dismissals from 37 innings. That distinction explains why the career average of 33.06 is higher than her 30.38 runs per innings. [Career denominators](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/14825/shafali-verma).

## Bowling became a real but small part of her WPL record

Delhi’s profile records Shafali’s entire WPL bowling sample in 2026: five bowling innings, 54 legal balls, 74 runs conceded and three wickets. That is nine overs across ten matches, not a regular four-over role.

Her standout spell was 2 for 16 from four overs against UP Warriorz in Match 7. She also made 36 from 32 balls in Delhi’s seven-wicket win and was Player of the Match. [Official match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-7).

That single spell supplied 24 of her 54 season deliveries and two of her three wickets. It demonstrates a successful tactical option without proving a stable bowling allocation. Even averaging only the matches in which she bowled gives 10.8 balls per bowling innings.

The balanced summary is a high-scoring-rate WPL career, a slower 2026 batting season and a small new bowling contribution. Upcoming-match analysis should establish her current role separately rather than importing her international form or assuming that one four-over spell will recur.
