## What changed in 2026

Mumbai’s official season table records 321 runs from seven innings, an average of 64.20, a strike rate of 151.41 and eight wickets. Her highest score was 100 not out. [MI season statistics](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/player-stats-wpl)

The century came against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in Match 16. She made 100 not out from 57 balls, with 16 fours and one six, as Mumbai posted 199/4 and won by 15 runs. The league’s report identifies it as the first WPL century. [Official match report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-16)

Those boundaries supplied 70 of her 100 runs: 16 × 4 plus 6. The remaining 30 were not boundary runs credited to her bat. Her innings strike rate was 175.44, faster than her season rate, but the season calculation also includes the other six innings.
