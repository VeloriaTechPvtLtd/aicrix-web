## Most catches by fielders in Test cricket

Steve Smith leads the published men’s Test fielding catch records with 224, followed by Joe Root on 220. Five players have at least 200. The figures below were checked on 14 November 2026 and concern catches by fielders, not wicketkeepers’ catches or combined catches and stumpings. [Cricket Australia’s Smith statistics](https://www.cricket.com.au/players/CA:1075/steve-smith) confirm his current total.

## Every fielder with at least 200 catches

| Rank | Player | Fielding catches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Steve Smith | 224 |
| 2 | Joe Root | 220* |
| 3 | Rahul Dravid | 210 |
| 4 | Mahela Jayawardene | 205 |
| 5 | Jacques Kallis | 200 |

The [ACS career fielding table](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Test/Overall/Fielding/Most_Catches_by_Fielder_in_a_Career.html) places Ricky Ponting immediately below the threshold on 196. There are no tied totals in the qualifying five. Kallis belongs because the threshold is “at least 200”, not “more than 200”. Ponting’s final total is also documented by [Cricket Australia](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/3262109/what-if-reassessing-ricky).

*ACS includes one Root catch for which its record does not establish whether he was keeping wicket. His published 220 therefore carries a classification caveat; it is not a claim that every event has been independently verified as a non-keeping catch. Removing that unresolved event would not change his position or membership of the 200-catch group.

## Fielding catches are not keeper dismissals

A wicketkeeper’s catch and a stumping are different dismissals. Adding them together produces a wicketkeeping-dismissals total, not a fielding-catches total. Equally, a player who has sometimes kept wicket can have legitimate catches elsewhere in the field. The role at the dismissal matters more than a permanent label attached to the player.

ACS illustrates the distinction with AB de Villiers: its fielders’ record excludes 101 catches from matches in which he kept wicket. It also separates one keeper catch from Viv Richards’s fielding record. A generic player-profile “catches” column cannot automatically be reused as a non-keeper leaderboard.

Nor does “fielder” mean only someone standing in the outfield near the boundary. Slip catches count. At Durban on 11 October 2026, Smith held catches off both Nathan Lyon and Pat Cummins from slip positions. Cricket Australia’s [match report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4590562/south-africa-australia-first-test-kingsmead-durban-match-report-day-three-news-scores-video-highlights-toss-hazlewood-cummins-starc-steve-smith-catches) identifies his second catch that day as the 221st of his Test career, the moment he passed Root. That milestone total is not his later 224.

## Career volume versus catches per match

Smith’s 224 catches across 127 Tests work out to 1.76 per match. Dravid’s 210 across 164 give 1.28, while Jayawardene’s 205 across 149 give 1.38. These are calculated career rates, not catch-success percentages. The match totals and catches agree between the fielding ledger and official career sources. [ICC on Dravid](https://www.icc-cricket.com/media-releases/dravid-ponting-and-taylor-inducted-into-icc-cricket-hall-of-fame), [ICC on Jayawardene](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/mahela-jayawardena-run-scoring-marvel-and-world-cup-star).

Jayawardene finished five catches behind Dravid despite playing 15 fewer Tests. That explains why aggregate order and catches per match give different comparisons without requiring a judgment about who had better hands.

A Test appearance does not guarantee two full innings in the field, and a match does not give every position the same number of chances. Bowling style, edges, field placement and innings duration change the opportunities. To calculate a success rate, the denominator would have to include eligible chances, including dropped catches. Neither total catches nor matches supplies that denominator.

## Use the cutoff before comparing records

Smith’s current total includes his October South Africa tour appearances. He missed the third Test; the [ICC’s 29 October update](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/neck-injury-confirmed-as-new-zealand-the-focus-for-smith) said his next selection consideration would be the December New Zealand series. Root’s latest displayed Test is the September Edgbaston match against Pakistan. These are dated career snapshots, not predictions of the next record change.

Use the all-Test table for this question. A country-only filter can omit a player’s appearances for another representative Test side. Refresh the ledger, its role notes and the next player below the threshold together; do not simply replace the leader’s number while leaving the rest at an earlier date.
