Pakistan beat England by eight wickets at Cardiff on 14 June 2017. England made 211 in 49.5 overs; Pakistan reached 215/2 in 37.1. [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/16712/eng-vs-pak-1st-semi-final-a1-v-b2-icc-champions-trophy-2017)

## England had a platform, not a finished total

England were 128/2 before Joe Root fell for 46 to Shadab Khan. The ICC identified his dismissal as a turning point. Hasan Ali removed Jonny Bairstow, Eoin Morgan and Ben Stokes, while Junaid Khan and Rumman Raees took two wickets each. [ICC match analysis](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/england-v-pakistan-key-plays)

England’s final eight wickets fell for 83 runs. That describes a failure to develop an existing platform, not an innings that collapsed from its first delivery. The distinction gives Pakistan’s middle-innings bowling its proper place in the explanation.

## Staying in did not guarantee acceleration

Stokes scored 34 from 64 balls without a boundary. [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/16712/eng-vs-pak-1st-semi-final-a1-v-b2-icc-champions-trophy-2017)

His strike rate was 53.13 runs per 100 balls when rounded to two decimals. That records a limited scoring return from a substantial stay. It does not establish his intentions or prove that any particular alternative shot would have succeeded.

## Pakistan preserved the continuity they had removed

Azhar Ali and Fakhar Zaman added 118. Azhar made 76 and Fakhar 57; Babar Azam and Mohammad Hafeez finished unbeaten on 38 and 31. [Chase record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/16712/eng-vs-pak-1st-semi-final-a1-v-b2-icc-champions-trophy-2017)

The opening partnership covered approximately 55.7% of the target of 212. Pakistan needed only 94 more when the first wicket fell, rather than asking the middle order to repair repeated early dismissals.

The chase ended with 77 legal balls unused: 300 minus 223. The large wicket margin and substantial time remaining came from two connected phases. Pakistan interrupted England’s transition to a larger total, then built the partnership that made their own remaining task smaller.
