Chris Gayle holds the IPL individual-innings sixes record with **17**, struck for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Pune Warriors India on 23 April 2013. His unbeaten 175 contained 102 runs from sixes alone. The [official match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/report-match-31-rcb-vs-pwi) records the innings and its 17 sixes.

The nearest qualifying innings in the checked through-2026 record list have 13, not 16. A useful comparison must preserve those ties and count performances rather than only distinct players.

There are four players but seven innings. Gayle appears three times and Sooryavanshi twice. Removing repeated names would turn an innings leaderboard into a different question and omit qualifying performances.

## A tie is still a tie when the scores differ

McCullum's 158 not out and Gayle's 128 not out share second place because both contain 13 sixes. The larger score does not break a tie in a sixes table. For the same reason, all four 12-six innings share fourth place.

The ordering within each tied group is chronological for readability. It does not imply that the earlier innings ranks above the later one. A publisher that numbers the seven rows from one to seven without a tie explanation obscures the actual record.

This matters particularly for Sooryavanshi's 97. A century is not required to join the group. He hit 12 sixes in the 27 May 2026 Eliminator despite finishing three runs short of a hundred. [Official IPL Eliminator report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-eliminator-srh-v-rr-match-report).

## Same sixes, different exposure

Sooryavanshi's two entries make a clean within-player comparison. His April innings used 37 balls; his May innings used 29. Both contained 12 sixes and five fours. The April figures are shown in his [individual innings record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/151902/player/51791/batting?inningsId=1), and the May figures in the official Eliminator report.

In each innings, sixes supplied 72 runs and fours supplied 20, a combined 92. That left 11 runs from other scoring strokes in the 103, but only five in the 97. The boundaries were identical in count while the final totals and opportunities were not.

As a descriptive rate, 12 divided by 37 is 32.4 sixes per 100 balls faced; 12 divided by 29 is 41.4. This is not the chance of a six on any future delivery. These are selected exceptional innings, not a representative sample of his batting.

## The record gap and the category boundary

Gayle's 17 stands four sixes above the joint-second mark of 13. In six-hit runs, the gap is 24. That is a more precise description of this record than comparing overall innings scores, which also include fours and runs between wickets.

Keep the competition boundary intact. An innings from another domestic T20 league or an international match does not enter an IPL record list. Nor does a new season aggregate replace a single-innings record. Sooryavanshi's season tally belongs in his separate season profile; this table concerns what happened in one innings at a time.

A six-count record also does not measure hitting distance, match-winning value or fantasy profitability. Those require different evidence. The narrow, verifiable answer remains Gayle's 17, with seven innings reaching at least 12 through the completed 2026 season.
