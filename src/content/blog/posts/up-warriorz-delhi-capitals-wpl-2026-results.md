This is a completed-season comparison, not an all-time head-to-head or a prediction for their next encounter. ## What changed across the pair?

UP's total dropped by 32 runs between meetings, from 154 to 122, a 20.78% reduction. Delhi had a smaller assignment but lost two more wickets while completing it. The contrast is a useful reminder to compare target size, chase progress and balls remaining together rather than ranking performances by the wicket margin alone.

For this season, the conclusion is straightforward: Delhi swept the pair, once through a last-ball finish and once through a recovered chase that settled qualification. Two results do not establish a permanent advantage or a future win probability.
