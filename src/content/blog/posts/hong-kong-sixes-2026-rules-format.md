## Six players and six overs

Hong Kong Sixes 2026 used six-player sides and six-over innings. It ran from 30 October to 1 November at Tin Kwong Road Recreation Ground. This is an edition-specific format, not an ordinary T20 match with a smaller squad. The organizer’s [3 October announcement](https://hongkongsixes.live/hong-kong-sixes-turns-it-up-to-six-for-bigger-2026-edition/) allowed one designated bowler to deliver two overs while the other bowlers generally delivered one.

That makes the individual allowance important. Six available team overs do not mean a captain can give three to the leading bowler. Conversely, limiting each of the five bowlers in the example below to one over would leave an over unallocated.

## A bowling allocation worked through

Consider a hypothetical side with wicketkeeper K and five bowling options A, B, C, D and E. Designate A for the extra over. One simple allocation is A, B, C, D, E, A: the five bowlers contribute 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 6 overs.

After the first five overs in that example, the team has one over left. B through E have already delivered their one-over allocations; A has delivered one of the designated two. The final over belongs to A in this plan, not whichever player has conceded the fewest runs so far.

This allocation assumes an uninterrupted innings and no exceptional replacement. An innings that ends early may leave some of those overs unused.

## Group position selects a different competition

The four pools each contained three teams. First place led to the Cup, second to the Plate and third to the Bowl. All three branches continued into semi-finals and placement matches; they were not three names for the same championship. [Official format announcement](https://hongkongsixes.live/hong-kong-sixes-turns-it-up-to-six-for-bigger-2026-edition/)

| Group finish | Branch | Final decides | Losing semi-finalists play for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | Cup | First and second | Third and fourth |
| Second | Plate | Fifth and sixth | Seventh and eighth |
| Third | Bowl | Ninth and tenth | Eleventh and twelfth |

The [official fixture page](https://hongkongsixes.live/fixture/) gives these placement routes. A Plate semi-final victory therefore earns a place in the fifth-place final, not a return to the Cup title race.

For a fictional Pool A side, compare two paths. Finish first, win the Cup semi-final, and the next match is for the championship. Finish second and win the Plate semi-final, and the next match is for fifth. Winning a semi-final has a different meaning because the group stage already selected the branch.

## Why 25 scheduled matches is not 25 men’s tournament matches

The published schedule has 12 group matches, six semi-finals, three placement play-offs and three finals. That is 24 men’s fixtures. Its separate women’s exhibition takes the event schedule to 25 slots. [Official fixture list](https://hongkongsixes.live/fixture/)

An independent cross-check uses team appearances: each of 12 teams has two group games, one semi-final and one final or placement game in the schedule. That produces 12 × 4 = 48 team appearances. Divide by two teams per match to obtain 24 fixtures. This checks the planned structure; it is not an assertion that every scheduled innings was completed.

## Read the edition as an archive

Afghanistan won the Cup, beating Australia in the 1 November final, according to the [organizer’s final report](https://hongkongsixes.live/afghanistan-script-history-with-stunning-final-chase-to-lift-maiden-hong-kong-sixes-title/). The event is over, so its fixtures should now be read as an archive rather than an upcoming schedule.

When comparing its statistics, keep this format separate from ordinary T20 cricket and keep the exhibition separate from the men’s tournament. For a match lookup, identify the edition and branch before interpreting a result: winning a Bowl final and winning the Cup final are different achievements, even though both scorecards say “final.”
