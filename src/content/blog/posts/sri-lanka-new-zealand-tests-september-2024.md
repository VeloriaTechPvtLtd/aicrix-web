Sri Lanka won both Tests against New Zealand at Galle in September 2024: the first by 63 runs and the second by an innings and 154 runs. Although the venue was unchanged, the matches followed very different scoring patterns. [First Test](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360634.html), [Second Test](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360635.html)

## Dates and results

| Test | Playing dates | Sri Lanka | New Zealand | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 18–20 and 22–23 September | 305 and 309 | 340 and 211 | Sri Lanka by 63 runs |
| Second | 26–29 September | 602/5 declared | 88 and 360 | Sri Lanka by an innings and 154 runs |

The first match included a rest day on 21 September because of Sri Lanka’s presidential election. It therefore spanned six calendar dates without becoming a six-playing-day Test. The second was scheduled through 30 September but finished a day early. [ICC schedule announcement](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/rest-day-returns-as-sri-lanka-announce-schedule-for-new-zealand-test-series)

## First Test: overturning a deficit

New Zealand’s 340 gave them a first-innings lead of 35. Sri Lanka’s second innings of 309 set a target of **275**: 305 + 309 − 340 + 1. New Zealand’s combined 551 (340 + 211) was 63 below Sri Lanka’s 614 (305 + 309). They needed 64 more in the chase to reach the winning target. [First scorecard](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360634.html)

## Second Test: no fourth innings required

Sri Lanka declared at 602/5, with Kamindu Mendis unbeaten on 182. New Zealand followed on after being dismissed for 88, then made 360. Their combined 448 remained 154 behind Sri Lanka’s single innings. Sri Lanka consequently had no second innings to list. [Second scorecard](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360635.html)

## Kamindu’s average and the wicket totals

Kamindu’s scores were 114, 13 and 182 not out: **309 runs in three innings but two dismissals**, giving an average of 154.50 rather than 103. Prabath Jayasuriya took 4 + 5 wickets in the first match and 6 + 3 in the second, totalling 18. Nishan Peiris took 3 + 6 in the second Test. [First card](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360634.html), [Second card](https://archive.nzc.nz/Scorecards/1360/1360635.html)

Those totals should remain attached to this two-match series, rather than being presented as season or career figures.
