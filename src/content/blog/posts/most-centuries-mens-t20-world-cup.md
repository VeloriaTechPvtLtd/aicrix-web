**Chris Gayle and Sahibzada Farhan share the men’s T20 World Cup record with two centuries each.** Gayle scored his hundreds in 2007 and 2016. Farhan scored both of his in 2026, becoming the first batter to make two hundreds in one edition. The record below covers completed men’s World Cup tournaments through 2026 and was checked on 14 November 2026. [ICC’s record report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/history-made-as-farhan-breaks-kohli-s-record-matches-gayle)

## The joint record holders

| Rank by centuries | Player | Team | World Cup hundreds | Editions containing those hundreds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Joint 1 | Chris Gayle | West Indies | 2 | 2007 and 2016 |
| Joint 1 | Sahibzada Farhan | Pakistan | 2 | 2026 |

Both players rank first on the measure being compared: number of centuries. Batting average, total runs or alphabetical order should not turn this tie into an outright lead. This is a table of the joint leaders, rather than a complete list of every World Cup centurion. The [current career table](https://www.cricketsky.com/icc-t20-world-cup-most-hundreds-s2/) also lists both with two hundreds.

## The four innings behind the record

| Player | Opponent and match date | Ground | Final score | Balls faced in completed innings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chris Gayle | South Africa, 11 September 2007 | Wanderers, Johannesburg | 117 | 57 |
| Chris Gayle | England, 16 March 2016 | Wankhede, Mumbai | 100* | 48 |
| Sahibzada Farhan | Namibia, 18 February 2026 | Sinhalese Sports Club, Colombo | 100* | 58 |
| Sahibzada Farhan | Sri Lanka, 28 February 2026 | Pallekele | 100 | 60 |

The asterisk means not out. A hundred counts once whether the batter finishes unbeaten or is dismissed.

Gayle’s 117 came in the first match of the inaugural men’s T20 World Cup. His second century followed nine years later against England. The innings scores and balls faced are recorded in [ICC’s Gayle retrospective](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/by-the-numbers-six-machine-and-a-record-setting-champion), with dates checked against [ICC’s historical tables](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/game-changers-and-record-makers-statistical-standouts-from-the-icc-men-s-t20-world-cups) and the [ACS England–West Indies archive](https://archive.acscricket.com/records_and_stats/progressive_team_v_team_records_wctt/England_v_West_Indies_wctt.html).

Farhan’s unbeaten Namibia hundred helped Pakistan reach 199/3 and win by 102 runs. The [BCCI match record](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/fc8bf9e5-dc36-4f4f-8c1b-0fe0cd6ba1a9/pakistan-vs-namibia/match-details) supplies the date, venue and result; [Cricbuzz’s innings record](https://m.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/139296/player/13625/batting) confirms 100 not out from 58 balls.

Ten days later, his 100 from 60 balls helped Pakistan post 212/8 against Sri Lanka. Pakistan won by five runs at Pallekele. [BCCI match record](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/233456/More/match-details)

## Career record versus single-edition record

These are related but different questions:

- **Most centuries across a World Cup career:** Gayle and Farhan, two each.

- **Most centuries in one men’s T20 World Cup:** Farhan, two in 2026.

Gayle’s two hundreds belong to separate tournaments. Farhan’s two belong to the same tournament. That is why Farhan can hold the single-edition record outright while sharing the career record. [ICC’s explanation of the distinction](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/history-made-as-farhan-breaks-kohli-s-record-matches-gayle)

A century count also differs from aggregate runs. A batter’s fifties and smaller innings contribute to total runs but do not add to the hundreds column. Neither measure, by itself, describes the whole tournament performance.

## Is the most prolific centurion also the fastest?

Not necessarily. **Finn Allen holds the fastest men’s T20 World Cup hundred at 33 balls**, set in 2026. He surpassed Gayle’s previous 47-ball mark from 2016. This speed record asks how quickly a batter reached 100, rather than how many centuries he scored. [ICC’s post-tournament records review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/all-records-broken-at-the-icc-men-s-t20-world-cup-2026?appview=true)

Notice that Gayle’s 2016 innings appears above as 100 not out from **48 balls**, while his century milestone took **47 balls**. Both figures can be correct: the completed innings can include a delivery faced after the hundred was reached. Do not use the final balls-faced column to build a fastest-century ranking.

## What counts in this record?

This article covers the senior **men’s T20 World Cup tournament itself**. It does not combine women’s World Cups, qualifying competitions, warm-ups, bilateral internationals or domestic leagues.

A century in an IPL match does not enter this table. Nor does a T20I hundred outside the World Cup. When comparing records, first match the competition, gender category, time period and measure. Those four checks prevent a correct statistic from becoming the wrong answer to the question.
