Smriti Mandhana had two Women’s T20 International centuries through 20 October 2026. Her first came against England in June 2025; her second came against Hong Kong China in September 2026. This is a dated international-format record, not her WPL or ODI century tally.

## The two hundreds

| Date | Opponent | Runs | Balls | Fours | Sixes | Calculated strike rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 28 June 2025 | England | 112 | 62 | 15 | 3 | 180.65 |
| 3 September 2026 | Hong Kong China | 124 | 64 | 14 | 7 | 193.75 |

Sources: [ICC report on her maiden century](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/mandhana-eyes-more-t20i-success-after-maiden-ton), [England innings scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/105892/player/10012/batting), and [ICC report on her second century](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/mandhana-surpasses-historic-milestones-in-asia-cup?appview=true).

At Nottingham, her 112 helped India win by 97 runs while she was standing in as captain. That innings contained 78 boundary runs. The second hundred contained 98 boundary runs: 14 fours contribute 56 and seven sixes add 42. The higher boundary contribution explains part of the difference between the two scoring rates without requiring a claim that the opposition or match conditions were equivalent.

## October form is a different sample

The subsequent T20I batting ledger contains no further hundred through 20 October. Against Zimbabwe that month, her entries were:

| Date | Runs | Balls | Dismissal status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 October | 9 | 6 | Out |
| 18 October | 47 | 25 | Not out |
| 20 October | Did not bat | — | No innings |

Source: [Format-labelled batting ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/10012/smriti-mandhana/all-matches/batting).

Those two batting innings produced 56 runs from 31 balls. The batting average is 56.00 because there was one dismissal; runs per innings is 28.00. The combined strike rate is 180.65, calculated from the totals rather than averaging the two innings rates. A did-not-bat entry adds neither an innings nor a dismissal. Later October ODI scores must not be added to this T20I sample.

## Captaincy has its own dated evidence

BCCI announced Mandhana as India’s full-time women’s white-ball captain on 6 October 2026. Its 9 November South Africa tour announcement then listed her as captain in the Test, ODI and T20I squads. These are separate statements: the October white-ball appointment alone does not establish a permanent Test appointment. [October announcement](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/smriti-mandhana-appointed-captain-of-indian-womens-cricket-team), [November squads](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/indias-womens-squad-for-all-format-tour-of-south-africa-announced).

The batting cutoff here remains 20 October; the captaincy information is dated separately through the November squad announcement.
