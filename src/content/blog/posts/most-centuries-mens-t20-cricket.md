**Chris Gayle leads men's T20 career centuries with 22 in the ACS record checked on 12 November 2026.** Babar Azam follows with 13 and Abhishek Sharma with 11. Quinton de Kock, Virat Kohli and David Warner share fourth place with ten each. [ACS all-T20 table](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Twenty20/Overall/Players/Most_Centuries_in_Career.html).

This is the wider recognized T20 category, not a list restricted to internationals. The threshold is inclusive: every player on ten is retained, so a tie is not cut off to manufacture a five-name list.

The next group has nine each: Jos Buttler, Faf du Plessis, Rilee Rossouw, James Vince and Sahibzada Farhan. Checking that boundary matters: it establishes why the ten-plus table ends after six names rather than implying that only six players have scored T20 hundreds. [Complete leading ACS rows](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Twenty20/Overall/Players/Most_Centuries_in_Career.html).

Gayle's advantage over Babar is nine hundreds. If Gayle's total stayed fixed, nine more would draw Babar level; ten more would put him ahead. That is arithmetic about the recorded gap, not a prediction about opportunity, career length or the likelihood of another century.

## Why Abhishek is no longer tied on ten

SportsAdda's table explicitly uses figures to 1 October 2026. At that point it lists Abhishek, de Kock, Kohli and Warner on ten each behind Gayle and Babar. The four-way tie belongs to that earlier snapshot, not the later one above. [Dated October table](https://www.sportsadda.asia/cricket/features/most-centuries-t20-cricket/).

On 27 October, Abhishek made 113 against New Zealand in Wellington. The Indian Express identifies it as his eleventh all-T20 hundred, taking him beyond Kohli's ten. The ICC independently identifies the same innings as his fourth **T20I** hundred. Those counts are compatible because they use different competition filters. [Contemporary all-T20 milestone](https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/cricket/abhishek-sharma-35-ball-hundred-ind-vs-nz-kohli-t20-century-record-10938828/lite/), [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/india-race-to-a-historic-chase-to-seal-t20i-series-in-wellington).

| Player group | 1 October snapshot | 12 November checked table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Abhishek Sharma | 10, tied third | 11, third outright |
| De Kock, Kohli and Warner | 10, tied third | 10, tied fourth |

The other three batters did not need to lose a hundred for their rank to change. Another player moved ahead. This is why an old headline calling de Kock joint third can be historically accurate while a later record gives him joint fourth.

## A milestone article is not a permanently current table

The 2 September report on de Kock's CPL century describes him joining Kohli and Warner on ten, behind Gayle and Babar. It supplies a useful dated check of those totals, but its ranking should remain attached to its date. [Contemporary ANI report](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/sports/cpl-quinton-de-kock-levels-with-virat-warner-becomes-third-joint-highest-t20-century-getter/).

Likewise, a match report can establish one hundred without independently establishing an entire career. Cricket Australia's account confirms Warner's unbeaten 110 against Sydney Sixers on 16 January 2026. The career figure in this table comes from the records ledger, not from assuming that a report of a century is also a complete count. [CA's Sydney report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4435560/sixers-v-thunder-bbl15-big-bash-sydney-smash-match-report-highlights-videos-scores-scg-starc-smith-warner-babar).

## Keep league and international totals inside the correct scope

The IPL's 13 May 2026 report calls Kohli's unbeaten 105 against KKR his ninth IPL hundred. Nine IPL hundreds and ten all-T20 hundreds are not competing answers. One is a league subset of the wider total. [Official IPL report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-57-rcb-vs-kkr-match-report).

Do not add a player's IPL or T20I count on top of an already inclusive T20 total. That would count the same performances twice. For the international-only leaders, use the separate T20I-century reference; for the all-format international record, use the Test/ODI/T20I combined guide.

Recognition also matters. An exhibition described informally as T20 does not automatically belong in the official career aggregate. Use the match's statistical classification, not merely its advertised length or the presence of famous players.

## What this record measures

A hundred count does not supply an innings denominator, a scoring-speed measure or a probability of repeating the achievement. This table therefore does not rank century frequency or predict who will reach Gayle first. Comparing those questions would require compatible innings and opportunity data.

An update should inspect the full qualifying group and players immediately below it. A new tenth hundred adds a row; a new eleventh can rearrange a tie without changing the number of qualifiers. Preserve the cutoff and shared ranks whenever the table is reused.
