## The Hundred 2026 official fantasy game explained

The official Hundred Fantasy Game in 2026 combined the men’s and women’s competitions in one squad. Its central distinction was the block: you managed a squad for a group of fixtures, rather than treating every match as a fresh contest. This guide describes that completed edition, not rules for Dream11 or a future Hundred season. The [official seasonal rulebook](https://www.thehundred.com/fantasy-rules) explicitly identifies the 2026 game.

## The squad was larger than the starting team

The [official selection FAQ](https://faqs.thehundred.com/hc/en-us/articles/6140112440093-How-do-I-select-my-Fantasy-team) required 14 players: seven men and seven women. Eleven were starters and three were ordered substitutes. The full squad had a 100-credit ceiling, with no overseas-player limit.

| Squad role | Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Batters | 3 | 6 |
| All-rounders | 1 | 4 |
| Wicketkeepers | 1 | 4 |
| Bowlers | 3 | 6 |

These are squad constraints, not a demand to reproduce the seven-and-seven split inside an eleven-player starting team. A hypothetical 14-player roster with four batters, three all-rounders, two keepers and five bowlers passes the role-count check. It still needs seven men, seven women, affordable credits and the applicable team limits. Passing one test does not establish that a roster is valid overall.

The [2026 rulebook](https://www.thehundred.com/fantasy-rules) specifies a maximum of three players per team for the first seven blocks. Do not automatically carry that qualified wording into a claim about the finals block.

## The eight block deadlines

The [official seasonal rules](https://www.thehundred.com/fantasy-rules) confirm eight blocks, with unlimited squad changes for each new block. The detailed rulebook gives these historical deadlines, all in British Summer Time.

| Block | 2026 deadline |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Tuesday 21 July, 14:45 BST |
| 2 | Friday 24 July, 15:00 BST |
| 3 | Monday 27 July, 15:00 BST |
| 4 | Friday 31 July, 15:00 BST |
| 5 | Monday 3 August, 15:00 BST |
| 6 | Friday 7 August, 15:00 BST |
| 7 | Monday 10 August, 15:00 BST |
| 8 | Friday 14 August, 14:15 BST |

The important clock was the scheduled opening match time. A delay to that match did not extend the deadline. For example, if Block 2’s opening match were delayed until 15:30, an attempted change at 15:10 would still fall after the published 15:00 lock. The delay is hypothetical; the deadline is the actual 2026 rule.

## Substitutes replaced absence across the block

The bench order was meaningful. The rules place substitutes in positions one through three and use that order when a starter does not compete within the block. Missing the opening fixture is not, by itself, the stated trigger if that player later competes in the same block.

Captain and vice-captain multipliers were 2× and 1.5× respectively. Crucially, a substitute replacing either absent leader did not inherit that multiplier. [Official selection and captain rules](https://faqs.thehundred.com/hc/en-us/articles/6140112440093-How-do-I-select-my-Fantasy-team).

Consider a simple hypothetical block with only one absent starter: your captain, Player A. Substitute 1, Player B, plays and replaces A, earning 30 ordinary fantasy points. B contributes 30, not 60. If your selected vice-captain separately earns 20 ordinary points, their 1.5× contribution is 30. Those two slots contribute 60 altogether. The example assumes no other substitution complications and makes no forecast about real players.

## Read the rules as a management system

Three separate checks matter: whether the original squad met its composition rules, whether edits were saved before the block lock, and whether a replacement inherited only the playing slot rather than the captain’s bonus. A valid roster can still be locked too late; a successful substitution can still lose the multiplier.

The published substitution wording does not explain every case, such as several unavailable bench players or competing role constraints after multiple replacements. Do not fill those gaps with another platform’s rules. For a disputed historical result, preserve the saved lineup, bench order, block fixtures and points breakdown before asking the game operator to explain it.

The dated deadlines above make this an archive guide, not an invitation to enter a current game. A navigation button advertising 2027 registration is not evidence that the old squad and substitution rules apply to 2027.
