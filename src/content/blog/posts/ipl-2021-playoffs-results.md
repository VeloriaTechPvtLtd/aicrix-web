Chennai Super Kings won IPL 2021 by beating Kolkata Knight Riders in Dubai on 15 October. Their routes to the final were different: Chennai won Qualifier 1, while Kolkata had to survive both the Eliminator and Qualifier 2.

## The completed playoff results

## Why Delhi played twice

Losing Qualifier 1 did not eliminate Delhi. Chennai progressed directly to the final; Delhi received another opportunity against the Eliminator winner. RCB had no equivalent second chance after their defeat to Kolkata.

KKR’s second chase nearly unravelled. From 123/1, they lost wickets rapidly and eventually needed six from the final two balls. Rahul Tripathi hit the first of those for six, ending Delhi’s tournament. [Qualifier 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/vivo-ipl-2021-qualifier-2-kkr-vs-dc-match-report)

## The final changed after halfway

CSK were 80/1 after ten overs; KKR later reached 88/0 at the same stage of their reply. Yet CSK finished at 192/3 and KKR at 165/9. [Final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/vivo-ipl-2021-final-csk-vs-kkr-match-report)

The second halves therefore produced **112 runs for Chennai and 77 for Kolkata**. Chennai’s 35-run advantage over that period overcame the eight-run halfway deficit to create the 27-run margin.

That comparison explains why the strong KKR opening did not settle the contest. It does not attribute the result to one delivery or treat the two innings as identical conditions. The official result remains a run-margin win for Chennai, not a conclusion drawn from the halfway scores.
