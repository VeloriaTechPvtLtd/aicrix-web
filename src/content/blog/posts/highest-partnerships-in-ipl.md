Virat Kohli and AB de Villiers’ **229-run stand for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Gujarat Lions on 14 May 2016** remains the highest IPL partnership through the completed 2026 season. The closest challenge in 2026 was KL Rahul and Nitish Rana’s 220 for Delhi Capitals against Punjab Kings. Both were second-wicket partnerships. [IPL record table through 2026](https://www.crictracker.com/highest-partnerships-in-ipl-history/)

This completed-season record was rechecked on **14 November 2026**.

A partnership measures the team runs added while two batters are together. It is not the sum of their complete innings, and the largest stand is not necessarily the opening stand.

## The five IPL stands of at least 210 runs

An asterisk means the partnership was unbroken when the innings ended. Equal run totals share a rank.

| Rank | Batters and team | Stand | Wicket | Opponent and date |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Virat Kohli and AB de Villiers, RCB | 229 | Second | Gujarat Lions, 14 May 2016 |
| 2 | KL Rahul and Nitish Rana, DC | 220 | Second | Punjab Kings, 25 April 2026 |
| 3 | Virat Kohli and AB de Villiers, RCB | 215* | Second | Mumbai Indians, 10 May 2015 |
| Joint 4 | Quinton de Kock and KL Rahul, LSG | 210* | Opening | KKR, 18 May 2022 |
| Joint 4 | Shubman Gill and Sai Sudharsan, GT | 210 | Opening | CSK, 10 May 2024 |

The next stand is 206, so these five make up the complete 210-or-more group through 2026. [Completed-season partnership table](https://www.crictracker.com/highest-partnerships-in-ipl-history/)

## How the 229 record was built

At Bengaluru, Kohli finished with 109 and de Villiers with 129 not out. Their partnership helped RCB reach 248/3; Gujarat Lions were dismissed for 104, a 144-run defeat. De Villiers was Player of the Match. [Official 2016 match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/match-report-m44-rcb-vs-gl)

Adding the two individual scores gives 238, not 229. Kohli had already scored before de Villiers joined him, while a partnership also includes extras during that shared period. To reconstruct a stand, use its start and end on the scorecard, not the final batting totals.

That distinction becomes particularly useful for an opener batting through an innings with several partners. His hundred may contribute to more than one stand; it cannot be assigned in full to each one.

## Rahul and Rana produced the leading stand of 2026

Delhi’s 220-run partnership on 25 April was nine runs short of the overall record and five above Kohli and de Villiers’ 2015 stand. Rahul made 152 not out and Rana 91 as DC reached 264/2 at the Arun Jaitley Stadium. Punjab nevertheless won by six wickets, finishing on 265/4 with seven balls remaining. [Official 2026 match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-35-dc-v-pbks-match-report)

The stand supplied **83.3% of Delhi’s total**, calculated as 220 ÷ 264 × 100. That is the partnership’s share of team runs, not either batter’s personal contribution. It also shows why a batting record and a match result need separate labels: a huge stand did not secure this result.

## The opening record is shared at 210

De Kock and Rahul carried LSG through an entire innings without losing a wicket in 2022. Their individual scores were 140 not out and 68 not out, leaving two extras in the 210/0 total. LSG won by only two runs. [Official 2022 match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2022-match-66-kkr-vs-lsg-match-report)

Gill and Sudharsan equalled the opening mark in Ahmedabad in 2024, but their stand ended before GT’s innings did. Gujarat finished on 231/3 and won by 35 runs. The shared record is therefore **210 runs**, not a requirement that both partnerships have the same unbeaten status. [Official 2024 match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2024-match-59-gt-vs-csk-match-report)

## A record stand is not a forecast

Kohli and de Villiers’ 215 not out at Wankhede in 2015 is a separate partnership from their 2016 record, even though the same two batters appear in both rows. RCB made 235/1 and won that earlier match by 39 runs. [Official 2015 match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/report-m46-mi-vs-rcb)

Use these records to answer which pair added the most in one innings. For a future match, batting order, expected time together and current conditions matter more than simply selecting the two names at the top of a historical list.
