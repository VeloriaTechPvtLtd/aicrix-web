AB de Villiers’ **31-ball century** leads the men’s ODI fastest-hundreds table maintained by the Association of Cricket Statisticians and Historians. He reached the milestone against West Indies at the Wanderers in Johannesburg on **18 January 2015**, then finished with 149 from 44 balls. [ACS record table](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/ODI/Overall/Batting/Fastest_Hundreds_by_Balls.html), [Guinness ODI entry](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/650709-fastest-one-day-international-hundred)

## The leading entries in the checked table

| Batter | Team and opponent | Balls to reach 100 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| AB de Villiers | South Africa vs West Indies | 31 |
| Corey Anderson | New Zealand vs West Indies | 36 |
| Shahid Afridi | Pakistan vs Sri Lanka | 37 |

This is the men’s ODI category. The cited live table was checked on 14 November 2026; that check is not an independent audit of every ODI or a guarantee against later changes.

## Thirty-one balls is not the full innings

The milestone counts deliveries faced up to the century. The final batting line counts the complete innings. De Villiers’ 44-ball 149 therefore does not contradict the 31-ball record.

His full-innings strike rate was 149 ÷ 44 × 100 = **338.64**, rounded to two decimal places. Nine fours and 16 sixes supplied 132 boundary runs, approximately **88.6%** of his final score. The other 17 runs came outside those boundaries.

## What happened in the match

South Africa’s opening pair had added 247 before Rilee Rossouw was dismissed for 128. De Villiers arrived with 11.3 overs remaining; Hashim Amla finished unbeaten on 153 as the team reached 439 for two and won by 148 runs. [ICC contemporary match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/de-villiers-31-ball-ton-lights-up-wanderers)

That context describes this innings, not a general claim that every batter arriving late can reproduce its scoring rate.

## Keep the record category attached

A fastest-century comparison needs three things: balls to the milestone, the competition’s statistical classification, and the coverage date of the record source. A domestic one-day performance is not automatically an ODI record, and a T20 hundred belongs to another format.

Likewise, this innings ended at 149. It cannot also supply a milestone for reaching 150. Read the exact achievement rather than merging different innings by the same player.
