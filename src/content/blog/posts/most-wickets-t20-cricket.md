## What counts as T20 cricket

T20 internationals are part of the broader recognized T20 record. Domestic and franchise competitions contribute too. Adding a player's T20I wickets to his overall T20 total would count the international wickets twice.

The classification is not determined solely by an innings containing twenty six-ball overs. The ECB's rules effective **24 March 2026** list the Men's Hundred under List A Twenty20 matches, alongside the men's T20 Blast. [ECB classification of official cricket](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2026/03/24/96b19af5-9737-4621-b55a-a3319c04a467/Classification-of-Official-Cricket-2026.pdf).

The ACS also treats the Hundred as a variant of List A T20 cricket, while recommending that it retain a separate tournament category. A Hundred-only statistic and an overall T20 career statistic therefore answer different questions even when the same performances contribute to both. [ACS guidance on the Hundred](https://acscricket.com/wp-content/uploads/The-Hundred-ACS-Guidance.pdf).

That is why a reader should check the record category before comparing two totals. ODI, Test and T10 wickets do not belong in this table.

## Rashid reached the overall and international records separately

Rashid passed Bravo's overall record in the SA20 qualifier between MI Cape Town and Paarl Royals on **4 February 2025**. He dismissed Dunith Wellalage to move beyond Bravo's 631 and finished the match with 633 career wickets. [Contemporary Cricbuzz report](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-news/133332/the-incredible-numbers-from-rashids-journey-to-t20-summit).

He became the first bowler to reach 700 recognized T20 wickets on **16 February 2026**, against UAE in the men's T20 World Cup. Muhammad Arfan was dismissed hit wicket for the milestone; Rashid finished that innings with 1/24. [Wisden milestone report](https://www.wisden.com/series/icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2026/cricket-news/rashid-khan-becomes-first-to-700-t20-wickets-with-bizarre-career-first).

The international record was a separate achievement. An ICC report published on 2 September 2025 recorded him moving past Tim Southee to 165 T20I wickets against UAE. That historical 165 is not an alternative current overall total, and it should not be added to 743. [ICC T20I record report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/rashid-khan-claims-t20i-wicket-record-in-afghanistan-victory).

## A career total is not a forecast for the next match

Shakib's team profile illustrates the importance of scope: it lists 525 wickets under all T20 matches, separately from 149 in T20 internationals and from United Tigers' T10 statistics. A zero in a new team's season panel would not erase the player's career record. [United Tigers player profile](https://www.unitedtigers.ae/players/shakib-al-hasan).

Likewise, cumulative wickets do not reveal how many overs a bowler will receive in an upcoming game. For a team-selection decision, recent participation, bowling allocation and the opposition matter alongside career experience. The record table answers who has taken the most wickets, not who is guaranteed to take the next one.

Use the overall table for the career record, a T20I-only table for international comparisons, and the relevant competition's final statistics for a single season. Keep those views linked rather than combining their overlapping totals.
