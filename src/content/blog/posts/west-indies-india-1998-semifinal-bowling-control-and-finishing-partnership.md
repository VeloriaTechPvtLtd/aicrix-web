West Indies beat India by six wickets at Dhaka on 31 October 1998. India made 242/6; West Indies reached 245/4 in 47 overs. Mervyn Dillon was player of the match. [Contemporary scorecard](https://i.imgci.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/1998-99/OD_TOURNEYS/WIC/SCORECARDS/IND_WI_WIC_ODI-SF2_31OCT1998.html)

## Different bowling contributions

Dillon took 3/38. Reon King, on ODI debut, bowled ten overs for 26 without a wicket. India were 21/2 before Sourav Ganguly’s 83 and Robin Singh’s unbeaten 73 helped repair the innings. [Bowling and batting record](https://i.imgci.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/1998-99/OD_TOURNEYS/WIC/SCORECARDS/IND_WI_WIC_ODI-SF2_31OCT1998.html)

King’s economy rate was 2.60, while India’s overall scoring rate was 4.84. His spell occupied one fifth of the available overs. A wicketless return can therefore still represent substantial restriction; it should not disappear from an explanation focused only on Dillon’s dismissals.

## India found a late partnership

Robin Singh and Nayan Mongia added 83 for the sixth wicket, moving the total from 153 to 236. [Fall of wickets](https://i.imgci.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/1998-99/OD_TOURNEYS/WIC/SCORECARDS/IND_WI_WIC_ODI-SF2_31OCT1998.html)

That stand supplied approximately 34.3% of India’s total. It restored scoring depth after the earlier wickets, but it did not erase the restrictive overs already delivered.

## West Indies kept their finishing pair together

Shivnarine Chanderpaul made 74. Brian Lara finished on 60 not out and Keith Arthurton on 40 not out, adding 89 after West Indies reached 156/4. Tendulkar took 2/29 from ten overs. [Chase record](https://i.imgci.com/link_to_database/ARCHIVE/1998-99/OD_TOURNEYS/WIC/SCORECARDS/IND_WI_WIC_ODI-SF2_31OCT1998.html)

At 156, West Indies needed 87 to reach the winning target of 243; the completed stand reached 89 as the final total passed that target. The partnership’s value was not just its size. By avoiding another dismissal, it let two established batters finish the remaining task. The six-wicket margin records that successful preservation of batting resources.
