## A completed international career

Alyssa Healy finished her Australia career with **7,333 international runs from 299 matches**. Her final international appearance was the Perth Test against India, which ended on **8 March 2026**. She scored 13 in Australia’s first innings and did not bat in the successful second-innings chase, as [Cricket Australia’s farewell report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4464567/alyssa-healy-farewell-final-test-australia-india-women-2026-waca-ground-legacy-era-new-generation) records.

That endpoint matters when reading her statistics. A retirement announcement made before the India series cannot supply the final career totals. Nor should a later domestic appearance be added to her international ledger: Tests, ODIs and T20Is are the three categories used here.

| Tests | 11 | 17 | 502 | 0 | 3 |

The run columns reconcile to 502 + 3,777 + 3,054 = **7,333**; the appearances add to **299**. The nine centuries are eight in ODIs and one in T20Is, not nine ODI hundreds. These totals exclude the WPL, WBBL and domestic one-day cricket.

The 17 Test innings also explain why matches and opportunities cannot be interchanged. A Test can provide two batting innings, while a player can appear without batting in a particular innings. Healy’s final match is a concrete example: she played, but the short second-innings chase never required her.

## The last ODI series was not three identical innings

Healy’s final three ODI innings against India show why a series total needs its underlying scores. She made a measured fifty in Brisbane, a single-figure score in the next game and then a much faster century in her farewell ODI.

| Played date in 2026 | Venue | Runs | Balls | Source |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 24 February | Brisbane | 50 | 70 | [First ODI scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/117047/ausw-vs-indw-1st-odi-india-women-tour-of-australia-2026) |
| 27 February | Hobart | 6 | 10 | [Second ODI scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/117056/ausw-vs-indw-2nd-odi-india-women-tour-of-australia-2026) |
| 1 March | Hobart | 158 | 98 | [Cricket Australia match report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4459801) |

Together those innings produced **214 runs from 178 balls**, a combined strike rate of **120.22**. The calculation is 214 ÷ 178 × 100, not an average of the three individual strike rates. Her final 158 supplied **73.83%** of the series runs; the first two innings together supplied 56.

This concentration is informative without being predictive. The series aggregate describes a strong overall return, but it does not mean that every innings proceeded at that pace or produced a similar score. The last knock had a disproportionate effect on both the total and the aggregate scoring rate.

The 158 was her second-highest ODI score, behind the **170 against England in the 2022 World Cup final**, according to the same official match report. It was also her eighth ODI hundred. The farewell innings therefore belongs in her career context, rather than replacing the longer record with one memorable afternoon.

## A wicketkeeper profile does not make every catch a keeping dismissal

Healy’s established role was wicketkeeper-batter, but the fielding role attached to a particular match still matters. Before the final India series, [Cricket Australia reported that Beth Mooney was expected to retain the gloves](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4455409/alyssa-healy-captaincy-wicketkeeping-australia-india-odis-multi-format-series-brisbane-beth-mooney-selection-alana-king). The first two ODI scorecards identify Mooney as wicketkeeper.

The [final Test report](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4464484) describes Healy catching Kashvee Gautam **at slip** off Alana King. That is a fielding catch, not a wicketkeeping catch merely because Healy’s player profile calls her a keeper. A career comparison must preserve the role filter, and run-out involvement should not be silently added to catches and stumpings.

For Healy, the useful summary is therefore a reconciled batting record plus clearly identified match roles. Her final-series batting numbers and that slip catch are observable facts; a blended “dismissals” total without a defined role would answer a different question.

## Why the final match must be included

A pre-Test tribute recorded ten Tests and 489 runs. The completed career table has eleven Tests and 502 runs: the extra appearance and 13 runs are exactly the contribution documented in the farewell report. That update should be made to the Test row and the international sum together. It should not be made to her ODI figures, which had already closed with the Hobart century.

Keeping those endpoints separate prevents a simple but consequential mistake: using an earlier career headline alongside a final-series table. The profile here is a record of completed appearances, not a statement about availability for a future international fixture. Domestic cricket remains a separate category regardless of when it is played.
