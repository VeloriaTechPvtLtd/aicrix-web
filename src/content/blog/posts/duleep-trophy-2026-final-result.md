**East Zone won the 2026 Duleep Trophy, but the final itself was drawn.** Their title was decided by a 372-run first-innings lead over South Zone in the match played from 6 to 10 September at MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai. Calling it a 372-run match victory would confuse the championship decision with an outright result. [BCCI match record](https://www.bcci.tv/matches/f126c6e3-0a51-4194-aded-8445fbe27e35/east-zone-vs-south-zone/scorecard), [official title report](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/commanding-east-zone-lift-third-duleep-trophy-title-2026).

## Kishan made the first innings the decisive one

After choosing to bat, East Zone began with a century opening stand between Abhimanyu Easwaran and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi. Ishan Kishan then made 270 from 334 balls, while Kumar Kushagra added a century. South Zone's Tripurana Vijay took four wickets, but the innings still reached 708. [BCCI report](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/commanding-east-zone-lift-third-duleep-trophy-title-2026).

Kishan's innings accounted for **38.14% of the team total**, calculated as 270 ÷ 708 × 100. The denominator includes extras, so this is his share of East Zone's scoreboard total, not his share of runs credited exclusively to batters. His contribution was enormous without being the whole innings.

## South Zone could not erase the first innings deficit

Tilak Varma made 99 and Devdutt Padikkal 62, but South Zone were dismissed for 336. Mukesh Kumar's 3/62 led East Zone's wicket-taking returns. The subtraction is straightforward: **708 − 336 = 372**. [Official match report](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/commanding-east-zone-lift-third-duleep-trophy-title-2026).

That 372 was a lead after each side's first innings. It was not a limited-overs target, nor an automatic declaration that the match had finished. East Zone still batted again. Keeping the innings label beside the number makes the result intelligible even to someone arriving from a search for “who won the Duleep Trophy final.”

## East Zone added another century before the draw

Kounain Quraishi finished unbeaten on 116 in the second innings. His seventh-wicket partnership with Anukul Roy added 150, helping East Zone extend an already substantial advantage. The teams then ended the contest with East Zone at 388/7. [PTI report via Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/cricket/news/east-zone-end-13-year-drought-beat-south-zone-to-win-duleep-trophy-2026-126091000639_1.html).

Adding that innings to the first-innings advantage gives **372 + 388 = 760 runs ahead**. It does not turn the result into a 760-run win. South Zone did not play a second innings, so there is no completed chase or second-innings deficit to describe as the winning margin. The useful headline remains “title on first-innings lead after a draw.”

## Match runs and innings runs tell different stories

Kishan followed his 270 with 80, giving him **350 runs across two innings**. The full scorecard records 334 and 98 balls for those innings, a combined 432 deliveries. Those totals describe his performance over the match; they should not appear as a single innings of 350. [Complete scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/163061/ezone-vs-tbc-final-duleep-trophy-2026).

Kishan was Player of the Match, while Tilak Varma received the tournament's Player of the Series award. Different award scopes can produce different recipients without contradiction. [BCCI awards account](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/commanding-east-zone-lift-third-duleep-trophy-title-2026).
