UAE beat New Zealand by seven wickets in the second T20I at Dubai on 19 August 2023. New Zealand made 142/8; UAE reached 144/3 in 15.4 overs, levelling the series at 1–1. [Full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/66883/uae-vs-nz-2nd-t20i-new-zealand-tour-of-united-arab-emirates-2023)

## First, restrict the target

Aayan Afzal Khan returned 3/20 and Muhammad Jawadullah 2/16, each from four overs. Together their eight overs produced five wickets for 36 runs, an economy rate of **4.5 runs per over**. Mark Chapman’s 63 from 46 balls gave New Zealand something to defend. [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/66883/uae-vs-nz-2nd-t20i-new-zealand-tour-of-united-arab-emirates-2023)

Aayan removed Mitchell Santner and Dane Cleaver from consecutive deliveries. That early interruption mattered because Chapman’s recovery did not turn into a large total. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/uae-script-history-with-series-levelling-win-over-new-zealand)

## Waseem changed pace

UAE were 44/2 after the powerplay. Muhammad Waseem had initially made 16 from 13 balls; he eventually finished on 55 from 29. The difference was 39 runs from his next 16 balls, not an innings played at one constant speed. [ICC report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/uae-script-history-with-series-levelling-win-over-new-zealand), [Batting card](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/66883/uae-vs-nz-2nd-t20i-new-zealand-tour-of-united-arab-emirates-2023)

His dismissal left UAE at 96/3 after 11 overs. With a target of 143, the remaining requirement was 47 from 54 legal balls. That is a much more informative description of the chase than simply noting that the captain was out.

## Finish without another wicket

Asif Khan’s unbeaten 48 from 29 balls and Basil Hameed’s unbeaten 12 from 12 completed a 48-run stand. UAE finished one run beyond the target because the winning boundary is credited in full. [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/66883/uae-vs-nz-2nd-t20i-new-zealand-tour-of-united-arab-emirates-2023)

The notation 15.4 means 94 legal balls, not 15.4 decimal overs. Against a 120-ball allocation, that leaves **26 balls unused**. The winning margin therefore captures two separate advantages: seven wickets still available and more than four overs remaining.
