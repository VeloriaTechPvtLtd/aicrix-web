Royal Challengers Bangalore and Mumbai Indians tied on 201 in Dubai on 28 September 2020. RCB then won the Super Over, scoring 11/0 after Mumbai’s 7/1. Virat Kohli hit the winning four off Jasprit Bumrah, but did not score all eleven runs himself. [Official IPL report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/dream11-ipl-2020-match-10-rcb-vs-mi-match-report)

## The six deliveries

Mumbai’s contemporary live account records RCB’s chase of eight as follows:

| Ball | Striker | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AB de Villiers | One run |
| 2 | Virat Kohli | One run |
| 3 | AB de Villiers | Dot; caught decision overturned on review |
| 4 | AB de Villiers | Four |
| 5 | AB de Villiers | One run |
| 6 | Virat Kohli | Four |

[MI’s delivery-by-delivery account](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/live-updates-mi-take-on-rcb-in-dubai)

Kohli therefore made **five from two balls** in the tie-break; de Villiers made **six from four**. Their contributions sum to eleven. RCB reached seven after the fifth delivery and needed one; the final boundary took the total to eleven rather than stopping the score at eight.

## Why a Super Over was needed

Mumbai were 122/4 after 16 overs and needed 80 from the last 24 deliveries to win. They added 79. Ishan Kishan fell for 99, and Kieron Pollard’s last-ball four brought the innings level at 201/5 against RCB’s 201/3. [IPL match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/dream11-ipl-2020-match-10-rcb-vs-mi-match-report)

The tie was resolved by the separate one-over contest, not by comparing the wickets lost in the original innings. Mumbai’s Super Over included a final-ball bye, another reason to distinguish team runs from runs credited to individual batters. [MI account](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/live-updates-mi-take-on-rcb-in-dubai)

## Keep the two encounters separate

A Kohli–Bumrah analysis should label these two deliveries as Super Over events. Combining them silently with regulation-innings figures changes the question being answered. Kohli’s dismissal in the main innings was caught Rohit Sharma, bowled Rahul Chahar, not a Bumrah wicket. [Official report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/dream11-ipl-2020-match-10-rcb-vs-mi-match-report)

This reconstruction describes that 2020 match; it is not a claim that every later competition uses identical tie-break conditions.
