## Choose the rules before the app

Daily and season-long fantasy cricket differ in how long a selection matters. In a match-based game, the team is selected for a particular fixture. In a season-long game, a squad or its management decisions persist across a tournament. But duration is only one choice: it does not tell you whether players are exclusive, transfers are allowed or standings use total points.

A friends-only contest can use either duration. “Private” describes the audience, not the player-selection system. Dream11’s [India App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/dream11-watch-play-compete/id1077286074), checked on 14 November 2026, explicitly advertises free private contests with friends. That does not establish a season-long exclusive-ownership league.

## Ask what persists between matches

Compare these three fictional league designs before deciding which suits your group.

| Design | What carries forward | Main decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match-by-match | Only results or a separate group leaderboard, if specified | Submit a selection for each fixture |
| Season squad with changes | Squad plus permitted transfers | Maintain a roster over time |
| Fixed tournament squad | Original selection | Choose once before the tournament starts |

These are possible designs, not universal app presets. Doosra’s [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.doosra.fantasyleague) illustrates why names need checking: it describes a season-long mode with transfers, a single-match mode and a “Draft” mode with no edits after the first match. That last label alone does not prove managers take alternating turns or exclusively own their players.

## Exclusive ownership changes the selection problem

In a hypothetical shared-pool league, managers A and B may both select Player X if their individual teams meet the rules. In an exclusive league, once A acquires X, B must choose someone else or later complete an allowed trade.

That is a separate choice from league length. A tournament-long competition can still allow overlapping squads. Ask explicitly whether the same real cricketer can appear on more than one manager’s roster; do not infer the answer from “private,” “draft” or “season.”

## Snake order versus a virtual auction

In a snake draft, the pick order reverses each round. A virtual-budget auction allocates players through bids within its stated constraints. The following invented four-manager sequence illustrates the reversing order, not any provider’s live settings.

| Draft round | Pick order | Overall pick numbers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A, B, C, D | 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| 2 | D, C, B, A | 5, 6, 7, 8 |
| 3 | A, B, C, D | 9, 10, 11, 12 |

D receives consecutive picks 4 and 5; A receives 8 and 9. Across two rounds, A’s positions sum to 9, as do B’s, C’s and D’s. Equal position sums do not prove equal player value. They simply check that the order has been reversed correctly.

In a virtual-budget auction, selection instead depends on the bidding process and roster rules. Before starting, settle nomination order, minimum bids, timer expiry, ties and what happens when a manager disconnects. Do not substitute real franchise auction prices for the rules of a social fantasy game.

## Decide how the season winner is determined

Total-points and head-to-head standings can produce different leaders. Consider two fictional managers over three rounds. A scores 100, 100 and 0; B scores 90, 90 and 90. A wins their first two direct comparisons, but B leads total points 270 to 200.

Neither result is an arithmetic mistake. One design rewards round victories; the other aggregates every point. Agree on the standings method, tie-breaks and any finals before play. A table labelled only “points” can conceal whether it means fantasy scores or league points awarded for wins.

## Write a short league agreement

Record the player pool, ownership rule, squad size and scoring starters. Add lock times, transfer or waiver rules, bench treatment, postponed-match handling and correction deadlines. Decide who may change settings and whether any scoring change applies only prospectively.

If custom scoring is important, test a small fictional scorecard before the first real fixture. A platform advertising private contests may not expose the scoring controls your group needs. Verify the actual format, region, costs and settings rather than relying on a comparison page’s description of a rival.
