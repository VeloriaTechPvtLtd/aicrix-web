Sachin Tendulkar finished his international career with **34,357 runs and 100 centuries in 664 matches**. That combines 200 Tests, 463 One-Day Internationals and one Twenty20 International. [BCCI’s completed-career summary, page 20](https://documents.bcci.tv/documents/bcci/documents/1638361890_BCCI%20Annual%20Report%202013-14.pdf#page=20).

These are international statistics. His IPL appearances and other domestic or exhibition matches are outside this total.

## Test centuries and double centuries

His **51 Test centuries and 68 fifties** amount to 119 innings of at least 50 runs. [Association of Cricket Statisticians and Historians](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Test/Overall/Batting/Most_Career_Runs_for_India.html).

Six of those Test innings reached 200:

- 217 against New Zealand in Ahmedabad, 1999.

- 201* against Zimbabwe in Nagpur, 2000.

- 241* against Australia in Sydney, 2004.

- 248* against Bangladesh in Dhaka, 2004.

- 203 against Sri Lanka in Colombo, 2010.

- 214 against Australia in Bangalore, 2010.

These six scores are already included in his 51 Test centuries. Adding double centuries to the century total would count the same innings twice. [BCCI’s century-by-century list](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/100-and-counting-sachins-tons-at-a-glance).

His final Test was against West Indies at Mumbai’s Wankhede Stadium on **14–16 November 2013**. He scored 74 in his last Test innings. [BCCI’s season report, pages 19 and 22](https://documents.bcci.tv/documents/bcci/documents/1638361890_BCCI%20Annual%20Report%202013-14.pdf#page=19).

## The ODI double hundred and hundredth century

His highest ODI score was **200 not out against South Africa at Gwalior on 24 February 2010**. [BCCI’s innings list](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/100-and-counting-sachins-tons-at-a-glance). He faced 147 balls and became the first man to score an ODI double century. [ICC’s account of the Gwalior innings](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/sachin-tendulkar-top-odi-innings).

His 100th international century was **114 against Bangladesh at Mirpur on 16 March 2012**. [BCCI’s milestone record](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/100-and-counting-sachins-tons-at-a-glance). The career total combines 51 Test hundreds and 49 ODI hundreds; it does not mean 100 hundreds in either format.

## One T20I is not one Twenty20 match

Tendulkar’s only T20 International was against South Africa at the Wanderers in Johannesburg on **1 December 2006**. He made 10 from 12 balls, was bowled by Charl Langeveldt and took 1/12, dismissing Justin Kemp lbw. [Match scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/3893/rsa-vs-ind-only-t20i-india-in-south-africa-t20i-match-2006), [career timeline](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/25/sachin-tendulkar).

The “I” matters. T20I statistics cover international matches, while a broader Twenty20 record can include domestic competitions. An IPL record covers that league. A comparison must use the same category for both players.

## Why the Test average appears as 53.78 or 53.79

The [ACS record](https://stats.acscricket.com/Records/Test/Overall/Batting/Most_Career_Runs_for_India.html) displays **53.78**, while [Cricbuzz](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/25/sachin-tendulkar) displays **53.79**. Both list the same 15,921 runs, 329 innings and 33 not outs.

The calculation is:

- Dismissals: 329 − 33 = **296**.

- Runs divided by dismissals: 15,921 ÷ 296 = **53.787162…**

- Keeping two decimal places without rounding gives **53.78**.

- Rounding to the nearest hundredth gives **53.79**.

The table uses 53.78, matching the BCCI and ACS displays. The difference does not require an extra innings or a different run total.

For ODIs, 452 − 41 gives 411 dismissals. Dividing 18,426 by 411 gives 44.832116…, consistent with the displayed 44.83.
