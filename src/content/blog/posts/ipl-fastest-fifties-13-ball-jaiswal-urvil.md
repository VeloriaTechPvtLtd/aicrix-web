Urvil Patel reached fifty in 13 balls for Chennai Super Kings against Lucknow Super Giants on 10 May 2026, matching Yashasvi Jaiswal’s mark for Rajasthan Royals against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2023. This comparison uses the record table published on 10 May 2026, rather than claiming a continuously updated leaderboard. ## Thirteen balls does not describe the whole innings

Jaiswal finished on 98 not out from 47 balls; Urvil made 65 from 23. The shared record describes how quickly each first reached fifty, not the number of balls in the complete innings. [Jaiswal’s innings](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/fastest-50-in-ipl-when-yashasvi-jaiswal-conjured-up-a-storm-at-the-eden-gardens), [Urvil’s innings](https://www.cricket.com.au/news/4501862/chennai-super-kings-beat-lucknow-match-report-ipl-2026-scores-highlights-josh-inglis-urvil-patel-equal-record-fastest-half-century-fifty)

Their final strike rates consequently differ: 98 ÷ 47 × 100 = **208.51**, while 65 ÷ 23 × 100 = **282.61**. Neither calculation replaces the 13-ball milestone.

## Why a record table needs its date

A report calling Jaiswal the sole holder before May 2026 and one calling him joint holder after Urvil’s innings can both be accurate for their publication dates. The useful comparison retains the date, competition and milestone together. It also keeps IPL performances separate from faster innings in other T20 competitions.
