**Gujarat Titans finished first in the IPL 2023 league table with 20 points.** Chennai Super Kings, Lucknow Super Giants and Mumbai Indians occupied the other playoff places. Chennai subsequently won the championship. [Historical standings](https://m.statisticstimes.com/sports/ipl/all-ipl-points-table.php) and [official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-final-csk-vs-gt-match-report).

## Why Chennai and Lucknow had 17 points

CSK and LSG each won eight games, lost five and had one no-result. Their meeting in Lucknow on 3 May was abandoned with LSG on 125/7 after 19.2 overs. The teams received one point apiece. [Official abandoned-match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-match-45-lsg-vs-csk-match-report).

Eight wins contributed 16 points; the no-result supplied the seventeenth. Chennai finished second with an NRR of +0.652, ahead of Lucknow’s +0.284. Their equal points and win totals therefore did not give them the same league position. [Historical standings](https://m.statisticstimes.com/sports/ipl/all-ipl-points-table.php).

## How Mumbai took the last playoff place

Mumbai finished with eight wins and 16 points. Rajasthan and RCB each finished on 14. That two-point advantage placed Mumbai fourth. [Final league standings](https://m.statisticstimes.com/sports/ipl/all-ipl-points-table.php).

Mumbai qualified despite a negative NRR. Rajasthan and RCB had positive NRRs but fewer points. This is a useful reminder to read the points column before comparing run rates: a positive NRR alone did not secure a playoff place.

## The playoff results

The league table determined the playoff pairings. The knockout results then determined the champion.

| Match | Result | What followed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Qualifier 1, 23 May](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-qualifier-1-gt-vs-csk-match-report) | CSK beat GT by 15 runs | Chennai reached the final |
| [Eliminator, 24 May](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-eliminator-lsg-vs-mi-match-report) | MI beat LSG by 81 runs | Mumbai advanced to Qualifier 2 |
| [Qualifier 2, 26 May](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-qualifier-2-gt-vs-mi-match-report) | GT beat MI by 62 runs | Gujarat reached the final |
| [Final, 29 May](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-final-csk-vs-gt-match-report) | CSK beat GT by 5 wickets, DLS method | Chennai won its fifth IPL title |

In the final, Gujarat made 214/4. Rain reduced Chennai’s chase to a target of 171 in 15 overs, and CSK finished on 171/5. The smaller winning total reflects the revised DLS target, not an error in the score. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2023-final-csk-vs-gt-match-report).

Gujarat’s first place in the league and Chennai’s title describe different stages of the same competition. When comparing seasons, keep the league finish separate from the playoff outcome, and keep playoff wins out of a 14-match league table.
