The auction for **IPL 2024 was held on 19 December 2023 at the Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai**. The edition name refers to the season the franchises were preparing for, not the calendar year in which the auction happened.

## Date and advertised start

IPL’s announcement specified a start of 11:30 AM in Dubai, equivalent to 1:00 PM Indian Standard Time. This was the scheduled start, not a claim about the time of the first completed sale. [Official IPL announcement](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-2024-player-auction-list-announced)

The event is easy to confuse with the auction held during calendar year 2024. That was the **IPL 2025 mega auction**, announced for 24–25 November 2024 in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. Dubai in December 2023 and Jeddah in November 2024 belong to different season editions. [IPL 2025 announcement](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2025-player-auction-list-announced)

## Five major purchases in Dubai

| Player | Buying franchise | Winning bid |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Mitchell Starc | Kolkata Knight Riders | ₹24.75 crore |
| Pat Cummins | Sunrisers Hyderabad | ₹20.50 crore |
| Daryl Mitchell | Chennai Super Kings | ₹14 crore |
| Harshal Patel | Punjab Kings | ₹11.75 crore |
| Alzarri Joseph | Royal Challengers Bangalore | ₹11.50 crore |

These selected results are documented in the [ICC’s auction-day report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/starc-and-cummins-shatter-records-at-the-ipl-2024-auction). They are not a complete sold-and-unsold register, and the franchises describe those purchases rather than current player contracts.

## What the record bids mean

Cummins briefly became the auction’s most expensive player before Starc’s later sale exceeded his price. The record description belongs to that day, not an automatically updated all-time ranking.

The two bids totalled ₹45.25 crore: ₹24.75 crore plus ₹20.50 crore. That is spending on two auction purchases, not a measure of their subsequent on-field contribution. Keep the date, buying franchise and bid together when comparing historical sales.
