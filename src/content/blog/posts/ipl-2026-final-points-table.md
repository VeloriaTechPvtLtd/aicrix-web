**Royal Challengers Bengaluru finished first in the IPL 2026 league table with 18 points.** Gujarat Titans and Sunrisers Hyderabad also had 18, while Rajasthan Royals took the fourth playoff place with 16. The table below is the completed league-stage record, not a live table for another season. [Official IPL standings with 2026 selected](https://www.iplt20.com/points-table).

## Why RCB were ahead of Gujarat and Hyderabad

The top three had identical win-loss records: nine wins and five defeats. They also had equal points. Their published NRRs separated them:

- RCB: +0.783.

- Gujarat: +0.695.

- Hyderabad: +0.524.

RCB’s advantage over Gujarat in that column was **0.088**, while Gujarat’s advantage over Hyderabad was **0.171**. These differences are in net-run-rate units, not winning percentages or runs added to a scorecard.

The [Cricbuzz season-table explanation](https://m.cricbuzz.com/cricket-series/9241/indian-premier-league-2026/points-table) identifies wins as the first separator when points are equal, followed by NRR when wins are also equal. For these three actual rows, both earlier measures were tied, so the NRR ordering matters.

All three won approximately **64.3% of their league matches**, calculated as nine divided by fourteen. Their identical win percentage did not make their league positions interchangeable.

## Why Rajasthan qualified ahead of Punjab

Punjab’s NRR, +0.309, was higher than Rajasthan’s +0.189. Nevertheless, Rajasthan finished fourth because they had **16 points to Punjab’s 15**.

That is a useful distinction when reading a table: a better NRR does not overtake a team with more points. Rajasthan’s eight wins supplied their 16 points; Punjab had seven wins and a no-result.

Do not turn this final comparison into a claim that one arbitrary extra run or wicket would have changed qualification. A counterfactual needs the relevant match result and the resulting points and NRR, not just the distance between two printed numbers.

## The no-result behind the odd point totals

Punjab and Kolkata each had one no-result. Their 6 April meeting at Eden Gardens was abandoned with KKR on 25/2 after 3.4 overs, and each side received one point. [Official abandoned-match report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-12-kkr-vs-pbks-match-report).

That explains their odd totals:

- Punjab: seven wins contributed 14 points, plus one for the no-result, making **15**.

- Kolkata: six wins contributed 12 points, plus one for the no-result, making **13**.

The same abandoned fixture produces two no-result appearances in the table, one for each team. It is still one match.

Across the ten rows there are **140 team appearances**, equivalent to 70 league fixtures. The records add to 69 wins, 69 losses and two no-result appearances. Those totals reconcile without counting the rain-abandoned game twice.

## League position and championship result

The league table determines who advances; it does not include playoff matches in those 14 appearances. RCB subsequently beat Gujarat by five wickets in the final on 31 May and won the championship. [Official final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-final-rcb-v-gt-match-report).

Keep that result separate from the table calculation. Adding the final win to RCB’s league record would change the sample and make the row inconsistent with the other teams.
