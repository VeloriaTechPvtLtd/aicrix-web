Shivang Kumar’s completed IPL 2026 record contains **13 appearances, nine wickets and 53 runs**. His bowling and batting opportunities were not equal: the ledger lists 13 bowling innings but six batting innings. [IPL statistics](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1455907/shivang-kumar)

Source: [Cricbuzz’s IPL-specific table](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/1455907/shivang-kumar). The headline appearance, run and wicket totals are also corroborated by [MyKhel’s season profile](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/players/shivang-kumar-ipl-p20804/).

His 234 legal balls equal 39 overs. The resulting bowling average is 375 ÷ 9 = **41.67**, economy 375 ÷ 39 = **9.62**, and strike rate 234 ÷ 9 = **26 balls per wicket**.

Thirty-nine overs across 13 bowling innings average three overs each. That does not establish that every individual spell lasted three overs or that future selection guarantees a full allocation.

## Two performances against Punjab

The official 11 April report identifies him as a left-arm wrist-spinner and records 3/33 against Punjab Kings. Punjab nevertheless completed a chase of 220 and won by six wickets. The bowling figures describe his contribution, not a Sunrisers victory. [Official report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-17-pbks-vs-srh-match-report)

On 6 May, he took 2/45 against the same opposition as Sunrisers won by 33 runs. [Official return-fixture report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-49-srh-vs-pbks-match-report)

Those two games supplied five of his nine season wickets, or 55.6%. They also illustrate why a team result should remain attached to an individual performance rather than inferred from it.

## Batting is a six-innings sample

With no not outs in those six innings, his batting average is 53 ÷ 6 = **8.83** and strike rate 53 ÷ 46 × 100 = **115.22**. The seven appearances without batting are not seven ducks. Keep that smaller opportunity count alongside the runs when assessing his first IPL season.
