The distinction between matches and innings matters: an appearance does not always include a chance to bat. Average uses dismissals rather than appearances. Here, 145 innings minus 16 not outs gives 129 dismissals, so 6,744 ÷ 129 = 52.28. Strike rate uses deliveries faced: 6,744 ÷ 7,811 × 100 = 86.34. These rates are rounded to two decimal places from the [published batting inputs](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/player-profile/babar-azam/59429).

The 20 hundreds and 38 fifties are separate scoring bands. Adding them gives 58 innings of at least 50, not 58 centuries. A century is not counted again in the fifties column.

## The 5000 and 6000 run milestones

Babar reached 5,000 ODI runs on 5 May 2023 against New Zealand in Karachi. He needed **97 batting innings**, completed across 99 matches, and broke Hashim Amla’s then-record of 101 innings. The figure 97 is an innings count, not a match count. [Contemporary AFP report](https://supersport.com/cricket/news/b02b8266-23f8-479e-a912-a3e0c3c0bdde/pakistan-s-babar-azam-becomes-fastest-to-5-000-odi-runs).

On 14 February 2025, also against New Zealand in Karachi, he reached 6,000 in **123 innings**, matching Amla’s record at the time. [The Indian Express report](https://indianexpress.com/article/sports/cricket/pak-vs-nz-babar-azam-6000-odi-runs-joint-fastest-hashim-amla-9836528/) identifies this as a shared achievement.

These milestones measure how quickly a batter accumulated career runs. They do not establish a current ICC ranking or predict the next innings.

## What his highest ODI score shows

His 158 against England came at Edgbaston on 13 July 2021. He faced 139 balls, with 14 fours and four sixes. England nevertheless chased Pakistan’s 331/9. [ICC’s match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/vince-century-sees-england-wrap-up-clean-sweep).

Those boundaries contributed 80 runs: 14 × 4 plus 4 × 6. That is 50.63% of his innings, while the overall strike rate was 113.67. Boundary contribution, scoring speed and team result are related observations, but none can substitute for the others.

## How to use these numbers before a match

Start with the format and cutoff. Babar’s ODI record cannot be used as his T20I or PSL record. Then compare a recent window with the career baseline, keeping the opponent and batting role in view. A large career total establishes experience; a short run of scores describes a particular period.

For fantasy analysis, selection and expected batting position come before projecting opportunities. Career average is not a promise of that many runs, and a historical century record is not a guaranteed return in the next fixture.

## Keep later formats outside the ODI calculation

The career page also contains Test and T20I records. Babar’s November Test appearances do not change the eight ODI innings listed here. A recent-form panel can place a Test above the October final simply because it was played later; that is not evidence of an additional ODI. Check the format attached to each entry before extending either the year table or the career totals.

The selected career figures use consistent runs, innings and not-outs. Cricket West Indies displays the average as 52.27, while division of its own 6,744 runs by 129 dismissals rounds to 52.28. This is why retaining the underlying inputs is more useful than comparing isolated rate labels. The article uses rounding to the nearest two decimal places throughout, without treating a hundredth-point display difference as a change in batting performance.
