India and Afghanistan each made 212 in Bengaluru on 17 January 2024. Their first Super Over also finished level, so a second one decided the third T20I. India won that decider and completed a 3–0 series sweep. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/record-ton-for-rohit-sharma-in-strong-comeback-knock)

## Three score comparisons, not one inflated total

| Stage | India | Afghanistan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main innings | 212/4 | 212/6 |
| First Super Over | 16 | 16 |
| Second Super Over | 11/2 | 1/2 |

Ravi Bishnoi took both wickets in Afghanistan’s second Super Over, conceding one run in three deliveries. [Contemporary result and deciders](https://www.skysports.com/cricket/news/13050501/india-overcome-afghanistan-in-double-super-over-drama-to-sweep-t20-series-3-0)

Keep the stages separate when reading the result. Adding the Super Over runs to 212 would create a misleading main-innings score. Likewise, a tied match followed by a winning decider is not a conventional runs-margin victory in the scheduled innings.

## India’s recovery gave the deciders their platform

India had been 22/4 before Rohit Sharma and Rinku Singh added an unbeaten 190 for the fifth wicket. Rohit finished on 121 not out and Rinku on 69 not out. India took 36 from the last over. [ICC recovery report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/record-ton-for-rohit-sharma-in-strong-comeback-knock)

The partnership supplied approximately **89.6%** of India’s total: 190 ÷ 212 × 100. It transformed an early collapse into a formidable target, but the defence still had to succeed. Gulbadin Naib’s unbeaten 55 helped Afghanistan level the scores. [ICC match account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/record-ton-for-rohit-sharma-in-strong-comeback-knock)

The two recovery stories should not obscure the decisive distinction: batting produced equality twice, while Bishnoi’s wickets ended the final contest. This match is best explained as a sequence of separate scoring stages rather than a single combined total.
