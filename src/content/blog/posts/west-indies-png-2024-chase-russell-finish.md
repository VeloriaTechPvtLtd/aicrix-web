West Indies beat Papua New Guinea by five wickets in Guyana on 2 June 2024. PNG made 136/8; West Indies reached 137/5 after 19 overs. The finish was comfortable on the final scorecard, but the last partnership began with a demanding equation. [ICC match report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/west-indies-strike-early-after-putting-papua-new-guinea-into-bat-in-t20-world-cup-2024-clash?sf190040171=1), [Scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/87507/wi-vs-png-2nd-match-group-c-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2024)

## PNG made the target competitive

Sese Bau’s 50 helped PNG recover from early wickets. In reply, West Indies lost wickets through the middle of the innings and were 97/5 with four overs left. [ICC account](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/t20cricketworldcup/news/west-indies-strike-early-after-putting-papua-new-guinea-into-bat-in-t20-world-cup-2024-clash?sf190040171=1)

The requirement was **40 runs from 24 balls**, a rate of ten per over. That is the useful checkpoint: the modest-looking target of 137 did not mean every stage of the chase was straightforward.

## The finishing stand used only three overs

Roston Chase finished on 42 not out from 27 balls and Andre Russell on 15 not out from nine. Their unbroken stand added 40 from 18 balls. [Batting and partnerships](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/87507/wi-vs-png-2nd-match-group-c-icc-mens-t20-world-cup-2024)

That partnership scored at **13.33 runs per over**, above the required ten. It used three of the four remaining overs, leaving six balls unused.

Do not add the final individual scores, 42 and 15, and call that the partnership. Chase had already scored 17 before Russell arrived. During the closing stand, Chase added 25 and Russell 15: **25 + 15 = 40**.

## The margin needs the checkpoint

Five wickets remaining describes the resources still available when West Indies won. It does not describe the pressure at 97/5. The equation, partnership and finishing cushion together show how a difficult late stage became a successful chase without another wicket falling.
