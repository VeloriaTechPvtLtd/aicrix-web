The published IPL Governing Council policy keeps the Impact Player regulation for the 2025–2027 cycle. That is the official starting point as checked on 14 November 2026. It is stronger evidence than a prediction that the rule will disappear because players or commentators dislike it. [IPL's cycle announcement, point 8](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/ipl-governing-council-announces-tata-ipl-player-regulations-2025-27).

This does not mean every administrative detail from an older explainer is automatically the final wording for 2027. Separate the continuing policy from the playing conditions for a particular season.

## Why the date on a rule guide matters

| Source | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official 2025–2027 cycle announcement | The regulation continues across the named cycle | Every detailed match-day procedure |
| A 2023 team FAQ | How that team explained the rule then | An authoritative update for later seasons |

An older summary is evidence of what was explained at that time. Before applying it to a later season, obtain the operative league document and amendments. Preserve both the rule and its effective date.

## Being named is not the same as being used

For match analysis, record three separate states: the starting XI, the named substitution options and the player actually introduced. A probable lineup is a fourth, less certain state. Collapsing them into one list makes a speculative role look confirmed.

An actual 2026 example is MI's opening game against KKR: the official report identifies Suryakumar Yadav as an Impact Player and records his 16 from eight balls. That confirms what happened in that match, not that he or any other player must have the same role next season. [Official Match 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-2-mi-vs-kkr-match-report).

## What to check before a 2027 match

Start with the league's [official documents](https://www.iplt20.com/documents), then check the match's announced XI and substitute list. For detailed questions about an overseas replacement, the moment a substitution can occur or whether a replaced player can return, use the applicable season's Impact Player provisions rather than filling gaps from an old summary.

Keep fantasy eligibility separate. A cricket substitution label is not, by itself, proof of a platform's points or lineup rules. Use the platform's effective rules for the contest concerned.

The defensible answer today is narrow and useful: the published cycle policy includes 2027. For a particular fixture, confirm the season document and actual substitution before turning that policy into a claim about a player's participation.
