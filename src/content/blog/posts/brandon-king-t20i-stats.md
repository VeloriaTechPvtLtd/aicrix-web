Dividing by 87 appearances would answer a different question, runs per match, and would not reproduce the batting average. The strike rate is 2,028 divided by 1,525 and multiplied by 100, or 132.98 after rounding. [Career totals](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-players/brandon-king/stats/)

The gap between matches and innings also matters. A match appearance is not automatically a batting opportunity, and an unbeaten innings is not the same as not batting. Those distinctions are particularly important when comparing an opener with a lower-order player.

## The 85 not out that helped beat India

King's highest T20I innings came against India in Florida on **13 August 2023**. He made 85 not out from 55 deliveries in an eight-wicket victory that secured the five-match series 3–2. His innings contained five fours and six sixes. [Cricket West Indies match report](https://www.windiescricket.com/news/superb-victory-shepherd-and-king-lead-west-indies-to-series-win-over-india/)

Those boundaries supplied 56 of his 85 runs, or 65.88%. He also shared a 107-run second-wicket partnership with Nicholas Pooran. The result is a useful example of King combining a substantial innings with boundary scoring, rather than merely registering a fast short cameo.

West Indies chased India's 165/9 with two overs remaining. The ICC's account was published on 14 August, but the match itself was played on 13 August. [ICC series report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/west-indies-break-six-year-drought-with-india-series-triumph)
