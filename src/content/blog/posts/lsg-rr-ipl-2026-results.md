Rajasthan Royals beat Lucknow Super Giants twice in IPL 2026, but the two scorecards tell different stories. RR defended 159 in Lucknow, then chased 221 in Jaipur. Treating the second game as a replay of the first would hide the most useful comparison.

The Jaipur match was on 19 May. The official report carries a 20 May publication date, which should not replace the fixture date in an archive.

## Lucknow: rescue the innings, then defend it

RR fell to 77/5 before Ravindra Jadeja's unbeaten 43 helped them finish on 159/6. Their final over produced 20 runs. In the chase, LSG slipped to 11/3; Mitchell Marsh made 55, but Jofra Archer's 3/20 helped dismiss the home side for 119. [Official Match 32 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-32-lsg-vs-rr-match-report).

The 40-run margin is 159 minus 119. It is not 41, even though LSG's winning target was 160. That distinction matters when moving between a target display and a result label.

The late acceleration also deserves context: 20 runs in the final over was about 12.6% of RR's total. That shows the size of the contribution, not proof that the game would otherwise have been lost. Removing runs after the event does not reproduce how a different chase would have unfolded.

## Jaipur: a much higher target was not enough

LSG reached 83 without loss in the powerplay and 220/5 at the finish. Marsh made 96 from 57 balls and Josh Inglis 60 from 29. RR answered through Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's 93 from 38 and Dhruv Jurel's unbeaten 53, finishing on 225/3. [Official Match 64 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-64-rr-vs-lsg-match-report).

LSG's first six overs ran at 13.83 runs per over. Their remaining 14 yielded 137 runs at about 9.79. Those are separate phases: a fast powerplay did not mean the same rate continued throughout the innings.

RR's 19.1 overs equal 115 legal balls. Their final scoring rate was therefore 225 × 6 ÷ 115, about 11.74 per over, with five balls unused. Dividing by the decimal 19.1 gives the wrong answer. [May scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/152207/rr-vs-lsg-64th-match-ipl-2026).

## Why the comparison needs more than the winner

RR won once batting first and once chasing. The two first-innings totals differ by 61, but these are not controlled experiments: different grounds, lineups and match situations are involved. They do not establish that 159 is a dependable Ekana par score or that 220 is insufficient every time in Jaipur.
