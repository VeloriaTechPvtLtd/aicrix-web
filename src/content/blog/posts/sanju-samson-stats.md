## ODI average and the dismissal denominator

Samson’s 510 ODI runs came from 14 innings, including five not-outs. His batting average uses nine dismissals:

**510 ÷ (14 − 5) = 56.67.**

Dividing by all 14 innings instead gives 36.43 runs per innings. Both calculations describe something useful, but only the first is the standard batting average. The [dated ODI innings list](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/8271/sanju-samson/all-matches/batting?matchType=2) also includes two matches in which he did not bat.

His maiden ODI century came in the Paarl series decider on 21 December 2023. Samson made 108 from 114 balls, batting at No. 3, as India posted 296/8. [Rajasthan Royals’ century report](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/sa-vs-ind-sanju-samson-slams-maiden-odi-century-in-series-decider); [ICC’s account of the innings](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/england-west-indies-stars-enjoy-t20i-rankings-jumps).

The career average should be read alongside the relatively small sample of nine dismissals, rather than compared with a much longer career without that context.

## T20I centuries and the 2026 World Cup

Samson’s three T20I centuries were all scored in 2024: **111 against Bangladesh, 107 against South Africa and 109 not out against South Africa**. The first two made him the first Indian batter to score centuries in consecutive men’s T20Is. The third came in Johannesburg on 15 November. [ICC consecutive-century report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/sanju-samson-scripts-history-with-second-t20i-hundred-in-a-row); [Johannesburg report](https://www.rajasthanroyals.com/latest-news/sanju-samson-century-4th-t20-india-vs-south-africa-series-2024-win).

At the 2026 T20 World Cup, he scored **321 runs in five innings**, averaged 80.25 and was named Player of the Tournament. His last three innings were 97 not out against West Indies, 89 against England and 89 in the final against New Zealand. The [ICC tournament review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/sanju-samson-adjudged-t20-world-cup-player-of-the-tournament) reports his tournament strike rate as 199.37.

Those World Cup runs are already included in his T20I career total. Adding the tournament figure to the career figure would count them twice.

## IPL batting and appearance counts

Samson’s IPL total of 5,181 runs came from 186 batting innings, with 23 not-outs. The average is **5,181 ÷ 163 = 31.79**. His highest IPL score at this cutoff was 119.

For Chennai Super Kings in 2026, he scored 477 runs in 14 innings, with two centuries, an average of 43.36 and a strike rate of 165.63. The [season table](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/ipl/player-stats/sanju-samson/61837) separates this season from his career totals. His last appearance was the [21 May match against Gujarat Titans](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-match-66-gt-v-csk-match-report).

Readers may encounter a **190-match** career total on Cricbuzz. This table uses the **191 matches** shown by Delhi Capitals and Times of India. Their 2024 records show 16 matches and 15 batting innings, whereas Cricbuzz’s season leaderboard counts 15 matches.

A relevant fixture is Rajasthan versus Kolkata on 19 May 2024. The toss took place and Samson was in the named XI, but rain prevented any delivery. The [official abandonment report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2024-match-70-rr-vs-kkr-match-report) and [named teams](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/91717/rr-vs-kkr-70th-match-indian-premier-league-2024) distinguish an appearance from a batting innings. Preserve that distinction when comparing databases.

## Latest five T20I innings

Across the New Zealand series from 22 October to 1 November 2026, Samson scored **67, 14, 18, 4 and 4**, in chronological order. The [dated batting list](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/8271/sanju-samson/all-matches/batting) gives 33, 17, 13, 2 and 10 balls respectively.

Those five dismissed innings total 107 runs from 75 balls:

- Batting average: 107 ÷ 5 = **21.40**.

- Strike rate: 107 ÷ 75 × 100 = **142.67**.

This short form window complements the career record. It does not replace it, and its results should not be treated as a forecast for a different opponent, role or set of conditions.
