*Completed final from 31 May 2026. Evidence refreshed on 14 November 2026.*

**Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the IPL 2026 final**, completing the chase with 12 balls remaining at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad. Gujarat made 155/8; RCB finished on 161/5 in 18 overs. Virat Kohli was Player of the Match for his unbeaten 75 from 42 balls. Rajat Patidar won the toss and chose to field. RCB’s target was **156**, rather than 155. Their final total reached 161 because Kohli ended the chase with a six when the scores were level. ## Washington Sundar held Gujarat together

Gujarat lost Shubman Gill and Sai Sudharsan early and reached **45/2 after the powerplay**. Jos Buttler and Nishant Sindhu added 29 for the third wicket before Sindhu fell for 20, leaving GT at 55/3.

Washington Sundar arrived at that point and remained unbeaten through the innings. His 50 from 37 balls gave Gujarat their only half-century. Arshad Khan’s 15 from six balls supplied a brief acceleration during a 26-run partnership with Sundar. [GT match report](https://www.gujarattitansipl.com/news/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-gujarat-titans-tata-ipl-2026-final-match-report).

RCB’s leading wicket-taker in the final was **Rasikh Salam Dar with 3/27**. Bhuvneshwar Kumar took 2/29 and Josh Hazlewood 2/37. Krunal Pandya contributed 1/23, including Buttler’s wicket. These four bowlers accounted for all eight dismissals. [Detailed scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/155409/rcb-vs-gt-final-ipl-2026).

## Iyer and Kohli gave the chase an early advantage

Venkatesh Iyer, introduced as RCB’s Impact Player, opened with Kohli and scored **32 from 16 balls**. Their stand reached 62 before Iyer fell at 4.3 overs. Padikkal’s dismissal soon afterward made it 63/2.

Rashid Khan then removed Patidar and Krunal Pandya in the ninth over. RCB were **91/4 after 8.5 overs**, with Kohli still at the crease. Rashid finished with 2/25 from four overs. [Official IPL report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-final-rcb-v-gt-match-report), [GT scorecard and wicket sequence](https://www.gujarattitansipl.com/live-scores/royal-challengers-bengaluru-vs-gujarat-titans-bcahm05312026270968).

The early runs mattered even after those wickets. At 91/4, RCB needed **65 from 67 balls**, a required rate of **5.82 per over**. The equation gave the remaining batters more time than the starting requirement of 7.80 per over.

Tim David’s 24 from 17 balls helped move the total to 132 before he became the fifth wicket at the end of the 14th over. RCB then required **24 from 36 balls**. The target was still unfinished, but the scoring requirement had fallen to four runs per over. [RCB’s review of the chase](https://www.royalchallengers.com/rcb-cricket-news/news/rcb-crowned-as-ipl-2026-champions).

## How Kohli finished the match

Kohli completed his fifty in 25 balls and stayed until the winning stroke. Jitesh Sharma finished unbeaten on 11 from 14 balls, sharing an unbroken **29-run sixth-wicket stand** with him. Kohli’s final 75 included nine fours and three sixes. [IPL final report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-final-rcb-v-gt-match-report), [full scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/155409/rcb-vs-gt-final-ipl-2026).

His last two scoring shots explain the final scoreboard:

- At **17.5**, a four off Arshad Khan took RCB to **155/5**, level with Gujarat.

- At **17.6**, a six raised the total to **161/5** and completed the win.

The six counts in full, even though only one run was needed to pass Gujarat’s score. Eighteen overs use 108 legal balls, leaving **12 of the scheduled 120** unused. [Kohli delivery record](https://www.cricbuzz.com/player-match-highlights/155409/2/1413/batting).

## Which RCB versus GT playoff match was this

The same teams had already met in **Qualifier 1 on 26 May at Dharamshala**. RCB scored 254/5 and beat GT by 92 runs, securing a direct place in the final. That result must not be confused with the five-wicket win in Ahmedabad. [Official Qualifier 1 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-qualifier-1-rcb-vs-gt-match-report).

Gujarat reached the final by beating Rajasthan Royals in **Qualifier 2 on 29 May at Mullanpur**, chasing 215 and finishing at 219/3 in 18.4 overs. [Official Qualifier 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-qualifier-2-gt-vs-rr-match-report).
