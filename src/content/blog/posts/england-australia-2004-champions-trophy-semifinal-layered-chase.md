England beat Australia by six wickets in the Champions Trophy semifinal at Edgbaston on 21 September 2004. Michael Vaughan took two wickets and made 86; Marcus Trescothick and Andrew Strauss helped complete the chase. [ICC rivalry review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/major-rivalries-england-v-australia-at-icc-events-x7776)

## Vaughan’s influence began with the ball

Australia made 259/9. Vaughan bowled ten overs for 42, dismissing Darren Lehmann and Damien Martyn, and was credited with Andrew Symonds’s run-out. Australia moved from 189/3 to 210/6. [Full scorecard](https://img.cricinfo.com/db/ARCHIVE/2004/OD_TOURNEYS/ICCCT/SCORECARDS/KNOCKOUTS/AUS_ENG_ICCCT_ODI-SEMI1_21SEP2004.html)

That sequence was three wickets for 21 runs. Two counted in Vaughan’s bowling figures; the run-out did not. Combining them as “three bowling wickets” would give the wrong record even though he contributed to all three dismissals.

His economy rate was 4.20 runs per over across a full allocation. Vaughan’s batting was therefore only one part of his involvement in shaping the result.

## The second wicket supplied the foundation

England lost Vikram Solanki at 21. Trescothick made 81 before falling at 161, giving the second-wicket pair a 140-run stand. Vaughan departed at 227; Strauss finished unbeaten on 52 from 42 balls as England reached 262/4 in 46.3 overs. [Chase and fall of wickets](https://img.cricinfo.com/db/ARCHIVE/2004/OD_TOURNEYS/ICCCT/SCORECARDS/KNOCKOUTS/AUS_ENG_ICCCT_ODI-SEMI1_21SEP2004.html)

The second-wicket stand covered approximately 53.8% of the winning target of 260. After it ended, Vaughan and Strauss added another 66 before Vaughan’s dismissal. Thus the chase did not depend on one partnership surviving until victory.

## The finish had a different tempo

Strauss’s 52 from 42 gives a strike rate of 123.81 runs per 100 balls. It describes his innings, not the speed of England’s entire pursuit. The finish at 46.3 overs left 21 legal balls unused: 300 minus 279.

England combined containment in Australia’s innings, a substantial rebuilding stand after an early wicket and a quicker finishing contribution. Vaughan linked the bowling effort to the chase, while Strauss ensured the conclusion had its own scoring momentum.
