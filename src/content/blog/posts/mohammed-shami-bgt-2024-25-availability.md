Mohammed Shami was absent from India’s original Australia squad in October 2024. By December, after returning to domestic cricket, he was still not cleared for the remaining two Border-Gavaskar Trophy Tests. This is the historical 2024–25 timeline, not a statement about his current fitness. [Original BCCI squad](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/squads-for-india-s-tour-of-south-africa-border-gavaskar-trophy-announced), [BCCI’s December assessment](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/medical-fitness-update-mohammed-shami)

## What was confirmed, and when

| Date | Officially documented position |
| --- | --- |
| 25 October 2024 | Shami was not named in the Australia squad or reserves. |
| 20 November 2024 | The ICC reported his seven-wicket Ranji comeback and Morne Morkel’s cautious response. |
| 23 December 2024 | The BCCI said he was not fit for consideration for the remaining two Tests. |

The November return came for Bengal against Madhya Pradesh. Morkel welcomed it while saying the team was monitoring his progress rather than rushing him back. That report was not a selection announcement. [ICC’s contemporary update](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/world-test-championship/news/morkel-provides-update-on-shami-s-return-to-india-squad-for-border-gavaskar-trophy)

## The December explanation

The BCCI said Shami had recovered from his right-heel problem. Separately, it reported swelling in his left knee following increased bowling workloads and said more controlled exposure was needed. The board recorded 43 overs in the Ranji match and participation in all nine of Bengal’s Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy games. [BCCI medical and fitness update](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/medical-fitness-update-mohammed-shami)

These are the board’s dated findings, not an independent diagnosis or a claim of permanent limitation. The decision concerned consideration for the remaining Tests, rather than erasing the fact that he had already returned to competition.

## Three different questions

A domestic scorecard shows participation. A squad release shows selection. A fitness assessment addresses availability for the proposed workload. In this episode, the first changed before the other two did.

That separation explains why reports of Shami taking wickets did not establish that he would appear in Melbourne or Sydney. For a later series, those later squad and availability announcements must be checked afresh; the December 2024 statement cannot answer a present-day selection question.
