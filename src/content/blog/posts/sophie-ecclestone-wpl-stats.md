## Seven wickets did not mean seven wicket-taking matches

The eight 2026 match entries contain wicket counts of 1, 0, 2, 1, 1, 0, 0 and 2, listed from the latest appearance backwards. They sum to seven wickets across five wicket-taking appearances. [Published match ledger](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/wpl/player-stats/sophie-ecclestone/66391)

That distinction changes the calculation:

- Seven divided by eight is **0.875 wickets per match**.
- Five divided by eight is **62.5% of appearances with a wicket**.
- Neither calculation establishes her probability of taking a wicket in the next match.

The first measure counts wickets; the second counts appearances that meet a condition. Confusing them would incorrectly turn 0.875 into an 87.5% success rate.
