Quinton de Kock finished CPL 2026 with the most runs, 392. The wicket lead was shared: Imran Tahir, Hunain Shah, Alzarri Joseph and Matthew Forde each took 16. A table naming only one of those four as the sole wicket leader would hide a tie. [Season summary](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-series/12123/caribbean-premier-league-2026)

The tables below cover the completed men’s season, including its playoffs. The batting threshold is at least 300 runs; the bowling threshold is at least 15 wickets. That produces five batters and six bowlers, rather than forcing both lists into an arbitrary top five.

## Total runs and scoring speed tell different stories

De Kock led Hetmyer by 25 runs, but faced 88 more balls. Hetmyer scored faster across his recorded balls, while de Kock accumulated more runs overall. Those statements are compatible: a runs table is not a strike-rate table.

Sadaqat’s 363 came from seven batting innings, fewer than any other player in this group. His runs per batting innings were 51.86, compared with de Kock’s 35.64. This comparison does not adjust for batting position, match conditions or the stage at which each innings began. Nor should runs per innings be substituted for a conventional batting average, whose denominator is dismissals.

Boundary composition adds another distinction. De Kock’s 29 fours and 20 sixes supplied 236 runs, 60.20% of his total. Hetmyer’s 19 fours and 29 sixes supplied 250, or 68.12%. These are shares of runs scored, not percentages of balls faced. [Season boundary counts](https://www.cricbuzz.com/cricket-series/12123/caribbean-premier-league-2026/stats)

Bowling overs and several related rates disagree across the retrieved providers, so they are not combined into this table. Equal wicket totals do not establish equal workload or equal cost per wicket; those questions require a reconciled delivery-and-runs ledger.

## Centuries and awards are not substitutes for the tables

Individual landmarks help explain the season without changing how the totals are ranked. The CPL’s report records de Kock’s 106 in a defeat to St Kitts and Nevis Patriots. A century can contribute to the leading season tally without winning that particular match. [CPL report published 2 September](https://cplt20.prezly.com/patriots-rally-to-hand-tridents-third-straight-defeat)

Hetmyer made 111 not out in the rain-shortened match against Trinbago, then another hundred in Qualifier 2. The organizer explicitly calls the latter his second of the season. That matters because one statistics table lists only one hundred beside his otherwise matching run total. [First hundred](https://cplt20.prezly.com/hetmyer-hundred-bests-knight-riders), [second hundred](https://cplt20.prezly.com/kingsmen-overcome-hetmyer-hundred-to-reach-final)

Treat each field as a separate claim. Agreement on total runs does not automatically validate every century count, appearance count or bowling denominator displayed beside it. For the same reason, an overall player award should not be relabelled as the most-runs or most-wickets award.

## How to use the completed season record

Use these thresholds to find the leading accumulators and then inspect the relevant innings or bowling spells. A historical leaderboard can identify players worth studying, but it cannot establish a later playing XI, a current role or expected fantasy points. Keep the men’s CPL season, its date range and its playoff coverage attached to every comparison.
