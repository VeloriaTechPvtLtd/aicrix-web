## Wickets and run control moved differently

Across 2024 and 2025 combined, he conceded 138 runs from 96 legal balls and took three wickets. In 2026 the corresponding figures were 145, 90 and six.

Wickets arrived more frequently in the later sample, but scoring against him was also faster. Neither direction should be hidden by calling the whole season simply better or worse. These are small samples, not a controlled comparison of identical match situations.
