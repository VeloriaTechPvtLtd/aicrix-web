West Indies retained the men's World Cup in 1979, beating England by 92 runs. Their innings had been in difficulty at 99 for four before Viv Richards and Collis King added 139. England later reached 183 for two, yet finished all out for 194. The two recoveries had very different endings. [ICC's 1979 overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/mens-cricket-world-cup-1979-overview)

## Richards had a partner, not merely an audience

King made 86 from 66 balls during the recovery. Richards went on to win Player of the Match, but the ICC's account emphasises King's attacking contribution while they were together. Reducing the stand to a famous batter receiving support would miss its balance. [ICC's 1979 overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/mens-cricket-world-cup-1979-overview)

Richards finished unbeaten on 138, including 11 fours and three sixes, as West Indies reached 286 for nine. Michael Holding's later ICC interview recalls confidence in that total while also acknowledging the uncertainty inherent in a one-day tournament. [ICC's Holding retrospective](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/on-this-day-windies-win-1979-world-cup)

## Why the length of the innings matters

The 1979 competition allowed 60 overs per innings. West Indies' total therefore belongs to that format, not to a modern 50-over match transplanted into the past. [ICC's 1979 overview](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/mens-cricket-world-cup-1979-overview)

Mike Brearley and Geoffrey Boycott added 129 for the first wicket, using 38 overs before Brearley departed. [MCC’s archival final history](https://www.lords.org/lords/news-stories/world-cup-finals-pt-1-the-prudential-cup-era)

The starting target was 287. Spread evenly over 60 overs, that is approximately 4.78 runs per over. That simple calculation is only a starting benchmark: if the batting side scores below it for a substantial period, the later requirement rises. Saving wickets does not by itself prevent that increase.

## The finish belonged to the attack

Garner's final return was five for 38, listed in the ICC's tournament statistical review. That same review records Richards' unbeaten 138 as the event's highest individual innings and King's 86 among its leading scores. The batting and bowling highlights therefore came from different phases of the same final. [ICC's 1979 statistical review](https://www.icc-cricket.com/news/1979-cricket-world-cup-in-numbers)

Holding took two wickets and Colin Croft three as England were dismissed in 51 overs. The wickets were not all Garner's, even though his five-wicket haul naturally dominates the closing highlights. [ICC's Holding retrospective](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/on-this-day-windies-win-1979-world-cup)

The opening stand preserved wickets but used more than half the innings allocation. England’s later fall from 183 for two to 194 all out then removed the other resource: partners with whom to complete the chase.
