Kranti Goud bowled in all eight of UP Warriorz’s WPL 2026 matches, taking three wickets from 23 overs. Her best figures were 2/18 against Gujarat Giants on 22 January. That performance supplied two-thirds of her season wickets, but it was only one part of a workload that varied between two and four overs per match.

The name also appears as Kranti Gaud in scorecards and on the official UP Warriorz website. These are records for the same player, not two bowlers whose totals should be added together. The team’s career panel lists 16 WPL appearances and nine wickets across seasons, which must not be mistaken for her 2026 return. [Official UP Warriorz records](https://www.upwarriorz.in/)

## Regular participation was not a fixed four over role

Goud received four overs in three consecutive appearances, on 15, 17 and 22 January. In the other five matches, she delivered three overs once and two overs four times. Across all eight, the observed average was 17.25 legal balls per appearance, or 2.875 six-ball overs. It is clearer to use balls here than to write that decimal as if it were scorecard overs notation.

Match length also matters. RCB completed its two chases in 12.1 and 13.1 overs, so neither bowling innings lasted the full 20 overs. Goud’s two-over allocation in those games belongs alongside that shorter team opportunity. It does not by itself prove that she was deliberately withdrawn because of her performance.

The distinction works in the other direction too: she bowled two overs against Delhi on 14 January even though the chase reached the end of the twentieth over. A short individual allocation is therefore not always explained by a short match. The scorecard shows what happened; it does not establish the captain’s reasoning.

## The best bowling return and the season tell different stories

Against Gujarat on 22 January, Goud conceded 18 runs in four overs and dismissed Danni Wyatt-Hodge and Anushka Sharma. The official report describes the early breakthrough and the pressure applied by Goud and Sophie Ecclestone, but Gujarat still won by 45 runs. An effective individual bowling performance was not the same thing as a winning team performance. [Official Match 14 report](https://www.wplt20.com/news/article/match-report-tata-wpl-2026-match-14)

Her remaining seven bowling innings combined for one wicket and 169 runs from 19 overs. That is an economy rate of 8.89 outside the best bowling return, compared with 4.50 in it. Showing both prevents the best-figures column from becoming a misleading shorthand for a typical match. It also keeps the sample in proportion: just three wickets make any wicket-based average sensitive to individual dismissals.

## What this record can tell you about her role

The historical evidence supports consistent selection and some bowling in every appearance, with a variable allocation. It does not support a guaranteed four-over forecast for another season. Nor can the season table alone establish how many deliveries came in the powerplay, middle overs or final five overs.

For a future match, start with that match’s confirmed XI and current bowling choices rather than treating a 2026 workload as a contract. For this completed season, the useful baseline is the full eight-match log: three four-over allocations, four two-over allocations, one three-over allocation and wickets in two appearances.
