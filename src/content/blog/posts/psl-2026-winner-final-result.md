## PSL 2026 winner and final result

Peshawar Zalmi won the 2026 Pakistan Super League, beating Hyderabad Kingsmen by five wickets at Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore, on 3 May. It was Zalmi’s second title, following their 2017 success. Aaron Hardie was named player of the final after taking four wickets and making an unbeaten half-century. [Official PSL report](https://psl-t20.com/hardies-all-round-masterclass-leads-zalmi-to-second-hbl-psl-title/).

The final margin was measured in wickets, not the one-run difference between the two totals: a successful chase ends once the target is reached.

## The first innings lost momentum after the powerplay

Hyderabad reached 69/2 after six overs but fell to 73/6 by 7.5. That change cost four wickets for four runs after the powerplay checkpoint. It turned a promising scoring position into a recovery job, even though more than half the innings remained. [Official report](https://psl-t20.com/hardies-all-round-masterclass-leads-zalmi-to-second-hbl-psl-title/) and [fall of wickets](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/peshawar-zalmi-vs-hyderabad-kingsmen-2026-final-scorecard-67260/).

Saim Ayub finished with 54 from 50 balls. Marnus Labuschagne’s 20 was the only other score of at least 20. Hardie removed both, while Nahid Rana added 2/22. The two run-outs in the innings were separate from the wickets credited to bowlers; team wickets and a bowler’s personal tally are not interchangeable. [Full batting and bowling records](https://cricketdatabase.com/matches/2026-05-03-hyderabad-kingsmen-v-peshawar-zalmi).

Hyderabad were dismissed with 12 balls of their allocation unused. That is a useful description of lost batting opportunity, not a licence to add an imagined number of runs to their score. The chase still began with a fixed target of 130.

## Why a small target did not make the chase simple

Zalmi lost both openers inside the first over and were 40/4 at 4.5. Babar Azam was dismissed without scoring. Hardie and Abdul Samad then took the total to 125 before Samad fell at 14.5. The fifth-wicket partnership was therefore 85, not the sum of their complete individual innings. [Scorecard](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/peshawar-zalmi-vs-hyderabad-kingsmen-2026-final-scorecard-67260/).

That stand occupied 60 legal deliveries: 4.5 means 29 balls completed, and 14.5 means 89. Its team scoring rate was 8.50 per over. At its start, Zalmi needed 90 from 91 balls; when it ended, only five were needed from 31. The recovery changed the chase from an early wickets problem into a short finish.

Samad made 48 from 34, including four sixes. Hardie finished with nine fours and no sixes. Their complete scores totalled 104, or 80% of Zalmi’s 130, but part of Hardie’s innings occurred outside their shared stand. The partnership and the combined individual contribution answer different questions.

## Read the finish in balls rather than decimals

Farhan Yousaf struck the winning boundary at 15.2, with Hardie still unbeaten. [Final-over commentary](https://www.mykhel.com/cricket/tbc-vs-tbc-2026-final-live-score-67260/). In a six-ball-over format, 15.2 represents 92 legal balls, not 15.2 decimal overs. Subtracting 92 from 120 gives the reported 28 balls remaining.

Hardie’s contribution spanned both innings, while Samad supplied the partnership that protected it. The final is best understood through those two phases: Hyderabad’s middle-order losses and Zalmi’s repair after 40/4. Neither the low target nor the comfortable number of balls remaining captures the whole route to the title.
