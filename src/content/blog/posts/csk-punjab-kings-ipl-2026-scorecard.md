Their contributions helped Chennai maintain its scoring after losing the two set top-order batters. Yuzvendra Chahal returned 1/21 from three overs; Vijaykumar Vyshak took two wickets. ## Two wickets briefly interrupted the finish

Punjab were 186/4 after 17 overs when Iyer fell, then 186/5 after the first ball of the next over removed Wadhera. They still needed 24 from 17 legal balls. Stoinis and Shashank completed the chase without another wicket. [Chase scorecard](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/149684/csk-vs-pbks)

That does not make the result inevitable: five wickets were still available, and the remaining deliveries had to be played.
