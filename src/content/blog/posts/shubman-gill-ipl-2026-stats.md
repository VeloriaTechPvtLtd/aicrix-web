## Gill finished IPL 2026 with 732 runs

Shubman Gill scored **732 runs in 16 innings for Gujarat Titans in IPL 2026**, with one century and six half-centuries. He was dismissed in every innings, leaving his batting average at **45.75**. His 449 balls faced produce a strike rate of **163.03**. These figures include the playoffs and the 31 May final. [Official GT player record](https://www.gujarattitansipl.com/players/shubman-gill-66818-profile) · [Cricbuzz completed-season panel](https://m.cricbuzz.com/player-match-performance/match/155409/player/11808/batting).

That total does not mean Gill played every Gujarat fixture. The team’s campaign contained 17 matches; Gill missed the 4 April meeting with Rajasthan Royals. The official match report records his absence and Rashid Khan’s stand-in captaincy. An absence is neither a duck nor a did-not-bat appearance. ## The playoffs were not three similar innings

Gill’s three playoff scores were 2, 104 and 10. Together they contributed 116 runs from 68 balls, but the century supplied **89.66% of those playoff runs**. Describing the whole stretch only by its combined strike rate of 170.59 would hide that uneven distribution.

The 104 from 53 balls came in the successful Qualifier 2 chase against Rajasthan. Gill hit 15 fours and three sixes before being dismissed lbw; it was not an unbeaten century. Those boundaries contributed 78 runs, exactly 75% of his innings. His score was also 47.49% of Gujarat’s final 219, a team-total share that includes extras in the denominator. [Official Qualifier 2 report](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/tata-ipl-2026-qualifier-2-gt-vs-rr-match-report).

The final brought a very different opportunity: ten runs from eight balls before dismissal. That short innings belongs in the season record just as much as the century does. A player’s season summary is not a full account of the team’s final or winning result.

## Separate accumulation from scoring speed

Subtracting the playoffs leaves **616 runs from 381 balls in 13 league innings**. That works out to 47.38 runs per dismissal and a strike rate of 161.68. The playoff average was lower, 38.67, even though the playoff scoring rate was higher.

The distinction is useful when two summaries seem to disagree about performance. Faster scoring does not necessarily mean more runs per dismissal. Here the playoff sample is also only three innings, dominated by one large score. It is a description of this campaign, not evidence of a permanent ability to perform better or worse in knockout matches.

## What the season total cannot show by itself

Gill hit 74 fours and 33 sixes across the season. Those shots supplied 494 runs, or 67.49% of his 732. The remaining 238 runs were not boundary runs. This arithmetic describes the scoring composition; it does not identify which overs or bowling types produced those boundaries. [Official season statistics](https://www.gujarattitansipl.com/players/shubman-gill-66818-profile).

Nor does an opening role guarantee an equal number of balls in every game. Gill’s table ranges from four balls against Punjab on 3 May to 53 in Qualifier 2. A comparison of only final scores would miss that difference in innings length. Balls faced record the opportunity actually used, not how many deliveries a batter would have received had he survived.

Keep this finished IPL season separate from Gill’s international-format record. Tests, ODIs and T20Is have different records. The 2026 IPL table also does not establish his availability, batting position or expected output in a later fixture.
