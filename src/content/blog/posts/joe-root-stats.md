## How far is Root from Tendulkar’s Test total

Root passed 14,000 Test runs on **20 June 2026**, during the second Test against New Zealand at The Oval. The ECB’s dated milestone page confirms the occasion. Tendulkar finished with **15,921**, the benchmark quoted in the contemporary Reuters report. [ECB milestone](https://www.ecb.co.uk/video/4522835/joe-root-goes-past-14000-test-match-runs), [Reuters report via CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/sport/englands-root-becomes-second-player-reach-14000-test-runs-6197401).

Using Root’s subsequent 14,278-run total:

- To draw level: 15,921 − 14,278 = **1,643 more runs**.

- To move ahead: 15,922 − 14,278 = **1,644 more runs**.

That is a gap at the stated cutoff, not a forecast of when he will reach it. Dividing the gap by his career average would not reliably predict a number of matches: a Test can provide one or two batting innings, and future scoring need not resemble his career rate.

## Averages and strike rates

The published innings, not-outs and balls-faced inputs allow the rates to be checked:

| Format | Dismissals | Balls faced | Calculated batting average | Calculated strike rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tests | 283 | 24,783 | 50.45 | 57.61 |
| ODIs | 160 | 9,157 | 50.17 | 87.66 |
| T20Is | 25 | 707 | 35.72 | 126.31 |

Average is runs divided by dismissals. Strike rate is runs per 100 balls. For example, Root’s Test average is 14,278 ÷ (309 − 26), while his Test strike rate is 14,278 ÷ 24,783 × 100.

The rates above are independently rounded to two decimal places. Cricbuzz displays the Test strike rate as 57.62, although its listed runs and balls yield 57.61; myKhel displays 57.61. The underlying runs and balls also agree with the Times of India profile. [Batting inputs](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/cricket/player-profile/joe-root/10129).

## Three unbeaten innings against India

Root’s July 2026 ODI series against India illustrates why runs per innings and batting average should be kept separate:

- **14 July:** 76 not out from 76 balls.

- **16 July:** 99 not out from 133 balls.

- **19 July:** 74 not out from 48 balls.

He scored **249 from 257 balls**, a combined strike rate of **96.89**. The three innings produced 83 runs per innings, but there was no dismissal, so a conventional runs-per-dismissal batting average cannot be calculated for that series. Calling it an “average of 249” would use the wrong denominator. [Match-by-match batting ledger](https://www.cricbuzz.com/profiles/8019/joe-root/all-matches/batting).

The innings also show different scoring speeds within a successful series. A career ODI strike rate does not describe the pace of every individual chase.

## Why match totals can change without more runs

Root scored five against Pakistan on 25 October 2026, then did not bat against Sri Lanka on 29 October. The latter appearance increased his ODI match count without adding a batting innings, runs or a dismissal. His next ODI, 17 from 36 balls against Australia at Perth on 13 November, increased all four: matches, batting innings, runs and dismissals. That moves the previous 199-match, 8,010-run snapshot to 200 matches and 8,027 runs. [Pakistan match](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/170733/pak-vs-eng-4th-match-pakistan-odi-tri-series-2026), [Sri Lanka match](https://www.cricbuzz.com/live-cricket-scorecard/170751/sl-vs-eng-6th-match-pakistan-odi-tri-series-2026).

When comparing profiles, check the latest match included before treating a different total as an error. Keep Root’s active Test and ODI figures dated, and do not read his older T20I record as evidence of current selection or recent international T20 form.
