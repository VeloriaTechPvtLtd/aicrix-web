**The Duleep Trophy 2026–27 began with six announced zonal squads of 15 players each.** Those original lists are useful historical records, but they are not identical to the squads available at every later stage. BCCI appended replacement notes by 30 August, and South Zone additions were reported before September’s final. [BCCI squad announcement](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/duleep-trophy-2026-27-all-six-squads-revealed).

This guide preserves the six original lists first, then records the verified changes separately. It does **not** present a partly reconstructed end-of-tournament roster as a complete final squad.

## BCCI’s changes documented by 30 August

BCCI’s article explicitly says it was last updated on 30 August. That is the documented page-update date, not proof that every replacement became effective that same day.

| Zone | Original or earlier player | Incoming player | How to read the change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| East | Shahbaz Ahmed | Mohd. Kounain Quraishi | Quraishi replaces Ahmed |
| North | Yudhvir Singh | Sahil Lotra | First step in the published sequence |
| North | Sahil Lotra | Gurnoor Brar | Brar replaces the earlier replacement |
| North | Yash Dhull | Uday Saharan | Separate replacement |

[Official replacement notes](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/duleep-trophy-2026-27-all-six-squads-revealed).

The North Zone chain is important. It reads **Yudhvir Singh → Sahil Lotra → Gurnoor Brar**, not three simultaneous additions. If reconstructing the roster at the state described by those notes, Brar occupies that slot. Saharan takes Dhull’s slot separately.

These changes do not automatically enlarge North’s 15-player list. Adding incoming names without removing outgoing names would produce an inaccurate active-roster count. The original six squads contain **90 selection slots**, calculated as six × 15; that is not a count of everyone who appeared during the season.

## South Zone’s later additions

A report dated **4 September 2026** said Devdutt Padikkal and Mohammed Siraj had been added to South Zone for the final. Crucially, it also said the two players they would replace were not yet clear. The appropriate update is therefore a dated addition note, not an invented pair of removals. [ANI report published by The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/sports/duleep-trophy-2026-padikkal-siraj-added-to-south-zone-squad-for-final-vs-east-zone/amp).

Neither player appears in the original South list above. That is not a contradiction once the dates are retained. An original announcement and a later final squad refer to different selection states.

This section records the additions the checked report establishes. It is not an exhaustive replacement history, and it should not be labelled a fully reconciled final 15. To establish who actually played a particular match, use that match’s confirmed XI and scorecard.

## The final venue also changed

BCCI’s early squad page described the event as taking place at its Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru. A later official announcement, dated **19 August**, moved the final to **MA Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai**, scheduled for 6–10 September. The other matches remained at the Centre of Excellence. [BCCI venue amendment](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/duleep-trophy-final-to-take-place-in-chennai).

Use the later venue release when identifying the final. Copying the old squad-page introduction into a fresh preview would preserve an outdated location even if the player names were copied correctly.

## Squad membership is not a playing-XI promise

A squad list establishes selection for a group or stage. It does not establish a starting place, batting position, bowling allocation or fitness for every match.

For a historical lookup, identify the date, then check the latest applicable replacement announcement and the actual XI. For a fantasy analysis, a player’s original selection alone is insufficient evidence of participation. Where a later release does not specify an outgoing player, keep that uncertainty visible instead of forcing the list back to 15.
