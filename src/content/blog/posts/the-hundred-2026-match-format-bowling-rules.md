## The Hundred uses five ball overs

The Hundred’s 2026 format gives each team one innings of up to 100 balls. The easiest way to follow it is to separate three numbers: a five-ball set, a change of bowling end after ten balls, and a bowler’s normal maximum of 20 balls. Those are different limits, not interchangeable descriptions of an ordinary T20 over.

The [official competition explainer](https://www.thehundred.com/info/competition-rules) describes bowling in sets of five or ten consecutive balls. The 2026 playing conditions express the same structure as 20 overs of five balls. Multiplying that official “20 overs” by six would incorrectly turn a Hundred innings into 120 balls.

| Feature in a full innings | Ball allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Maximum innings length | 100 |
| One over or set | 5 |
| Interval between changes of bowling end | 10 |
| Normal maximum per bowler | 20 |
| Opening powerplay | 25 |

## A bowler change is not an end change

A captain can switch bowlers after five balls without switching the end from which the next set is bowled. Alternatively, the same bowler can deliver ten consecutive balls. Under clause 17, the limit is two consecutive five-ball overs, including restrictions on parts of consecutive overs. The batters do not exchange ends merely because a set ends. [ECB 2026 conditions, clause 17](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2026/07/17/5c8379f1-a589-4a04-942f-b73ab42ca93d/The-Hundred-2026.pdf)

That distinction matters when reading a live scoreboard. A new bowler after ball 65 does not necessarily mean the attack has changed ends. Likewise, seeing the same name continue after a five-ball set does not mean the scoreboard has missed a bowling change.

## A worked allocation with 40 balls left

Imagine an uninterrupted innings after 60 valid balls. Bowler A has delivered 15 earlier balls in complete sets and did not bowl the immediately preceding set. The captain wants A back now.

| Stage | Team balls completed | A’s balls delivered | Balls left in the innings |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Before A returns | 60 | 15 | 40 |
| After A delivers balls 61 to 65 | 65 | 20 | 35 |
| After another bowler delivers 66 to 70 | 70 | 20 | 30 |

A cannot deliver a fresh ten-ball spell here: that would take the personal total to 25. Instead, A completes five, another bowler handles the next five from the same end, and the bowling end changes after ball 70. These are hypothetical allocations, not a claim that this is the best tactical choice.

Tracking only the team’s remaining balls would miss the constraint. The captain also needs each bowler’s remaining allowance and the immediately preceding sets. The example assumes complete sets, no interruptions and no exceptional mid-set replacement.

## The powerplay and timeout have different boundaries

The opening 25 balls form the powerplay in a full-length innings, with at most two fielders outside the circle. This is also explained in the [official powerplay FAQ](https://faqs.thehundred.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004733537-What-is-a-powerplay). Afterwards, the normal outside-circle maximum is five in the men’s competition and four in the women’s, subject to over-rate restrictions. [2026 conditions, clauses 28.2 and 28.5](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2026/07/17/5c8379f1-a589-4a04-942f-b73ab42ca93d/The-Hundred-2026.pdf)

The optional fielding-side timeout lasts 90 seconds and cannot be taken during the first 25 balls. The captain requests it while the ball is dead; it can occur within or between sets. Players must be ready when the interval ends. [Clause 12.8](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2026/07/17/5c8379f1-a589-4a04-942f-b73ab42ca93d/The-Hundred-2026.pdf) The [official timeout FAQ](https://faqs.thehundred.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004731478-What-is-a-timeout) also explains that the coach can enter to discuss tactics.

## Shortened innings need a fresh allocation

Do not carry the full-innings table unchanged into a rain-reduced match. Clauses 13.3 and 28.4 specify revised bowling limits and a shortened powerplay. A partial set shared after a bowler breaks down also counts towards both bowlers’ limits. [2026 conditions](https://resources.ecb.co.uk/ecb/document/2026/07/17/5c8379f1-a589-4a04-942f-b73ab42ca93d/The-Hundred-2026.pdf)

For a viewer, the practical check is simple: identify the revised innings length first, then follow the team ball count, the individual bowling allowances and the powerplay endpoint separately. A 2026 match-format guide does not establish the rules for a later season or for a fantasy platform.
