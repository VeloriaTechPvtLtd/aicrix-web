Sandeep Warrier was Mumbai Indians’ official replacement for Jasprit Bumrah in IPL 2023. The league announced the signing on 31 March for INR 50 lakh. Warrier did not play an IPL match that season, so his squad registration should not be mistaken for an on-field replacement in the XI. [Official IPL advisory](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/delhi-capitals-name-abhishek-porel-as-rishabh-pants-replacement-sandeep-warrier-joins-mumbai-indians-as-jasprit-bumrahs-replacement), [Contemporary season review](https://www.wisden.com/series/indian-premier-league-2023/cricket-news/cut-or-keep-what-should-mumbai-indians-do-with-squad-for-next-season-ipl-2023-2)

## Three separate Mumbai transactions

| Announcement | Incoming player | Player replaced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 31 March 2023 | Sandeep Warrier | Jasprit Bumrah |
| 6 April 2023 | Riley Meredith | Jhye Richardson |
| 9 May 2023 | Chris Jordan | Jofra Archer |

Mumbai’s own announcements identify Richardson as Meredith’s replacement counterpart and Archer as Jordan’s. Neither changes the answer to the Bumrah question. [Meredith announcement](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/ipl-2023-mumbai-indians-riley-meredith-replacement-jhye-richardson-mi), [Jordan announcement](https://www.mumbaiindians.com/news/chris-jordan-replaces-jofra-archer-at-mi)

The transaction should retain four pieces of information: incoming player, outgoing player, franchise and season. Sharing a bowling role does not make several different registrations one transaction.

## Checking the appearance claim

The March 2023 IPL advisory credited Warrier with five previous IPL games. The league’s March 2024 advisory still gave him five. That unchanged total is consistent with Wisden’s explicit contemporary statement that he did not get a game in 2023. [2024 advisory](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/gt-name-sandeep-warrier-as-replacement-for-mohd-shami-mi-add-kwena-maphaka-to-squad-for-the-injured-dilshan-madushanka)

Zero appearances is not the same as appearing without taking a wicket. There is no 2023 Mumbai bowling sample here from which to calculate his economy or compare his performance with Bumrah’s.

## The Shami story came a year later

Gujarat Titans named Warrier as Mohammed Shami’s replacement on 20 March 2024, also for INR 50 lakh. That was a new team and season, not a correction to the earlier Mumbai signing. [Official 2024 announcement](https://www.iplt20.com/news/article/gt-name-sandeep-warrier-as-replacement-for-mohd-shami-mi-add-kwena-maphaka-to-squad-for-the-injured-dilshan-madushanka)

The repeated player name and fee explain the potential confusion. They do not establish any current contract, selection or fitness status.
