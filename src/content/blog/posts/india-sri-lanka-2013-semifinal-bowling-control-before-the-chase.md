India beat Sri Lanka by eight wickets at Cardiff on 20 June 2013. Sri Lanka made 181/8 from 50 overs; India reached 182/2 in 35. Ishant Sharma took 3/33 and was player of the match. [BCCI match report](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/report-ct-2013-sf-2-india-vs-sri-lanka)

## Completing the allocation was not enough

Sri Lanka’s team scoring rate was 3.62 runs per over. India’s chase rate was 5.20. The comparison does not require a claim that the surface changed: it describes how each side used its innings.

Mahela Jayawardene and Angelo Mathews added 78, and Ravichandran Ashwin finished with three wickets. [BCCI report](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/report-ct-2013-sf-2-india-vs-sri-lanka)

The partnership supplied approximately 43.1% of Sri Lanka’s total. It was meaningful recovery, but the completed innings still left India a modest numerical requirement.

## A retirement and an overturned decision

Tillakaratne Dilshan retired hurt early and returned near the end. MS Dhoni handed the keeping gloves to Dinesh Karthik and bowled; a Jayawardene lbw decision was overturned on review. [BCCI account](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/report-ct-2013-sf-2-india-vs-sri-lanka)

Neither Dilshan’s temporary departure nor the reversed lbw decision should be counted as a dismissal. They changed what spectators saw at the crease without adding a wicket to the completed record. Describing the unusual passages accurately matters more than making them sound decisive.

## The opening pair reduced the remaining task

Rohit Sharma and Shikhar Dhawan added 77. Dhawan scored 68 and Virat Kohli finished unbeaten on 58. [BCCI score summary](https://www.bcci.tv/news/article/report-ct-2013-sf-2-india-vs-sri-lanka)

The opening stand covered about 42.3% of the target, leaving 105 after the first wicket. India finished with 90 legal deliveries unused. The batting complemented the bowling rather than repairing a crisis: restriction set the size of the task, and a substantial opening partnership prevented early dismissals from complicating it.
